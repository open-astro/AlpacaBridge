### Fixed

- **QHY Q-Focuser: a move no longer trips "communications compromised"** (QHY, issue #2031): the firmware answers position through a move but no telemetry for about 10 s after every move command, and the state poll, which needed both, latched the link fault 9.7 s into every long move, so `Move`, `Position` and `IsMoving` threw until telemetry returned (ConformU's focuser range sweep failed on `Move - To 0`). Telemetry is now polled best effort on its own cadence (once a second while idle, 500 ms deadline, a 5 s hold-off after a miss) and only a failed position exchange counts toward the fault; the last reading is kept, a move in progress serves it whatever its age, and `Temperature` reports a `DriverException` only once the firmware has had 30 s of idle time to answer and did not.
- **QHY Q-Focuser: no external probe is reported as such, not as -111 C** (QHY, issue #2032): the firmware's no-probe sentinel (`o_t` at or below -100 C) now makes `Temperature` throw `PropertyNotImplemented` naming the fix (plug a probe in, or set `temperatureSource` to the controller board), and `DeviceState` omits it; ConformU flagged the old -111.3 C.
- **QHY Q-Focuser: a unit that stops taking USB data no longer hangs the connect forever** (QHY, issue #2030): the drain before the reply kick had no timeout and parked the connect, holding the port, on a unit that enumerated without its 12 V supply; the probe and every transaction now bound it (`util::tty_drain_bounded`, 500 ms), the probe treats the stall as "not this device" and a transaction fails with a `DriverException` that names the replug.

### Added (tests)

- **Q-Focuser telemetry cases** (issue #2031, #2032, #2030): "telemetry silence after a move keeps the position poll going and latches no fault" and "Temperature refuses a telemetry reading older than the staleness bound while Position still serves" (`test_qhy_focuser_snapshot.cpp`, over `FakeQhyQFocuser::set_telemetry_muted`), "No external probe: Temperature is PropertyNotImplemented and the board sensor still answers" (`test_qhy_focuser.cpp`), and "tty_drain_bounded returns at once on a pty that takes data" (`test_serial_link.cpp`).
