### Changed

- **Pointing math helper**, Bisque, Celestron (NexStar and CGX), iOptron, OnStep, Sky-Watcher, SynScan and ZWO telescope drivers, issue #1631. Local sidereal time, the hour and degree wraps, the shortest RA delta, the destination pier-side rule and the RA/Dec and site range checks now live once in `util/pointing.h` instead of eight hand-kept copies; the drivers call it. The `InvalidValue` text for a target or site out of range is now the same on every mount: `<Label> must be in range 0 to <24 hours`, `<Label> must be in range -90 to 90 degrees`, `SiteLatitude must be in range -90 to 90 degrees`, `SiteLongitude must be in range -180 to 180 degrees`. CGX, iOptron and ZWO change their text on the wire (they said `[0, 24)`, `[-90, 90]`, `[-180,+180]`), and the coordinate-pair checks (`SlewToCoordinates`, `SyncToCoordinates`, `DestinationSideOfPier`, `Park`) now read `<Operation>: RightAscension must be in range 0 to <24 hours` instead of `<Operation>: RA out of range`; the error numbers do not change. ZWO's hour-angle wrap is now [-12, 12), so an hour angle of exactly +12 h reads as -12 h. A tiny negative input to the hour or degree wrap now lands on 0, never on 24 h or 360 degrees (`wrap_hours(-1e-18)`, `wrap_degrees(-1e-15)` in the test). Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_pointing_math.cpp` (golden sidereal-time table, wrap edges, pier side, every validator's text and `InvalidValue` code).
