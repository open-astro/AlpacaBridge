### Fixed
- **iOptron: `Tracking` stays true while the mount pulse guides or flips the meridian** (AlpacaCore, issue #2186): the `:GLS#` system status reads 3 (auto-guiding) for the whole of a guide pulse and 4 while the mount flips the meridian, and the driver mapped only 1 and 5 to `Tracking` true, so a client that polled `Tracking` during a 2-4 s pulse saw it off (eleven false "tracking lost" faults in 50 minutes of guiding on an HAE29C). A pulse is a correction on top of sidereal tracking and `IsPulseGuiding` already reports it, so 3 and 4 now read as `Tracking` true; a GOTO (2), a stopped mount (0, 7) and a parked one (6) still read false, and the equatorial-slew guard is unchanged. Covered by "iOptron Telescope Driver - Tracking stays true while the mount reports guiding or a meridian flip" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_ioptron_telescope.cpp`.
