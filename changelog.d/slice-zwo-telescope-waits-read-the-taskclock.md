### Changed

- **ZWO telescope test fake**, ZWO telescope driver, issue #1596. A stateful `FakeZwoMount` (`AlpacaCore/tests/fake_zwo_mount.h`) answers the AM-mount command dialect over loopback, sends no reply to the commands the driver sends blind, reports the full `:GU#` status flag set, and derives GOTO, park, home, jog and guide-pulse motion from the shared `FakeTaskClock`; its contract is pinned by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_fake_mount.cpp`, and `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_telescope_clock.cpp` drives each timed wait and deadline of the telescope driver (connect retry, poll cadence, pulse window and its end on a slew, the 1500 ms Tracking wait, busy back-off, sync retry, slew timeout, the GOTO, FindHome, MoveAxis, Park and Unpark forced Slewing windows, the 2 s park inference) with `advance()`. No driver behaviour changes. The ZWO telescope tests that used their own mount fakes (`test_zwo_async_slew_failure.cpp`, `test_zwo_telemetry_concurrency_stress.cpp`, the telescope storms in `test_zwo_concurrency_stress.cpp` and the getter-stall cases in `test_zwo_telescope.cpp`) now run on `FakeZwoMount`, and none of them sleeps to wait for a driver timer.
