### Changed

- **iOptron telescope driver waits and deadlines run on an injected `TaskClock`** (iOptron, issue #1223): every `create_ioptron_telescope*` factory takes a `util::TaskClock&` as its last, defaulted argument (`util::default_task_clock()`, so hardware behaviour and existing callers are unchanged), and the slew poll and settle sleeps, the slew deadline, the cache ages and the Slewing, Park and PulseGuide windows read it; the clock-sync thread's 250 ms start delay is now a cancellable wait, and so is the 750 ms retry delay after a failed site or clock command, so a disconnect inside either no longer waits the delay out (`iOptron Telescope Driver - a disconnect inside the clock-sync start delay does not wait it out`, `iOptron Telescope Driver - a disconnect inside the site-sync retry delay does not wait it out`). UTCDate time and serial/TCP I/O timeouts stay real. The slow cases in `test_ioptron_telescope.cpp` and `test_ioptron_async_slew_failure.cpp` step a `FakeTaskClock` instead of sleeping, with their assertions unchanged.
