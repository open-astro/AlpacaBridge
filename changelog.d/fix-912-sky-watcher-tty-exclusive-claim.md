### Fixed
- **Sky-Watcher direct: another program opening the mount's serial port no longer kills the link** (AlpacaCore, issue #912): the driver now claims the port with `TIOCEXCL` (released with `TIOCNXCL` on disconnect), so a second non-root process gets `EBUSY` instead of reprogramming the line; and after every silent exchange timeout it re-applies its own line settings, so a latched link fault clears on its own once the board answers, without a client reconnect. Covered by `test_skywatcher_serial.cpp` ("the connected port is claimed exclusively and released on disconnect", "a latched fault from a reprogrammed line recovers without a reconnect").
