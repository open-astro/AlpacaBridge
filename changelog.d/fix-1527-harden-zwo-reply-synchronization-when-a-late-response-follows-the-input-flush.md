### Fixed

- **ZWO mount: a late reply is no longer read as the next command's reply**, ZWO mount protocol wrapper, issue #1527. After a query times out with no reply (for example the 250 ms Dec read of the pulse-guide reconcile), the wrapper now holds the next query until that reply arrives and the line is quiet, or 600 ms after the timeout, so the late reply is discarded instead of being returned as the next `:GU`, `:GAT` or `:GT` answer. The wait applies to queries only: a blind command, including a guide pulse and the abort `:Q`, is never delayed. An exchange with nothing owed pays nothing. A reply later than 600 ms, and a late reply of the same shape as the next one (RA for RA, Dec for Dec), cannot be told apart and can still be misread. Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_mount_reply_sync.cpp`, over a pty and over TCP.
