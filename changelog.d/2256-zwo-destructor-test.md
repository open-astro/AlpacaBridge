### Fixed (tests)

- **ZWO destructor case: the pulse no longer waits for the GOTO, so the destructor meets a held GOTO again** (tests, issue #2256): "ZWO async - the destructor reaps a held GOTO with a pulse drain and the poll live" held the GOTO on `:Sr`. Since open-astro#2093, PulseGuide queues under `target_write_mutex_`, which the GOTO body holds from `:Sr` through `:MS`. So the pulse blocked until `:Sr`, `:Sd` and `:MS` had each timed out silently (about 15 s), the destructor ran after the GOTO was over, and the case failed only when the fake read the queued `:Sd`/`:MS` before the checks ran (nightly ASan, 11-14 and 11-15). The case now holds the GOTO on its `:SMTI` time write, before the body takes the target lock, checks that the pulse queues inside the initiator budget, and checks that no `:Sr` follows.
