### Fixed

- **Player One thermal switch connect no longer blocks getters**, Player One switch driver, issue #1760. `UniqueID` and `MaxSwitch` returned only after the connect handshake finished, because connect held `mutex_` across `init_camera` and the capability probe. Connect now runs under `transition_mutex_` and a per-link `io_mutex_`, status reads and heater/fan writes take `io_mutex_`, and `mutex_` is a short state lock ([decision 0016](docs/decisions/0016-getters-never-wait-on-device-io.md)). Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_playerone_switch.cpp` ("no-I/O getters do not wait behind the connect handshake", "disconnect returns with a read in flight").
