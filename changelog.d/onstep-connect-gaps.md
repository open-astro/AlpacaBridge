### Breaking changes

- **OnStep `SideOfPier`, `DestinationSideOfPier`, `SiderealTime` and `SlewToAltAz` throw `InvalidOperation` while the site is unknown** (issue #1893, decision 0019): on a mount whose site the device config does not set and whose `:Gt#`/`:Gg#` read fails, they answered for latitude and longitude 0 and now refuse. **After upgrading:** set the site in the device config, or have the client write `SiteLatitude` and `SiteLongitude`; a mount whose site reads at connect sees no change.

### Fixed

- **OnStep members that use the site refuse while it is unknown** (OnStep driver, issue #1893): when neither the device config nor the mount's `:Gt#`/`:Gg#` read gave a site, `SideOfPier`, `DestinationSideOfPier` and `SiderealTime` answered for longitude 0, and `SlewToAltAz`/`SlewToAltAzAsync` converted at latitude and longitude 0 and sent the mount to the wrong place. They now throw `InvalidOperation` with the site getters' reason, as decision 0019 rule 2 asks; an Alt/Az slew retries the site read first and sends nothing when it still fails; see Breaking changes.
- **An OnStep connect that cannot finish now fails** (OnStep driver, issue #1895): a status poll that does not start, or a link lost during the connect's first reads, used to leave a connect that returned success with nothing polling, or with `Connected` reading false. Both now fail the connect and undo it as a Disconnect does. While no `:GU#` has answered since the connect, a command that checks for a park or writes the status through (`PulseGuide`, `MoveAxis`, `FindHome`, `Tracking=true`, `Unpark`, the slews and syncs) reads `:GU#` first and throws `DriverException` when that read fails, instead of taking the unread status for "not parked"; `AbortSlew`, `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` and `Tracking=false` are stops and still go out. Covered by new cases in `test_onstep_telescope_snapshot.cpp` and `test_onstep_link_loss.cpp`, with two more that pin the hold-window clears after a failed `:MS#` or `:Mg` send (#1887; its second-firmware question is answered from the firmware source in `onstep.instructions.md`). ConformU was not re-run.
