### Breaking changes

- **Sky-Watcher direct, Bisque, Celestron NexStar, iOptron and ZWO AM refuse an equatorial slew with `Tracking` false** (issue #1274): `SlewToCoordinatesAsync` and `SlewToTargetAsync`, the slews the router still serves, used to start the slew with `Tracking` off and now raise `InvalidOperation` (0x40B). A client that slews without turning `Tracking` on first stops slewing these mounts. **After upgrading:** turn `Tracking` on before slewing, including after Park/Unpark, FindHome or `Tracking=false`.

### Changed

- **Equatorial slews are refused while `Tracking` is false on five more telescope drivers** (issue #1274, Sky-Watcher direct, Bisque, Celestron NexStar, iOptron and ZWO AM): `SlewToCoordinates`, `SlewToCoordinatesAsync`, `SlewToTarget` and `SlewToTargetAsync` now raise `InvalidOperation` (0x40B) at the driver with "<member> requires Tracking to be true" (over HTTP the synchronous forms already answer `MethodNotImplemented`, issue #775), as ITelescopeV4 requires and SynScan already did; before, the mount slewed. An out-of-range coordinate still raises `InvalidValue` first, and nothing is sent to the mount for a refused slew. A retarget while a slew is in flight is still accepted: on iOptron while the mount reports a GOTO, guiding or a meridian flip, on ZWO and Bisque while the driver's own slew runs, even where the mount reads Tracking as off during it. Syncs and Alt/Az slews are unchanged, as is the Celestron CGX, which already refused (`Enable tracking before starting an equatorial slew`). The refusal is a breaking change, see Breaking changes. Tests: `test_skywatcher_motion_limits.cpp` "SkyWatcher tracking guard", `test_bisque_telescope.cpp` "Bisque Telescope Driver - all four equatorial slews are refused while Tracking is false", `test_celestron_async_slew_failure.cpp` and `test_ioptron_telescope.cpp` and `test_zwo_async_slew_failure.cpp` ("... all four equatorial slews are refused while Tracking is false"), and `test_celestron_cgx_telescope.cpp` for the CGX; the retarget cases are `test_ioptron_telescope.cpp` "... a slew while the mount reports motion is not refused for Tracking", `test_zwo_async_slew_failure.cpp` "... a retarget of its own GOTO in flight is not refused for Tracking" and `test_bisque_telescope.cpp` "... a retarget of its own slew in flight is not refused for Tracking".
