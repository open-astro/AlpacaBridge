### Fixed

- **Unsupported members answer `NotConnected` while disconnected on the ZWO camera, ZWO mount, Sky-Watcher, Bisque, SynScan and Gemini Cover Lite drivers, and GPhoto Bayer offsets** (AlpacaCore, issue #1919): members the device lacks (for example `SlewToAltAz`, `SyncToAltAz`, `SideOfPier` and rate writes, `DoesRefraction`, SynScan `FindHome` and `TrackingRate` writes, ZWO camera `Gains`, `Offsets`, `BayerOffsetX/Y`, `SubExposureDuration` and the exposure, gain and offset limits, and the Cover Lite's `OpenCover`/`CloseCover`/`HaltCover`) threw `NotImplemented` before checking the connection, and GPhoto `BayerOffsetX/Y` threw `InvalidOperation` (geometry not yet known). They now check the connection first and answer `NotImplemented` only once connected; connected answers do not change. The contract sweep now probes every driver's unsupported members while disconnected and accepts only `NotConnected`, `InvalidValue` or a clean return.
- **Player One camera `ReadoutMode` writes other than 0 answer `InvalidValue`** (AlpacaCore, issue #1919): `ReadoutModes` lists only `Normal`, so any other index is out of range. It answered `NotImplemented`, connected or not; it now answers `InvalidValue`, as GPhoto and the iGuider camera do.
