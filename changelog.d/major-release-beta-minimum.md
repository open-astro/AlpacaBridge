### Changed (docs)
- **Beta channel: a major release spends at least four weeks in beta** (docs/beta-channel.md, `/bump-release`): promoting an `X.0.0` now also needs 28 days since its first beta tag, on top of 14 days since the last beta tag. The hardware checks a release waits on are tracked in an `X.Y.0 promotion` milestone, which must be empty before promotion.
