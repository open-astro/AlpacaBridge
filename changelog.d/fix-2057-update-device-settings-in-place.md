### Added

- **In-place telescope optics update**, AlpacaHTTP, issue #2057. `POST`/`PUT /management/v1/updatedevice` sets a registered telescope's `apertureDiameter` and `focalLength` (metres) on the live driver and in its persisted entry without removing the device, so the connection and `uniqueId` stay; unknown keys, non-telescope or unregistered devices and negative, non-finite or wrongly typed values are refused with HTTP 200 and a non-zero `ErrorNumber`, and a cross-origin request is refused with 403. A `deviceNumber` that overflows int is refused instead of wrapping onto another device. The Bisque, Celestron, iOptron, OnStep, SkyWatcher and SynScan telescope drivers now keep the aperture diameter and focal length in atomics and derive ApertureArea from the diameter on read, so a live update is race-free and ApertureArea returns to 0 when the diameter is set to 0 (`Bisque ApertureArea follows the diameter and returns to 0 (#2057)`). Covered by the `updatedevice` block in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp`.
