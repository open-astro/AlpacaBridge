### Fixed

- **A Gemini focuser whose USB cable is pulled reads Connected false at once and reconnects with one Connect**, Gemini focuser driver (Automatic Astro Focuser Pro), issue #1723, decision 0009 rule 2. A hangup on a reply read, or `EIO`/`ENXIO`/`ENODEV` on a command write, used to throw a `DriverException` "Serial link lost (errno N)" while `Connected` stayed true and the dead port stayed claimed, until the state poll's 30 s link-loss bound ran out. The wrapper now releases the port at once, `Connected` reads false, every member that talks to the focuser throws `NotConnected`, `DeviceState` is empty, the state poll ends, and `get_link_fault()` gives the loss reason until the next Connect or Disconnect. Nothing reopens the port until the client's next Connect, which reopens it without a Disconnect first; a pull during the connect's seed (firmware query, first poll or temperature read) fails that Connect with `NotConnected`, and a pull during a poll's temperature read ends the poll in that cycle. No loss time is stamped (no #521 motion-on-relink consumer). Calls on a pulled focuser already failed before this change, so nothing that worked stops working. `util::PolledState` gains `lose_link()`, which the poll uses to end at once on that evidence. Tests: the seven `Gemini Focuser Link -` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_gemini_focuser_snapshot.cpp` and `PolledState - lose_link sets lost at once and the poll ends and calls on_lost once` (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_polled_state.cpp`). Pty fake evidence only; not checked on the rig.
