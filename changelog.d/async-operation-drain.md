### Fixed

- **A start during a disconnect's join no longer leaves a body running**, `util::AsyncOperation` (every driver on the slot), issue #1713. `cancel_all_and_join()` takes the bodies out under the slot mutex and joins them outside it; a `start()` in that window installed a new body that nothing joined, so it could outlive the disconnect. Such a `start()` now throws `InvalidOperation` and changes nothing, also when it was waiting at the stale bound; a `start()` after the call returns works as before. The OnStep telescope now joins its status-poll and pulse-end slots at every connect as well, so a pulse-end body a `PulseGuide` started after a Disconnect's join cannot run on into the next session. Covered by "a start() while cancel_all_and_join() joins is refused" and "a start() waiting at the stale bound is refused by cancel_all_and_join()" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_async_operation.cpp`, and "a reconnect joins a pulse-end body started after the disconnect's join" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_onstep_telescope_snapshot.cpp`.
