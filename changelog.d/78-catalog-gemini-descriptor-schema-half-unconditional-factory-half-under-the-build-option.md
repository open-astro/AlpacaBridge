### Changed
- **Gemini focuser, flat panel and Power & Data Hub switch moved into the device catalog** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, issue #950): one schema (`gemini_schema.cpp`, every build) and one factory (`gemini_catalog.cpp`, only under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_GEMINI`) replace the router's three register arms, its sanitize branch and three driver includes; the "Gemini support not enabled" text is unchanged (tests `Builtin catalog - register_builtin_factories makes the Gemini devices available only when built`, `Builtin catalog - register_builtin_schemas describes the Gemini devices in every build`). The factories call the hardware-free `create_*_by_index` paths, so no port scan runs at registration (the laziness cases in `test_gemini_focuser_deferred_connect.cpp` are unchanged). The switch still refuses an unknown `switchType`, a `serial` connection with no `portPath` and a negative `hubIndex` with the same texts, and a saved config the catalog can only warn about is refused by the factory instead of auto-detecting (test `Builtin catalog - Gemini switch normalize refuses a bad switchType, a missing port or a negative hubIndex`). `GET /management/v1/devicecatalog` now lists the three `gemini` descriptors, and a saved Gemini entry keeps only the fields its device type declares (a flat panel drops a stale `focuserIndex`; test `Builtin catalog - Gemini sanitize keeps the declared fields per device type`). With Gemini support off, an unknown `switchType` now answers "Unknown Gemini switchType" instead of "support not enabled". `scripts/check_layering.py`'s AlpacaHTTP vendor-include baseline drops from 20 to 17.
