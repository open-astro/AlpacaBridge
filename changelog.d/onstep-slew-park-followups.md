### Fixed

- **OnStep: a slew or sync while the mount homes with home sensors is refused** (follow-up to issue #1379): such a home runs as a guide move, not a GOTO, so the GOTO-running check missed it; the slew ended the waiting `FindHome` and the mount refused it as already moving. It now throws `InvalidOperation` ("<member> is not allowed while the mount is homing") before anything is sent. Test: `test_onstep_telescope.cpp` "OnStep Telescope Driver - a slew or sync while the mount homes is refused".
- **OnStep: `IsPulseGuiding` ends when AbortSlew or a MoveAxis stop on its axis ends the pulse**: it read true until the pulse's own end time, up to 10 s after the mount had stopped it. Test: `test_onstep_telescope.cpp` "OnStep Telescope Driver - AbortSlew and a MoveAxis stop on its axis end IsPulseGuiding".
- **OnStep: a Park the mount answers "already parked" is a no-op, and a FindHome it answers "mount parked" is `InvalidWhileParked`**: OnStep 4.x gives these codes when the driver's status cache still read the mount unparked; the Park threw `InvalidOperation` and the FindHome gave the wrong error code. Test: `test_onstep_telescope.cpp` "OnStep Telescope Driver - a park answered already parked is a no-op and a home answered parked is InvalidWhileParked".
- **OnStep: a no-op Park or FindHome drops an earlier body's kept refusal**: `Slewing` kept raising a refusal from a Park or FindHome that had failed before, after a new `Park` or `FindHome` call that had nothing to do. Test: `test_onstep_telescope.cpp` "OnStep Telescope Driver - a no-op Park or FindHome drops an earlier body's kept refusal".
- **OnStep: a site the connect could not read throws the link fault while faulted** (issue #1659): `SiteLatitude`, `SiteLongitude`, `SiderealTime` and `DestinationSideOfPier` fetched it again, waited out the reply timeout under the driver's lock and swallowed the failure (`DestinationSideOfPier` then answered from a default site). `SideOfPier` now reads the position before the site, so a reply still clears the fault. Test: `test_onstep_link_loss.cpp` "OnStep link loss - a site the connect could not read throws the fault while faulted".
- **OnStep: the mount clock read rejects a local time past 24 h and a month or day past 255** (follow-up to issue #1626): `std::chrono` wrapped a month or day above 255 into range, so `257/01/26` passed the calendar check. Test: `test_onstep_telescope.cpp` "OnStep Telescope Driver - UTCDate throws InvalidOperation when the mount clock cannot be read".

### Changed (docs)

- `onstep.instructions.md` and decision 0009's OnStep Adoption row: the homing refusal, `IsPulseGuiding` ending on a stop, the park and home codes 9 and 18, the no-op Park or FindHome clearing a kept refusal, the clock read's ranges, and a site the connect could not read throwing the fault while faulted.
