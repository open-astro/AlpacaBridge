### Changed

- **The iOptron iMate PowerBox and ToupTek StellaVita switches share the soft-PWM engine** (AlpacaCore, issue #2722). Both boards now run as `util::PowerPortSwitchDriver<util::OwnedSoftPwmPorts<util::GpiodLineBackend>>`, as the ASIAIR switches do ([decision 0027](docs/decisions/0027-gpio-soft-pwm-composition.md)), and their two port wrappers (`ioptron_powerbox_wrapper`, `touptek_powerbox_wrapper`) and the `TouptekPowerboxSDK` and `IoptronGpioBackend` seams are deleted: `util::GpioLineBackend` is the seam. Clients see no change: the same names, descriptions, UniqueIDs, port tables, PWM defaults (50 Hz iMate, 100 Hz StellaVita), persisted config fields, connect that changes no line and disconnect that never power-cycles a port. Backed by "a PWM port bit-bangs its duty at the configured frequency" in `test_ioptron_switch.cpp` and `test_touptek_stellavita_gpio.cpp`, which read the high edge at t0 and the low edge at half a period from `FakeGpioLineBackend` over `FakeTaskClock`.
- **A StellaVita port set read-only is not claimed** (AlpacaCore, issue #2722): the engine claims writable lines only, so a read-only port reads on without a GPIO line behind it, where the old wrapper read its line. Backed by "the claim covers the writable ports on the configured chip" in `test_touptek_stellavita_gpio.cpp` (GPIO 18, 10 and 17 claimed, GPIO 4 not).
- **A failed PWM write on the iMate or StellaVita now stops that port and answers DriverException from SetSwitchValue until the next Connect** (AlpacaCore, issue #2722), the engine rule of decision 0027 ("a failed PWM write logs ERROR, stops that port's worker and no other" in `test_soft_pwm_ports.cpp`).

### Added (tests)

- **iMate and StellaVita switch cases run over `FakeGpioLineBackend` and `FakeTaskClock`** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #2722): the connect, power-on state, saved-state and held-line cases of both switches, the contract sweep tier-2 hosts, the `[stress]` registrations and the fake ConformU factories use the fake backend (bundled with a clock in `fake_ioptron_gpio.h` and `fake_touptek_powerbox_sdk.h`) instead of the removed wrapper fakes, with no real sleep. `test_touptek_powerbox_seam.cpp` becomes `test_touptek_stellavita_gpio.cpp`; the cases of the deleted fake and its locked decorator go with them.
- **`FakeGpioLineBackend` refuses what libgpiod refuses** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #2722): a second `open()` while its lines are claimed throws DriverException, and a write while closed or past the claimed lines fails, as they do on the chip ([decision 0015](docs/decisions/0015-sdk-seams.md) rule 4). Backed by "FakeGpioLineBackend refuses a second open and a write while closed or out of range" in `test_soft_pwm_ports.cpp`.
