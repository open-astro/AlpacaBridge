### Fixed

- **NotConnected before NotImplemented while disconnected**, Celestron NexStar and CGX, iOptron mount, switch and iGuider, OnStep, Player One (and the iOptron camera alias), QHY, SVBONY, ToupTek (and the Altair alias), ToupTek StellaVita and WeeWX drivers, issue #1919. A member the driver does not implement (for example `SlewToAltAz`, `SideOfPier` writes, `FastReadout`, `SubExposureDuration`, `Gains`, async switch writes, a read-only switch id) now answers `NotConnected` (0x407) while the device is disconnected, and `NotImplemented` (0x400) only once connected. Covered by the "operations throw NotConnected" case of the contract sweep (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_contract_sweep.cpp`, `not_implemented_probes`). The ZWO, Sky-Watcher, Bisque and SynScan drivers and the Gemini cover methods are pending on the same issue.
