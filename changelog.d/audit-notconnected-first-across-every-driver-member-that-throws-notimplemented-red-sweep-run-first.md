### Fixed

- **NotConnected before NotImplemented while disconnected**, Celestron NexStar and CGX, iOptron mount, switch and iGuider, OnStep, Player One (and the iOptron camera alias), QHY, SVBONY, ToupTek (and the Altair alias), ToupTek StellaVita, gPhoto camera and WeeWX drivers, issue #1919. A member the driver does not implement (for example `SlewToAltAz`, `SideOfPier` writes, `FastReadout`, `SubExposureDuration`, `Gains`, async switch writes, a read-only switch id, gPhoto `Offset`) now answers `NotConnected` (0x407) while the device is disconnected, and `NotImplemented` (0x400) only once connected. Covered by the "operations throw NotConnected" case of the contract sweep (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_contract_sweep.cpp`, `not_implemented_probes`). The ZWO, Sky-Watcher, Bisque and SynScan drivers and the Gemini cover methods are pending on the same issue. The iOptron mount's `DoesRefraction`, rate and `SideOfPier` members also read the connection state under the driver mutex.
