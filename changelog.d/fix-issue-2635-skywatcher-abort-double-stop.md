### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher: a goto cancelled by AbortSlew no longer sends both axis stops a second time**, Sky-Watcher direct driver, issue #2635. AbortSlew stops both axes, confirms the stop and ends the goto. The goto's cancelled body then unwound through its failure path and sent `:L` on both axes again, taking the board link just after AbortSlew had returned, when a client may already be sending its next command. The board ignores a stop on a stopped axis, so nothing moved. The body now leaves the axes to an AbortSlew whose stops were answered, as Park and FindHome already did (#2604). A goto cancelled by a disconnect, or one that fails on its own, still stops both axes. Covered by "SkyWatcher async - a goto cancelled by AbortSlew sends no stop as its body unwinds (#2635)" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_skywatcher_async_slew_failure.cpp`.
