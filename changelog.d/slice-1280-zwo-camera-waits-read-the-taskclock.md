### Changed
- **ZWO camera waits and deadlines read the task clock** (ZWO, issue #1280): the first-temperature retry sleep, the pulse-guide end deadline and turn-off sleep, and the 250 ms exposure-retry pacing window now go through `util::TaskClock`, so tests advance a `FakeTaskClock` instead of sleeping. `create_zwo_camera`, `create_zwo_camera_by_index` and `create_zwo_camera_bound` take the clock as a trailing parameter that defaults to the real one (`zwo_camera_driver.h`; `zwo_catalog.cpp` and the router are unchanged), so the server's behaviour is unchanged. Tests: `ZWO camera clock - first CCDTemperature retry sleeps on the task clock`, `ZWO camera clock - IsPulseGuiding ends at the task clock deadline`, `ZWO camera clock - pulse guide turns off after the duration on the task clock` and `ZWO camera clock - exposure retries are paced by the task clock`.
