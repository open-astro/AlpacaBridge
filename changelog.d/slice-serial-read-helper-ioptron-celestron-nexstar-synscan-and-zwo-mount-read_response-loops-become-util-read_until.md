### Changed

- **iOptron, Celestron NexStar, SynScan and ZWO mount replies are read with `util::read_until`** (mount wrappers, issue #1644, part of #1604): the four connected-path `read_response()` loops, over serial and TCP, no longer poll byte by byte with a 10 ms pacing sleep; they wait in `poll()` for the `#` terminator (or, where the `#` is optional, for the `#` or a 100 ms idle gap, whichever comes first, through the new `ReadSpec::Stop::TerminatorOrIdleGap` in `util/serial_read.h`), with the same timeouts and bytes as before: when the timeout runs out, the next byte within one read slice (100 ms over serial, 200 ms over TCP) still settles the reply, as it did in the byte-at-a-time loop, so a late `#` ends its own reply instead of answering the next command, and a reply that starts after the timeout is still a timeout (a Celestron or SynScan binary payload is the exception: each payload byte within one read slice of the last still counts, so one that starts after the timeout is read whole, as before). A line that goes away while a command waits now reads as a hangup at once: the iOptron, Celestron and SynScan wrappers throw a `DriverException` ending "link lost (hangup): <errno text>" instead of timing out after the full reply window, and the ZWO mount latches `link_lost()` as before. Tests: `iOptron mount - a hangup while a command waits for its reply reads as a hangup`, `Celestron mount - a hangup while a command waits for its reply reads as a hangup`, `SynScan mount - a hangup while a command waits for its reply reads as a hangup`, `ZWO mount - a hangup while a command waits for its reply latches the link lost` and, per wrapper, `<mount> - a read that does not require the '#' ends at the '#'`, `<mount> - a '#' that arrives within one read slice after the timeout ends its own reply` `<mount> - a reply that starts after the timeout is still a timeout`, and `Celestron mount - a binary reply that starts after the timeout is read whole`, `SynScan mount - a binary reply that starts after the timeout is read whole`, `iOptron mount - a TCP flush waits one read slice for each stale byte` and `iOptron mount - a blind-command ack drain reads on while acks keep coming` (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_mount_read_until_hangup.cpp`); the new stop in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_serial_read.cpp` (`read_until - a TerminatorOrIdleGap read ends at the terminator and leaves later bytes in the fd`, `... with no terminator ends after the idle gap`, `read_until_socket - a TerminatorOrIdleGap read ends at the terminator or after the idle gap`).
