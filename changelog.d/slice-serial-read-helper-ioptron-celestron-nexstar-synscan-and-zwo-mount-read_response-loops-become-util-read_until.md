### Changed

- **iOptron, Celestron NexStar, SynScan and ZWO mount replies are read with `util::read_until`** (mount wrappers, issue #1644, part of #1604): the four connected-path `read_response()` loops, over serial and TCP, no longer poll byte by byte with a 10 ms pacing sleep; they wait in `poll()` for the `#` terminator (or, with no terminator, for a 100 ms idle gap), with the same timeouts and bytes as before. A line that goes away while a command waits now reads as a hangup at once: the iOptron, Celestron and SynScan wrappers throw a `DriverException` ending "link lost (hangup): <errno text>" instead of timing out after the full reply window, and the ZWO mount latches `link_lost()` as before. Tests: `iOptron mount - a hangup while a command waits for its reply reads as a hangup`, `Celestron mount - a hangup while a command waits for its reply reads as a hangup`, `SynScan mount - a hangup while a command waits for its reply reads as a hangup` and `ZWO mount - a hangup while a command waits for its reply latches the link lost` (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_mount_read_until_hangup.cpp`).
