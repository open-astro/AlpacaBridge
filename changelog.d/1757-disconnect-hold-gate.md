### Fixed

- **"A Disconnect during a connect returns at once" no longer fails on a busy runner** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1757). The contract sweep's case and the Gemini flat panel, WandererAstro rotator, Astroasis and Celestron focuser cases read Connecting right after `disconnect()` with nothing holding the connect in place. If the connect thread had not yet reached the fake's hold, the Disconnect dropped it at the driver's first cancel point, and a timed hold (150-300 ms) or timed opener could also end in a scheduler pause, so Connecting read false with a correct driver. It ejected merge-queue runs for the iOptron telescope, QHY camera, Gemini flat panel and WandererAstro switch rows. The new `AlpacaCore/tests/connect_hold_gate.h` (`ConnectHoldGate`, `connect_hold()`) holds the connect until the case releases it (capped at 5 s, so a disconnect that joins the connect still fails the case instead of hanging it), and the cases wait until the connect is inside the hold before they disconnect. No driver changes.
