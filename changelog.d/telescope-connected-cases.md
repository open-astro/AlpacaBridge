### Added (tests)
- **Required case 7 "State machine" and ZWO mount case 2 "Device metadata"** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1863): a `State machine` case each for the Bisque telescope (Slewing, AtPark, AtHome and IsPulseGuiding NotConnected while disconnected and false while idle, then Park and Unpark), the Player One Phoenix and ZWO EFW filter wheels (Position NotConnected, the idle slot, -1 while moving, the target, and across a Disconnect), and the ZWO EAF focuser and CAA rotator (IsMoving NotConnected, false while idle, true right after a move, false after Halt), plus `ZWO Mount Telescope Driver - Device metadata` with the exact description, driver info, version, interface version and fallback UniqueID. `FakeBisqueMount` now clears its parked state on `Unpark`, as TheSkyX does.
- **iOptron telescope connected cases** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1864): SiteLatitude, SiteLongitude, SiderealTime, SideOfPier (from the hour angle, both sides) and TrackingRate (read from the mount and written as `:RTn#`) are value-checked over `FakeIoptronMount`, whose `:GLS` tracking-rate digit is now scriptable and follows `:RTn#`; SetSideOfPier, the two rate setters and SyncToAltAz assert their NotImplemented codes with nothing sent, the rate getters read 0.0, SlewSettleTime, ApertureDiameter, ApertureArea and FocalLength read back what was written, and SetPark sends the mount's current Alt/Az as `:SPH`/`:SPA` without parking.
- **Bisque telescope connected optics and rate case** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1865): ApertureDiameter, ApertureArea and FocalLength read back on a connected driver, and RightAscensionRate and DeclinationRate read 0.0, with no TheSkyX transaction.
- **ZWO mount connected cases** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1866): the exact TrackingRates list, each rate sent as its command (`:TQ`, `:TL`, `:TS`) and read back, InvalidValue for the King rate, CanSetTracking, EquatorialSystem Topocentric, both rate getters 0.0, and SetPark sending `:Sp01` without parking, with a mount refusal raised as InvalidOperation.
- **SynScan connected rate case** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1867): CanSetTracking is true, and DeclinationRate and RightAscensionRate read 0.0 connected and NotConnected after a Disconnect.
- **Celestron NexStar and CGX connected capability cases** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1868): CanSetPierSide, CanSetRightAscensionRate and CanSetDeclinationRate are false on a connected driver, and SlewToAltAz is NotConnected while disconnected and MethodNotImplemented once connected, with no Alt/Az GOTO or AUX packet sent.
