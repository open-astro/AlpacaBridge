### Fixed

- **An iOptron mount whose USB cable is pulled reads Connected false at once and reconnects with one Connect**, iOptron telescope driver, issue #1723, decision 0009 rule 2. Over serial a hangup on a reply read used to throw a `DriverException` "iOptron mount serial link lost (hangup)", a hangup in a blind command's ack drain (Park, Unpark, AbortSlew, PulseGuide, Tracking) was taken for a silent mount and the command reported success, and `EIO`/`ENXIO`/`ENODEV` on a write threw a `DriverException` "Failed to send command", while `Connected` stayed true for ever and the dead port stayed claimed. The wrapper now releases the port at once, `Connected` reads false, every member throws `NotConnected` (no Slewing, AtPark, Tracking or site value from before the pull is served, the GOTO settle re-measure, the Park park-position read and the HAE29C wedged-park finalizer included), `DeviceState` is empty, an async slew body ends on its failed dispatch, and `get_link_fault()` gives the loss reason until the next Connect or Disconnect. Nothing reopens the port until the client's next Connect, which reopens it without a Disconnect first; a pull during the connect's reads fails that Connect with `NotConnected`. A mount that stops answering keeps the existing fault latch (three failed exchanges in a row, `DriverException`, `Connected` true), which now refuses `IsPulseGuiding` too. While it stands, the position, Alt/Az and status reads try the mount again (one status read at most every 5 s on the driver's task clock, nothing sent in between): the latch used to clear only through AbortSlew or a reconnect, and now a good reply clears it, while writes still refuse. A fault that stands for 30 s with no good reply (`util::kLinkStalenessBound`) is now a lost link, found by the next call other than `IsPulseGuiding` (it stays lock-free) and `AbortSlew` (it skips `check_connected()` so its stop still goes out), or by the base client-silence watchdog's Slewing check with no client call, on serial and Wi-Fi alike. Over TCP a hangup is otherwise unchanged: a failed exchange for the latch. No loss time is stamped (no #521 motion-on-relink consumer). Not breaking: calls on a pulled mount already failed before this change and now fail with `NotConnected` instead of `DriverException`; the `IsPulseGuiding` refusal only affects a mount whose every other member was already refused by the latch. Tests: the twenty-two `iOptron Link - ...` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_ioptron_telescope.cpp`, on a new pty fake (`PtyIoptronMount` in `AlpacaCore/tests/fake_ioptron_mount.h`) and the TCP fake. Fake evidence only; not checked on the rig.
