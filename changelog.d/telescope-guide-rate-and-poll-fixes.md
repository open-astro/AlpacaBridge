### Fixed

- **Celestron CGX and Bisque refuse an out-of-range single-axis guide rate with `InvalidValue` while disconnected** (AlpacaCore Celestron CGX direct USB and Bisque telescope drivers, issue #1786): a `GuideRateRightAscension` or `GuideRateDeclination` write outside the mount's range (CGX 10 to 100% of sidereal, Bisque 0 to 1x sidereal, or not finite) answered `NotConnected` on a disconnected mount, because the base class read the pair through the connection-checking getter before the range check. Each driver now validates the one value first, then checks the connection and stores only that axis under one lock; an in-range write on a disconnected mount is still `NotConnected`, writing one axis keeps the other and a refused write keeps both. Celestron NexStar and iOptron only refuse non-finite rates, which the router's number parsing already rejects, so they are unchanged. This closes the remaining part of #1786 after SynScan, Sky-Watcher and ZWO.
- **Bisque: a cancelled or left-behind Park no longer sets AtPark** (AlpacaCore Bisque telescope driver, issue #2062): `park()` polls IsParked without a lock and wrote `parked_ = true` whenever the mount reported parked, so a Park followed by AbortSlew or Unpark, or by a Disconnect and Connect, could end with `AtPark` true and slews refused for a park the client had cancelled. AbortSlew, Unpark and every disconnect path (the Disconnect and the relink of a lost link included, which now also clear `parking_`) bump `park_epoch_` under the driver mutex as a new Park does; a retired Park returns quietly at its next poll boundary (no IsParked polling into a later session) and writes neither `parked_` nor `parking_`, including when the retirement lands while its last IsParked reply is on the wire. An uncancelled Park still ends with `AtPark` true. A Park interrupted by a Disconnect now returns instead of answering `NotConnected`.
- **Sky-Watcher: a sub-floor RightAscensionRate written during an RA pulse is duty-cycled after it, not driven at the floor rate** (AlpacaCore Sky-Watcher direct driver, issue #1720): the pulse-end restore wrote the effective RA rate with `:I`, which clamps at the slow-mode floor, so RA tracked at the floor rate until the duty worker's first burst. The restore, the re-check inside its in-place branch and the dispatch-failure recovery now hand a duty-regime drive (a pre-armed duty rate, or a sub-floor or zero effective rate) to `apply_ra_drive_locked()`, the same applier `Tracking=true` uses, which stops RA for the duty worker, and skip the post-stop rate verify.
- **SynScan: Disconnect no longer waits out a silent handset's read timeout** (AlpacaCore SynScan driver and protocol wrapper, issue #2070): the wrapper holds its lock across a reply read, so with a handset that stopped answering, `set_connected(false)` queued behind the read in flight for the full 5 s response timeout, plus the joins behind it. The disconnect (and the destructor) of the connected session now raises a wrapper interrupt that ends the read in flight and every later one at once with `NotConnected`, with no fault text and no link loss; it is cleared when the disconnect ends and by the next connect. A disconnect of a second, never-connected SynScan device does not touch the connected one's exchanges. The shared read loop gains `util::ReadSpec::cancel` (outcome `Cancelled`) for it; no other wrapper sets it.

### Changed

- **iOptron: connect reads `:GUT#` once** (AlpacaCore iOptron mount driver and protocol wrapper, issue #1784): the UTC sample and the site's timezone and DST now come from one reply (`read_utc_reply()`, `parse_utc_time()`, `get_site_info(gut_reply)`) instead of two exchanges. The UTC field keeps its strict 18-character check and the site fields their 17-character one, so a reply with an unusable UTC still primes the site. UTCDate and the site values after connect are unchanged; a site refresh past its cache TTL still sends its own `:GUT#`.

### Added (docs)

- **Celestron CGX direct USB on a Raspberry Pi 5** (issue #2234): `docs/celestron-cgx-pi5-usb.md` records why the Pi 5's USB controller dies when `cdc_acm` binds the CGX (`SET_LINE_CODING` that the CGX never completes) and the reporter's opt-in workaround (a guard script, a udev rule and a modprobe.d file that bind the CGX's data interface to `usbserial`, with `portPath` `/dev/serial/by-id/usb-Celestron_CGX__CGX_v1-if01-port0`), its undo steps and why it is not a `host_setup.sh` default. Pointers in `SUPPORTED-DRIVERS.md` and the Celestron instructions.

### Added (tests)

- **Guide-rate precedence and per-axis cases for CGX and Bisque** (issue #1786): `individual guide-rate range errors precede NotConnected` and `individual guide-rate writes keep the other axis` in `test_celestron_cgx_telescope.cpp` and `test_bisque_telescope.cpp`.
- **Bisque park retirement cases** (issue #2062): five `[park-race]` cases in `test_bisque_telescope.cpp` (AbortSlew, Unpark, a reconnect, a lost link's Disconnect or relink, and an AbortSlew during the last IsParked exchange); the #1838 stale-Park timeout case is now `a retired Park's exit leaves a newer Park refusing slews (#1838)`, since a retired Park no longer polls to its timeout.
- **Sky-Watcher sub-floor restore cases** (issue #1720): the mid-pulse sub-floor case in `test_skywatcher_async.cpp` now checks that the first RA command after the pulse is a stop, and `a pulse dispatch failure after the live :I hands a sub-floor RA rate to the duty worker` covers the recovery.
- **SynScan disconnect against a silent handset** (issue #2070): `a disconnect interrupts a read a silent handset never answers` over TCP and serial in `test_synscan_telescope_snapshot.cpp`, and `read_until - ReadSpec::cancel ends a waiting read with Cancelled` in `test_serial_read.cpp`.
- **iOptron one-`:GUT#` connect** (issue #1784): the `:GUT` counts in `test_ioptron_telescope.cpp` are one per connect, and `the connect's one :GUT# reply also primes the site` checks the site after connect.
