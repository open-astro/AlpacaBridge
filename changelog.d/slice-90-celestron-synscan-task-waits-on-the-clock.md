### Changed
- **Celestron and SynScan task waits run on the task clock** (Celestron, SynScan): `create_celestron_telescope_with_site`, `create_celestron_telescope_deferred`, `create_synscan_telescope_with_site` and `create_synscan_telescope_deferred` (and the `_auto` forms) take an optional `util::TaskClock&` (default `util::default_task_clock()`), and every cancellable task wait, settle sleep, slew deadline and pulse-guide timer of both drivers goes through it instead of `steady_clock` and `sleep_for`. Behaviour on a real clock is unchanged; pointing time (UTC extrapolation) stays on the host clock. The async slew-failure suites drive the position-cache and pulse timers with `FakeTaskClock::advance()`.

### Fixed
- **A cancel stored by a reaper can no longer be missed by a parked task** (Celestron, SynScan): each reaper stored its cancel flag and called `task_cv_.notify_all()` without passing through `task_mutex_`, so a task between its flag check and its block missed the wakeup and slept out its whole wait; the new `notify_task_waiters()` locks `task_mutex_` first (same shape as the Sky-Watcher fix). Covered by "a reaper's cancel is not lost between a parked task's check and its block" in `test_celestron_async_slew_failure.cpp` and `test_synscan_async_slew_failure.cpp`.
