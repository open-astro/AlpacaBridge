### Changed

- **Device names are brand + model**, AlpacaCore drivers, AlpacaHTTP, web UI, issue #2215. Every driver's `Name` is now the brand followed by the model, with nothing appended ([decision 0024](docs/decisions/0024-device-names.md)). The Sky-Watcher direct driver's `Sky-Watcher EQM-35 Pro (EQMOD, eps: measured)` is `Sky-Watcher EQM-35 Pro`, and the SynScan driver drops `(SynScan)`, so one mount has one name on both paths. The ZWO focuser reads its model with `EAFGetType()` at connect, so an EAFN is `ZWO EAFN` rather than `EAF`; the ZWO filter wheel and rotator, the ToupTek wheel and focuser and the camera SDK names gain their brand when the SDK string lacks it (`util::branded_name()`). OnStepX is named from its `:GVC#` product description, else the `:GVP#` product. The QHY wheel is `QHY CFW`, the Celestron direct mount `Celestron CGX/CGX-L`, Bisque `Bisque Paramount`, the ASIAIR Plus switches drop `(RK3568)` and `(Pi CM4)`, camera dew heaters and thermal switches carry the camera model (`ZWO ASI2600MC Pro Dew Heater`), and an unknown mount is `<Brand> Telescope`, never `Mount`. What used to follow the model goes in the Description, which `configureddevices` now lists and the device card shows, so the EQMOD eps source stays visible. Two new contract-sweep cases check every driver's name, disconnected and connected. A client that remembers a device by name rather than UniqueID may need to pick it again once.
