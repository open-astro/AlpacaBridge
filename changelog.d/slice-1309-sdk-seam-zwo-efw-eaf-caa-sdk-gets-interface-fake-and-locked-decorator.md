### Changed
- **ZWO filter wheel driver reaches the EFW SDK through an injectable interface** (AlpacaCore, issue #1309): `ZWOEFWSDK` (`zwo_efw_sdk.h`) is implemented by the existing `ZWOEFWSDKWrapper` singleton, and `create_zwo_efw_filterwheel` and `create_zwo_efw_filterwheel_by_index` gain overloads taking it. The two-argument factories still pass the real wrapper, so runtime behaviour is unchanged. The EAF and CAA seams were already on main; they now share scripted `FakeZWOEAFSDK` and `FakeZWOCAASDK` headers (moved out of the focuser and rotator tests) and, with the new `FakeZWOEFWSDK`, a `Locked` decorator each. New fake-SDK cases in `test_zwo_seam_fakes.cpp`, three connected `[stress]` registrations in `test_zwo_concurrency_stress.cpp` and three tier-2 rows in the contract sweep cover them. ConformU was not re-run.
