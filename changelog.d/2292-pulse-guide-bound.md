### Fixed

- **PulseGuide refuses a duration past a bound, and a failed QHY frame read no longer ends the server** (AlpacaCore, issue #2292). The Sky-Watcher, SynScan and ZWO AM mounts and the ZWO, Player One, SVBONY, ToupTek and QHY cameras accepted any positive `Duration`; above 60 s (`kDefaultMaxPulseGuideMs`) they now answer `InvalidValue` before anything moves. The ZWO AM used to shorten a longer pulse to 60 s and the QHY camera to 65.5 s without saying so; both refuse it now. Drivers whose protocol caps a pulse (iOptron, Celestron NexStar and CGX, OnStep, Bisque) keep their own bound. The QHY camera's frame read was the one SDK call in its exposure worker outside a `try`: a throw from it (the SDK wrapper's "camera not open" after a disconnect) ended the process; the exposure now fails with that text instead.
