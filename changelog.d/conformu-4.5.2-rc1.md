### Changed (tooling)
- **ConformU standard moves to 4.5.2-rc.1 (build 56111)** (`/conformu`, AGENTS.md, `/driver-build`, OnStep notes): the maintainer's beta download now serves 4.5.2-rc.1, which keeps the Telescope PulseGuide test declinations within ±65° (ASCOMInitiative/ConformU#35), so the four North/South issues at HA ±9 on mounts that report refracted coordinates (OnStepX on the TeSeek HM-17) no longer appear. `/conformu` now installs it from the project's `tools/conformu-4.5.2-rc.1` pre-release and stops on a sha256 mismatch, instead of from the unversioned beta URL, checks the installed build against it, and the dependency graph reports `ConformU@4.5.2-beta`. Reports made with 4.5.1 stay valid.
