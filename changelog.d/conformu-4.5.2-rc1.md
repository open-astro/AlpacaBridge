### Changed (tooling)
- **ConformU standard moves to 4.5.2-rc.1 (build 56111)** (`/conformu`, AGENTS.md, `/driver-build`, OnStep notes): 4.5.2-rc.1 keeps the Telescope PulseGuide test declinations within ±65° (ASCOMInitiative/ConformU#35), so the four North/South issues at HA ±9 on mounts that report refracted coordinates (OnStepX on the TeSeek HM-17) no longer appear. `/conformu` installed it from a sha256-checked project mirror until the ConformU 4.5.2 release (build 56139, functionally identical) replaced it as the standard (issue #1276); the mirror has been removed. Reports made with 4.5.1 stay valid.
