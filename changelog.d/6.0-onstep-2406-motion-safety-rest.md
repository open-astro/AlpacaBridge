### Fixed
- **OnStep Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore, issue #2406): a GOTO that ends more than 600 arcsec (10 arcmin) from its target is stopped, and Slewing reports the failure (a synchronous slew throws it) instead of reporting a landing. A GOTO the mount starts only after a status frame that shows none is still stopped 120 s after its start. The 120 s bound now also fires when the status poll cannot read the mount.

### Breaking changes
- **OnStep Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore, issue #2406): a reconnect after a link loss now always sends AbortSlew's stops (a park still running is left to finish). Before, an outage shorter than 5 s left a GOTO or MoveAxis running. A SyncToCoordinates or SyncToTarget after the first sync since the Connect is refused with InvalidValue when it would move the pointing by more than 5 degrees on the sky. The first sync of each session is not bounded, because an OnStep mount has no reference at power-up and that sync sets it. **After upgrading:** re-issue a GOTO or MoveAxis after a reconnect if it was still wanted, and do a large correction as the first sync after Connect, or as a GOTO and a sync near it.
