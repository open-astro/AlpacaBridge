### Fixed

- **SynScan: SiteLatitude and SiteLongitude no longer hold the driver while the handset ignores the site read** (SynScan, issue #1265; `synscan_telescope_driver.cpp`): the getters used to read the site from the handset under the driver mutex while it was unread, so a handset that ignores `w` made every other getter and `DeviceState` wait the full response timeout, once per retry window, while the getters returned 0.0. They now serve the site the status poll cached (as `SiderealTime` does, decision 0010) and send nothing to the handset; while disconnected they throw `NotConnected` and while the site is unread they throw `ValueNotSet` (0x402, "SynScan site is not known yet: ...") instead of returning 0.0. A configured, handset-read, poll-read or written site reads back as before. Covered by "SynScan snapshot - a site getter does not hold the driver while the handset ignores w", "SynScan snapshot - an unread site refuses SiteLatitude and SiteLongitude as not known yet" and "SynScan snapshot - a configured, handset-read or set site is served".
