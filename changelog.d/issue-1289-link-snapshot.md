### Fixed

- **OnStep: a Disconnect or a relink no longer races the unplug check** (issue #1289): `link_alive()` checks the serial node without the wrapper lock, so a Disconnect that landed between that check and the record left a loss recorded after the Disconnect, and the next Connect could send the #521 stops (`:Q#`, `:Qn/s/e/w#`); a relink that landed there saw its new link marked lost and closed. The loss is now recorded only while the node that was checked is still the current link, a Disconnect marks the link changed before it closes the port, and the close after a recorded loss checks again that the link is still the lost one. Tests: `test_onstep_link_loss.cpp` "OnStep link loss - a Disconnect during the node check records no loss", "... a Disconnect still closing the port records no loss", "... a reconnect during the node check keeps the new link" and "... a reconnect after the loss is recorded keeps the new link", and the TSan case `test_onstep_concurrency_stress.cpp` "OnStep wrapper - link_alive races connect and disconnect during an adapter swap".
