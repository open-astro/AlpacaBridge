### Changed

- **Bisque telescope poll through `PolledState`**, Bisque driver and `util::PolledState`, issue #2494. `PolledState` gains `begin_poll()` and `publish()`, and its probe may return `std::optional<Frame>`: `nullopt` means the device answered and the probe published itself, which counts as a good probe for the fault latch (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_polled_state.cpp`: a self-publishing probe clears a latched fault, a `publish` after a `write()` returns false, a `nullopt` probe that never publishes ages the frame to loss). The Bisque status poll now runs on it, so the driver no longer keeps its own fault latch, poll thread or loss flag; the slew outcome still arrives only with its frame, and a write between the poll's token and its publish drops both (`[bisque][snapshot][polled]` in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_bisque_telescope_snapshot.cpp`). One visible difference: a frame that no poll tick refreshed for 30 s (`util::kLinkStalenessBound`) now reads as a lost link, as on every other `PolledState` driver. Decision 0010 records the change. Validated on the fake TheSkyX only, no Paramount rig run.
