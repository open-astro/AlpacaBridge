### Breaking changes

- **Bisque PulseGuide is capped at 25 s instead of 30 s**, Bisque driver, issue #2494. A PulseGuide of 25001 to 30000 ms used to succeed and now returns `InvalidValue` (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_bisque_telescope.cpp`: the maximum duration is accepted and one more is InvalidValue). Upgrade step: a guider that sends Bisque pulses longer than 25 s splits them into pulses of 25 s or less.

### Changed

- **Bisque telescope poll through `PolledState`**, Bisque driver and `util::PolledState`, issue #2494. `PolledState` gains `begin_poll()` and `publish()`, and its probe may return `std::optional<Frame>`: `nullopt` means the device answered and the probe published itself, which counts as a good probe for the fault latch (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_polled_state.cpp`: a self-publishing probe clears a latched fault, a `publish` after a `write()` returns false, a `nullopt` probe that never publishes ages the frame to loss). The Bisque status poll now runs on it, so the driver no longer keeps its own fault latch, poll thread or loss flag; the slew outcome still arrives only with its frame, and a write between the poll's token and its publish drops both (`[bisque][snapshot][polled]` in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_bisque_telescope_snapshot.cpp`). One visible difference: a frame that no poll tick refreshed for 30 s (`util::kLinkStalenessBound`) now reads as a lost link, as on every other `PolledState` driver. FindHome is now an asynchronous send plus one-second `IsSlewComplete` polls, and PulseGuide is capped at 25 s (see Breaking changes), so neither holds the wire long enough to read as a lost link (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_bisque_telescope_snapshot.cpp`: a FindHome past 31 s and the longest pulse keep Connected true). A FindHome that times out after 60 s names the last `IsSlewComplete` error in its message, and a Park that times out after 120 s names the last `IsParked` error (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_bisque_telescope.cpp`: the timeout names the last IsSlewComplete error, the timeout names the last IsParked error). Decision 0010 records the change. Validated on the fake TheSkyX only, no Paramount rig run.
