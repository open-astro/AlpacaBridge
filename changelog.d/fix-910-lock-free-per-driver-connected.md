### Fixed

- **iOptron and Bisque telescopes: `Connected` no longer takes the lock a connect holds** (iOptron, Bisque, issue #910, refs #1600): `get_connected()` now reads an atomic that is raised only when the connect has finished and dropped first on a disconnect (Bisque also drops it when the link is declared lost), so it answers at once and reads false for as long as `Connecting` is true. Before, the iOptron getter waited for the whole connect handshake. Covered by "iOptron telescope - Connected answers at once and reads false while a connect is on the wire" in `test_ioptron_getter_locks.cpp` and "Bisque telescope - Connected answers at once and reads false while a connect is on the wire" in `test_bisque_getter_locks.cpp`. The blocking-getter list in `async_connectable.h` is now empty for telescopes, and `scripts/check_docs_drift.py` check 5 still reports `MISSING FROM THE BLOCKING LIST` if either getter takes a lock again.
