### Added
- **remotealpaca: Rotator of an upstream Alpaca server** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, web UI, issue #2708, slice 5 of #1753): the "Remote Alpaca" vendor now also serves the Rotator device of another Alpaca server as a device of this server, with the same four settings (upstream host, port, device number, poll period) and the same Connect, snapshot and link rules as the other proxied devices. `IsMoving`, `Position`, `MechanicalPosition`, `TargetPosition` and `Reverse` answer from the poll's snapshot; `CanReverse` and `StepSize` are read once at Connect and keep answering while the link is faulted. A member the upstream lacks answers the upstream's own `NotImplemented`, and an IRotatorV1 or V2 upstream's missing `MechanicalPosition`, `MoveMechanical` and `Sync` answer `NotImplemented` without being called. `Move`, `MoveAbsolute`, `MoveMechanical`, `Sync` and the `Reverse` write are forwarded in the command slot and written through, so `IsMoving` reads true at once after a move; `Halt` is forwarded even while the link is faulted. While the link is faulted the frame getters and every other command throw `DriverException`. Built under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_REMOTEALPACA`; fake evidence only, ConformU against the simulators is a rig step.
