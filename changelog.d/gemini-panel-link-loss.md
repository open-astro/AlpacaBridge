### Fixed

- **A Gemini flat panel whose USB cable is pulled reads Connected false at once and reconnects with one Connect**, Gemini flat panel drivers (Cover Lite, Automatic FlatPanel v2, Motorized Flat Panel V3), issue #1723, decision 0009 rule 2. A hangup on a reply read, or `EIO`/`ENXIO`/`ENODEV` on a command write, used to throw a `DriverException` "Serial link lost (errno N)" while `Connected` stayed true and the dead port stayed claimed. The wrapper now releases the port at once, `Connected` reads false, every member throws `NotConnected`, `DeviceState` is empty, and `get_link_fault()` gives the loss reason until the next Connect or Disconnect. Nothing reopens the port until the client's next Connect, which reopens it without a Disconnect first. A cover or calibrator command running when the cable is pulled fails with `NotConnected`; its kept error is cleared by that Connect. No loss time is stamped (no #521 motion-on-relink consumer). Calls on a pulled panel already failed before this change, so nothing that worked stops working. Tests: `Gemini Flat Panel Lite - a cable pulled during a command loses the link at once and Connect reopens`, `Gemini Flat Panel Lite - a write to a pulled adapter loses the link at once`, `Gemini Flat Panel Slots - a cable pulled during a cover move loses the link and a reconnect starts clean` and four more for the idle cover read, the connect state seed (Lite and Pro) and a Connect over a lost link (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_gemini_flatpanel.cpp`). Pty fake evidence only; not checked on the rig.
