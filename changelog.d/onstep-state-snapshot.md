### Changed

- **OnStep telescope: position, status and `DeviceState` are served from a state snapshot** (AlpacaCore, issue #1783, decision 0010; `onstep_telescope_driver.cpp`): one status poll reads the mount every second (`:GU#` and RA/Dec each tick, Alt/Az every second tick) and RightAscension, Declination, Altitude, Azimuth, Tracking, AtPark, AtHome, Slewing, SideOfPier and `DeviceState` answer from memory, so a getter never waits on the mount and the three 2 s caches and the getters' single read under the operation lock are gone (`test_onstep_telescope_snapshot.cpp`, "getters and DeviceState make no mount exchange at 100 ms per exchange"). `TimeStamp` is the time of the measurement ("DeviceState TimeStamp is when the poll measured, not when it was asked"). Client writes are read back at once, and a poll that sampled before a write cannot undo it ("a write is read back at once and a poll that sampled before it does not undo it"). Three failed poll ticks latch the link fault and a fault standing 30 s is a lost link, found by the poll ("a stale frame is served, three failed ticks refuse it, and past the bound the link is lost"); the 2 s faulted-read back-off of #1626 is gone, since no getter reads the mount. The poll starts with the connect and is joined before a disconnect closes the port ("the poll starts with the connect and ends with the disconnect").
- **OnStep: `IsPulseGuiding` reads true until the position after the pulse is measured** (#1783): a pulse guide adds one poll tick 50 ms after its end while the 1 s ticks run on, and `IsPulseGuiding` stays true until that frame is published (at most 2 s more), so a position read right after it goes false is not one taken mid-pulse ("IsPulseGuiding stays true until a frame sampled after the pulse is published", "a pulse guide keeps the poll's 1 s ticks and adds one at the pulse's end").
- **OnStep: a synchronous slew publishes the position where it ended** (#1783): RA, Dec, Alt and Az read right after `SlewToCoordinates` returns are the end values, with no poll tick between ("a synchronous slew publishes the position where it ended"); a failed end read keeps the last values and counts toward the link fault, as a failed poll tick does ("a failed end-of-slew read keeps the last values and counts toward the fault"). A control-path `:GU#` read that equals the frame does not drop the poll sample in flight ("a control-path status read drops the poll sample in flight only when it differs").
- **OnStep: the tracking restore after a GOTO is sent by the status poll** (#1230, #1783), under the same operation lock as the `:GU#` that read the GOTO ended, and not while a Park or FindHome is pending ("the poll restores tracking after a GOTO that ended without it, but not while a Park is pending"). Before, a `Slewing` read sent it.
- **`util::LockGate::single_read()` removed** (AlpacaCore, #1783; `util/lock_gate.h`): the OnStep getters were its only caller, and they no longer read the mount. `exchange()` and `StateLock` are unchanged; its cases in `test_lock_gate.cpp` went with it.
- **OnStep: the slew and sync GOTO check reads `:GU#` every time** (#1379, #1783), so a GOTO or home started from the hand controller is seen at once; it read the 2 s status cache before.

### Fixed

- **OnStep: `SiteLatitude` and `SiteLongitude` throw `InvalidOperation` until the coordinate is known** (#1783, decision 0019): a site the connect could not read answered 0.0. The status poll retries the mount's site ("the site getters throw until the site is known").
- **OnStep: AbortSlew reports a link lost at its tracking restore** (#1783): the restore swallowed every failure, `NotConnected` included, so AbortSlew reported success on a gone link (`test_onstep_link_loss.cpp`, "AbortSlew reports a link lost at its tracking restore").
