### Changed

- **Sky-Watcher slew, park, home and axis-stop bodies run in `util::AsyncOperation` slots** (Sky-Watcher): `SlewToCoordinatesAsync`, `SlewToTargetAsync`, `Park` and `FindHome` start their background body on one slot and each axis's `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` stop-completion body on a slot of its own, on the injected task clock, so a new command no longer waits for the body it replaces and a stop on one axis never supersedes the other axis's restore (`SkyWatcher slot - a stop on one axis does not supersede the other axis's stop body`). A slew started during FindHome no longer leaves `Slewing` true (`SkyWatcher slot - a slew that replaces a FindHome in flight does not leave Slewing true`). A failure after the initiator returned still makes `Slewing` throw until the next slew (`test_skywatcher_async_slew_failure.cpp`, unchanged).
- **`util::AsyncOperation::start()` reports a refused thread as an `AlpacaException`** (Sky-Watcher): an EAGAIN from the OS now reaches the caller as a `DriverException` and leaves the current body, the generation and the kept failure unchanged (`AsyncOperation - a refused thread is an AlpacaException and changes nothing`).
