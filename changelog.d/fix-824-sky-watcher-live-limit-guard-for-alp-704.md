### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher direct: the minimum-altitude and meridian limits now stop MoveAxis and tracking** (AlpacaCore, issue #824). With a limit set, a guard polls the axes every 250 ms while MoveAxis drives one and every 2 s while only tracking, and stops tracking and every MoveAxis axis the moment the motion carries the tube from at or above the altitude floor to below it, or the counterweight from inside the meridian limit to past it, with one WARNING log line naming the limit. Each stop is sent on its own, a Dec MoveAxis stop first, then tracking, then an RA MoveAxis stop, and a stop that fails (a tracking stop that times out on a long deceleration ramp, a link error) is tried again at the next poll. Motion that starts outside a limit is not stopped, so the mount can always be driven back; gotos, Park and FindHome are not watched, and a sync, which moves the reported position without moving an axis, is not read as a crossing; with both limits off (the default) the guard never starts. A disconnect waits for the guard to finish. A MoveAxis Dec stop that lands while a Tracking=false is still stopping the RA axis no longer restores the DeclinationRate offset, which made that Tracking=false fail as superseded. An East or West guide pulse on the tracking RA axis no longer loses the pulse distance driven since the last position read when it ends: with a limit set the guard's 2 s reads made ConformU measure PulseGuide RA changes short by up to 1.07 s of the 2.51 s expected on the EQM-35 Pro, and any client reading RA during a pulse could see the same jump. The web UI help for both limit fields now describes the live stop, and the meridian limit field no longer says it is not enforced.

### Added (tests)

- **Sky-Watcher live limit guard cases** (issue #824): ten cases over the loopback fake mount (MoveAxis below the floor, tracking past the meridian limit, motion starting outside a limit, limits off, disconnect during a guarded MoveAxis, a timed-out tracking stop that must not hold back the MoveAxis stop, a failed stop retried at the next poll, a sync below the floor that must not stop tracking, a MoveAxis Dec stop that must not restore the DeclinationRate offset into a tracking stop, an RA guide pulse under the guard that must leave the reported RA continuous), and the Sky-Watcher `[stress]` registration now runs with both limits set.
