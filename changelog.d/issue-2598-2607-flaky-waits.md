### Fixed (tests)

- **Celestron reaper-cancel case waits for the status poll to park** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #2598): "Celestron async - a reaper's cancel is not lost between a parked task's check and its block" required no waiter on the fake clock right after Connect, but since the NexStar telescope moved to `util::PolledState` (#2566) its status poll parks there as soon as Connect starts it. The case now waits for that one waiter before it installs its hook, so the race with the poll thread is gone.
- **Bisque FindHome supersede case moves the clock only after the second command claims** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #2607): "Bisque motion owner - a MoveAxis, a Park or an AbortSlew during FindHome leaves AtHome false" advanced the fake clock while the MoveAxis, Park or AbortSlew might not have claimed yet, so a loaded host could let FindHome reach its 60 s timeout first. The case now waits until the fake receives that command's first wire command, which each sends only after its claim.
