### Fixed (tests)

- **Celestron reaper-cancel case waits for the status poll to park** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #2598): "Celestron async - a reaper's cancel is not lost between a parked task's check and its block" required no waiter on the fake clock right after Connect, but since the NexStar telescope moved to `util::PolledState` (#2566) its status poll parks there as soon as Connect starts it. The case no longer counts waiters: its `set_before_block` hook holds only the first pulse body's thread, taken from the pulse spawn seam, so the poll thread can neither fail the count nor take the hold.
- **Bisque FindHome supersede case moves the clock only after the second command claims** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #2607): "Bisque motion owner - a MoveAxis, a Park or an AbortSlew during FindHome leaves AtHome false" advanced the fake clock while the MoveAxis, Park or AbortSlew might not have claimed yet, so a loaded host could let FindHome reach its 60 s timeout first. The case now waits until the fake receives that command's first wire command, which each sends only after its claim.
