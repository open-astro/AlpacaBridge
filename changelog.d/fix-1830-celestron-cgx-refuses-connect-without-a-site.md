### Fixed

- **Celestron CGX connects without a site**, Celestron CGX driver, issue #1830. `Connected=true` no longer throws when `SiteLatitude` or `SiteLongitude` is unset; the direct AUX link stores no site. While either coordinate is unset, `SiteLatitude`, `SiteLongitude`, the position getters (`RightAscension`, `Declination`, `Altitude`, `Azimuth`, `SideOfPier`), `DestinationSideOfPier`, `SiderealTime`, `FindHome`, `Park`, `SyncToCoordinates`, the slew methods, `Tracking=true` and `PulseGuide` throw `InvalidOperation` naming the missing setting. Writing both coordinates makes the mount usable without a reconnect. Covered by "connects without a site and refuses what needs one" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_celestron_cgx_telescope.cpp`; the rule is [decision 0019](docs/decisions/0019-connect-without-site.md).
