### Changed
- **Bisque, Celestron NexStar and SynScan telescopes: the Slewing and IsPulseGuiding windows are `util::HoldWindow`s** (AlpacaCore, issues #1832, #1833, #1836, decision 0022): each driver's raw `slew_force_until_` deadline becomes `slew_hold_`; the Celestron and SynScan per-axis pulse flag and end time (`pulse_guide_active_`/`pulse_guiding_active_` and `pulse_guide_end_time_`) become one `pulse_hold_` window per axis, and the Celestron pulse position holds and one-shot corrections (`pg_hold_*`, `pg_*_correction_*`) become `pg_hold_` and `pg_correction_`. Every arm, clear and read stays where it was, with the same 8 s, `kRefineStartForce` and pulse-plus-1 s holds. No behaviour change. The three decision 0022 rows are now `partial`: landing still waits on the slew window in the Bisque status poll and retarget guard, the Celestron SlewToCoordinates and Park waits and every SynScan landing wait (rule 3), and the rule 2 gaps each row names stay open.

### Added (tests)
- **Bisque, Celestron and SynScan hold-window cases** (issues #1832, #1833, #1836): Bisque's status poll and retarget guard ask TheSkyX nothing for 8 s after a slew command; a Celestron async GOTO reports landed before the 8 s hold ends; a Celestron Dec pulse holds RA at its pre-pulse value only once the RA pulse window has closed (no earlier case caught a broken cross-axis hold); AbortSlew inside the hold clears Slewing at once on Celestron and SynScan; a SynScan pulse keeps its axis until virtual time reaches its end; and a pin that a SynScan GOTO the handset reports done keeps Slewing until the 8 s hold ends.
