### Changed

- **Celestron NexStar position, status getters and `DeviceState` are served from a polled snapshot, and `TimeStamp` is when the poll measured** (Celestron, issue #1337): the 2 s position cache is gone; one status poll runs for the life of a connection (1 s, on the task clock) and RightAscension, Declination, Altitude, Azimuth, Slewing, SideOfPier, Tracking and `DeviceState` answer from its frame with no mount round trip, so a read stays under the ConformU fast-response target on a slow link (`test_celestron_telescope.cpp`, "Celestron snapshot - getters and DeviceState make no mount transaction at 100 ms per transaction"); a client write reads back at once and a poll that sampled before the write cannot undo it ("a setter's value is read back at once and a stale poll does not undo it"); a moved mount is served after the next poll, stamped when measured ("a position the mount moved is served after the next poll, stamped when measured"); past the staleness bound the link-loss policy of decision 0009 applies, and `DeviceState` is the empty list with no `TimeStamp` ("past the staleness bound DeviceState is empty with no TimeStamp"). A site written by a client while the poll reads the mount's location is no longer overwritten by the older value ("a site written during the poll's location read is not overwritten").
