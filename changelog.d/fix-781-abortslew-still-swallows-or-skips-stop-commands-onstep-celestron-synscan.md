### Fixed
- **AbortSlew reports a stop it could not send on OnStep, Celestron and SynScan** (issue #781; `onstep_telescope_driver.cpp`, `celestron_telescope_driver.cpp`, `synscan_telescope_driver.cpp`): OnStep swallowed every direction stop in an empty `catch` and then cleared `Slewing`, and Celestron and SynScan ran the cancel and both axis stops bare, so a throw from the cancel skipped both axis stops. Each driver now tries every stop on its own, and when any failed it throws `DriverException` ("AbortSlew stop failed: ...") after all were tried, leaving `Slewing` and the slew state unchanged because the mount may still be moving. The success path is unchanged. Covered by the "AbortSlew reports a stop it could not send" cases in `test_onstep_telescope.cpp`, `test_celestron_telescope.cpp` and `test_synscan_telescope.cpp`.
