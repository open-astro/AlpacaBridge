### Fixed
- **iOptron telescope: getters no longer wait behind the connect, a cache refill or a command** (AlpacaCore, issue #1829): every exchange with the mount now runs with the driver state lock free, under a per-command operation lock in the same order as the OnStep and Sky-Watcher slices (`transition_mutex_`, then `io_mutex_`, then `mutex_` as a leaf), through the shared `util::LockGate`. `Name`, `TargetRightAscension`, `TargetDeclination`, `SiteElevation`, `UTCDate`, `GuideRate`, `TrackingRates`, the capability and limit getters and the rate and refraction refusals answer while a connect (an auto-detect scan included), a `:GAC`/`:GEP`/`:GLS` refill, a GOTO, Park, FindHome, PulseGuide, MoveAxis, a Tracking or site write or the clock-sync body is on the wire; a getter whose cache is fresh answers under the state lock alone, one whose cache has lapsed still asks the mount and waits for the wire for that one read, and one that arrives during a connect answers `NotConnected` at once. The disconnect joins the slew and clock-sync bodies before it takes any driver lock, then clears the state and closes the port under the operation lock. The synchronous slews' completion wait now clears its in-progress flag under the state lock (it took no lock before). Park, FindHome, slews, syncs, pulse guides and the HAE29C workarounds behave as before.

### Changed (docs)
- **iOptron instructions and decision 0016** (docs, issue #1829): the lock order and the `StateLock`/`Wire` rule are written down in `ioptron.instructions.md`, and the iOptron telescope row of decision 0016 is `adopted`.
- **HAE29C ConformU report** (docs, issue #1829): `AlpacaCore/conformu/iOptron/HAE29C/Linux-arm64-usb.txt` is the ConformU 4.6.0-rc.1 (build 56430) run of 2026-11-04 on the converted driver (0 errors, 0 issues, 0 timing violations; the site and the values that follow from it are redacted), replacing the 4.5.0 run of 2026-08-25; it is the telescope half of the 4.6.0-rc.1 rig check, so `SUPPORTED-DRIVERS.md`, the testing instructions and `/conformu` no longer call the pin provisional.

### Added (tests)
- **iOptron getter timing** (issue #1829): `test_ioptron_getter_locks.cpp` times the identity, target, site-elevation, UTCDate, guide-rate and capability getters against a connect held on the wire and against a held position refill (each under 100 ms; replies held 1.5 s), checks that a cache-backed getter answers `NotConnected` at once during the connect and from its fresh cache while a target write is held on the wire, and a `[stress]` case in `test_ioptron_concurrency_stress.cpp` races those getters against commands and reconnects.
