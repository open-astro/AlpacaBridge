### Fixed
- **Sky-Watcher direct: MoveAxis and SyncToCoordinates no longer leave a running pulse's axis turning** (AlpacaCore, issue #630): both paths cancelled the pulse on the other axis without stopping it, so Dec kept moving at guide rate with IsPulseGuiding false, and a sync during a Dec pulse failed with "Motor not stopped". MoveAxis now cancels only its own axis's pulse (none for a no-op `MoveAxis(axis, 0)`), so a Dec pulse runs on and ends itself; sync stops both axes before writing the position and restarts tracking after. A refused MoveAxis or sync no longer cancels a pulse at all.
