- **Polled-state module**, Astroasis and QHY focuser drivers, issue #1597. The poll cadence (start to start, missed ticks skipped), the fault latch after three failed polls, the 30 s stale-to-lost rule and the served-frame rules now live once in `util::PolledState` (`AlpacaCore/include/alpacacore/util/polled_state.h`), and both focusers compose it in place of their identical hand-written copies; behaviour is unchanged (`test_astroasis_focuser.cpp` and the QHY focuser tests pass as before). The module's cadence, latch, loss and served rules are covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_polled_state.cpp`. [Decision 0010](docs/decisions/0010-snapshot-served-state.md) gains rule 7 and a per-row Symbol column in its Adoption table, and [decision 0016](docs/decisions/0016-getters-never-wait-on-device-io.md) rule 2 names the module's mutexes as leaves.
