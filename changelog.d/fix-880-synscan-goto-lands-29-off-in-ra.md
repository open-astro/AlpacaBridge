### Fixed

- **SynScan GOTO refines its landing** (SynScan, issue #880): after `SlewToCoordinates`, `SlewToTarget` and their async forms complete, the driver reads the handset's own position and re-issues the GOTO, aimed at the target corrected by the residual, while RA or Dec is more than 3 arcseconds off, for at most 3 extra passes (`kMaxLandingRefinePasses`), so a handset that lands 29.4 arcseconds off in RA now ends inside ConformU's 10 arcseconds. `Slewing` stays true until the last pass ends and `AbortSlew` stops a pass; a residual above 10 arcseconds after the last pass fails the slew with a `DriverException` (sync) or through `Slewing` (async) instead of reporting success. 16-bit V3 handsets and landings more than 600 arcseconds off are not refined. Covered by `test_synscan_goto_landing.cpp`. The async GOTO task waits through the cancellable `task_wait_for`, so Disconnect, MoveAxis, a new slew and driver destruction during an async GOTO return at once instead of waiting for the handset (`test_synscan_goto_landing.cpp`, four stop-path cases), and the slew settle time is applied after the last pass.
