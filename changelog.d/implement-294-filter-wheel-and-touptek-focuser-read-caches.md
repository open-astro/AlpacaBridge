### Changed

- **ToupTek AAF focuser answers IsMoving, Position and Temperature from one 100 ms status frame** (AlpacaCore, issue #294): `DeviceState` and the three getters used to call the SDK once per property; they now share one refill of three `aaf_get` calls, `move()` and `halt()` drop the frame, MaxStep is read once at connect, and three failed refills latch a "ToupTek AAF communications compromised" `DriverException` that clears on the next good read (tests `ToupTek AAF Focuser Driver - DeviceState is one status read, served from the TTL cache`, `ToupTek AAF Focuser Driver - A dead link refuses reads instead of serving the cache`). The ioptron, Player One, ToupTek and ZWO filter wheels are unchanged: their `DeviceState` is the one `Position` read, which `ToupTek AFW - DeviceState is one device read and static members cost none` pins.
