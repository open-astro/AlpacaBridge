### Fixed
- **SynScan: a Dec-only GOTO refinement pass no longer moves the RA aim** (SynScan, issue #1030): the handset's precise RA readback ramps down about 15 arcseconds (one second of sidereal motion) and jumps back once a second, so an RA read that is inside the 3 arcsecond landing tolerance can be followed by passes, driven by Dec, that add the RA residual to the aim and drag RA away. The RA aim is now kept when the RA residual is inside the tolerance, while the Dec aim is corrected as before (`refine_goto_landing_locked`). Both tolerances stay 3 arcseconds. Covered by `a Dec-only refinement pass leaves the RA aim alone (#1030)` in `test_synscan_goto_landing.cpp`. The phase-lock RA correction is not part of this change.
