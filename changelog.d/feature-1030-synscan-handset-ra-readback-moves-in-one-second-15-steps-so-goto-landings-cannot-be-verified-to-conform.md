### Fixed
- **SynScan: GOTO landing refinement no longer chases the handset's one-second RA readback steps** (SynScan, issue #1030): the handset's precise RA readback ramps down about 15 arcseconds (194 counts, one second of sidereal motion) and jumps back once a second, so every RA read is 0-15 arcseconds low depending on phase. The 3 arcsecond landing tolerance sat below that, so refinement passes chased the ramp and each extra GOTO disturbed Dec by about 10 arcseconds. An RA residual up to 16 arcseconds (one quantum plus 1 arcsecond of readback noise, `kLandingRaToleranceArcsec`) now counts as landed; Dec keeps its 3 arcsecond tolerance. Covered by three cases in `test_synscan_goto_landing.cpp` (RA inside the quantum issues no extra GOTO, RA just outside and Dec over tolerance are still refined). The phase-lock RA correction is not part of this change.
