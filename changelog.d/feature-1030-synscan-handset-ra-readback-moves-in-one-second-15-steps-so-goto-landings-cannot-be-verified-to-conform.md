### Fixed
- **SynScan: a Dec-only GOTO refinement pass no longer moves the RA aim** (SynScan, issue #1030): the handset's precise RA readback ramps down about 15 arcseconds (one second of sidereal motion) and jumps back once a second, so an RA read that is inside the 3 arcsecond landing tolerance can be followed by re-GOTO rounds, driven by Dec, that add the RA residual to the aim and drag RA away. In a re-GOTO landing round the RA aim is now kept when the RA residual is inside the tolerance, while the Dec aim is corrected as before (`refine_goto_landing_locked`); this matters for mounts not known to be equatorial, whose every landing round is a re-GOTO, since an equatorial mount finishes its landing with timed moves after at most one re-GOTO. Both tolerances stay 3 arcseconds. Covered by `a Dec-only refinement pass leaves the RA aim alone (#1030)` in `test_synscan_goto_landing.cpp`, on an Alt-Az mount.
