### Changed

- **SynScan: a second PulseGuide or a MoveAxis on the axis of a running pulse takes the axis over at once** (AlpacaCore, issue #2263): it no longer cancels and waits for the older pulse first (decision 0006 rule 3), so the handset gets no stop between the two motions and the older pulse sends that axis nothing more; the newer command's own end stops the axis or restores tracking. A PulseGuide whose rate command fails leaves the older pulse running to its own end. A PulseGuide sent while the older RA pulse retries a failed tracking restore is now accepted and takes the axis over, where it used to be refused with `InvalidOperation`. A pulse on the other axis still runs on, and `MoveAxis(axis, 0)`, AbortSlew, the slews, Park and disconnect stop a pulse as before. The driver-wide motion counter is replaced by the per-axis motion owner (`util::MotionOwner`).
