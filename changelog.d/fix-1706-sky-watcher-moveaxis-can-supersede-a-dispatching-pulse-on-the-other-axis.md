### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher MoveAxis no longer drops a PulseGuide on the other axis**, Sky-Watcher driver, issue #1706. A MoveAxis on one axis that landed while a PulseGuide on the other axis was stop-waiting its own axis made the pulse throw "Motion superseded before dispatch" and never start. A pulse dispatch now follows any command that starts or stops only the other axis, MoveAxis and its stop included; commands that own both axes (goto, Park, sync, abort, home, reconnect) still supersede it. Covered by `SkyWatcher async - MoveAxis(RA, rate) during a Dec pulse's dispatch stop-wait does not drop the pulse (#1706)` and `SkyWatcher async - MoveAxis(RA, 0) during a Dec pulse's dispatch stop-wait does not drop the pulse (#1706)` in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_skywatcher_async.cpp`. The same holds for a Tracking=false, RightAscensionRate or DeclinationRate write that stops one axis, for the stops of a sub-floor rate's duty bursts and for the RA restart of the post-slew tracking rate check (`SkyWatcher async - an RA-only stop during a Dec pulse's dispatch stop-wait does not drop the pulse (#1706)`, `SkyWatcher async - a Dec offset stop during an RA pulse's dispatch stop-wait does not drop the pulse (#1706)`, `SkyWatcher async - an RA duty stop during a Dec pulse's dispatch stop-wait does not drop the pulse (#1706)`, `SkyWatcher async - the post-slew rate check's RA restart during a Dec pulse's dispatch stop-wait does not drop the pulse (#1706)`). An East/West pulse that stops RA before it starts (tracking off, or a guide rate near sidereal while tracking) was dropped the same way by a Dec MoveAxis, and while tracking it left RA stopped (`SkyWatcher async - MoveAxis(Dec, rate) during an RA pulse's dispatch stop-wait does not drop the pulse (#1706)`). A pulse's stop-wait is the only one that follows: an older MoveAxis stop-wait on the pulse's own axis stays superseded by the pulse and the slot is cleared on every exit (`SkyWatcher async - a Dec pulse dispatch supersedes an older MoveAxis(Dec) stop-wait (#1706)`). Back-to-back Dec pulses keep this too (`SkyWatcher async - MoveAxis(RA, rate) during a back-to-back Dec pulse's stop-wait does not drop it (#1706)`).
