### Fixed

- **Bisque: Name, Connected, RightAscension and the other getters no longer wait behind a TheSkyX exchange** (Bisque, issue #1438): the whole Bisque telescope driver now exchanges with TheSkyX with the driver state lock free, under the protocol wrapper's per-link lock: connect and handshake (under a transition lock no getter takes), FindHome, Park and its polls, slews and their completion polls, sync, `SetTracking`, MoveAxis and the position read at the end of sync, slew complete, FindHome and Park. The read takes its snapshot token under the state lock, samples without it and publishes under it, so a read right after sync, slew complete, FindHome or Park still sees the new position. Covered by "Bisque snapshot - Name, Connected and RightAscension answer while FindHome runs in TheSkyX", "Bisque snapshot - RightAscension answers while the read at the end of SyncToCoordinates is slow" and "Bisque snapshot - Name, Connected and Tracking answer while a SetTracking exchange is slow" in `test_bisque_telescope_snapshot.cpp`.
