### Fixed
- **QHY CFW3 filter wheel: a failed move now makes `Position` raise** (QHY, issue #1475; `qhy_cfw3_filterwheel_driver.cpp`): a goto that times out or fails on the wire was only logged, and the next `Position` did a live `NOW` read and returned a slot. The failure is now kept and `Position` raises it as a `DriverException` with the failure text ("QHYCFW3 move to slot 4 failed: ..."), with no wire read, until the next `Position` PUT or a reconnect clears it (`A failed move makes Position raise until the next Position PUT` asserts the text, the code, that a second read raises again without a `NOW`, and that the next PUT settles; `A reconnect clears a failed move`, which fails if the `move_failure_.reset()` in `teardown_locked` is removed). A new shared rule in `ascom-contract.instructions.md` (summarised in `AGENTS.md`) says the same for every async operation body. Fake wheel only, ConformU not re-run.
