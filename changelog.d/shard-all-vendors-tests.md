### Changed (tooling)
- **The all-vendors CI tests run on three runners** (CI, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `run_all_tests.sh`): `build-vendors-shard` builds the instrumented all-vendors tree on each of three runners and runs every third test (`CTEST_SHARD=<k>/3`, `ctest -I k,,3`). `build-vendors` keeps the name the required check and the review bot wait for, passes only when all three shards passed, and merges their coverage JSON into the same report. One runner took about 11.5 min of ctest (683 s for 5,267 test-seconds); the estimate is under 4 min per shard, which brings the review bot's wait from about 15.5 min to about 7. `run_all_tests.sh` without `CTEST_SHARD` runs every test, as before.
- **`OnStep telescope - Park and FindHome race the getters and a reconnect` runs 20 passes, not 60** (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_onstep_concurrency_stress.cpp`): each pass costs about 4.4 s of TCP read slices after blind commands, so the case took 265 s, the slowest in the suite and the floor of a sharded run. It now takes about 90 s and keeps four reconnects.
