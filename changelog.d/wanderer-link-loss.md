### Fixed

- **A WandererCover, SFW filter wheel or WandererBox whose USB cable is pulled reads Connected false at once, and one that stays silent drops it after 30 s of fault**, WandererAstro cover, filter wheel and box drivers, issue #1988, decision 0009 rules 2 to 5. A hangup on the status reader used to count only as silence: `Connected` stayed true, the dead port stayed claimed, the 10 s silence latch then threw `DriverException` "communications compromised" for ever, and a command written to the pulled adapter threw `DriverException` "Serial write failed". Now a hangup (`EIO`, `ENXIO` or `ENODEV`) on a read or a command write loses the link at once, and so does a silence fault that has stood for 30 s (`util::kLinkStalenessBound` on the driver's task clock, so 40 s after the last frame; `util::StreamLinkHealth` gains `fault_stale()`). The wrapper clears its cached status and firmware date, stops its reader and releases the port; `Connected` reads false, every operational member throws `NotConnected`, `DeviceState` is empty, and `get_link_fault()` gives the loss reason until the next Connect or Disconnect. Names, focus offsets and other static metadata answer as they do while disconnected. Nothing reopens the port until the client's next Connect, which reopens it without a Disconnect first (the wheel homes on it, as on every Connect). A hangup while the cover or wheel Connect waits for its first frame now fails that Connect at once with `NotConnected` instead of waiting out the timeout, and keeps no reason. A fault younger than 30 s keeps `Connected` true as before. No loss time is stamped (no #521 motion-on-relink consumer). Calls on a pulled device already failed before this change, so nothing that worked stops working. Tests: five `[link-lost]` cases per driver in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_wandererastro_covercalibrator.cpp`, `test_wandererastro_filterwheel.cpp` and `test_wandererastro_box_switch.cpp`, a mid-wait hangup case for the cover and the wheel, three `fault_stale()` cases in the new `AlpacaCore/tests/test_stream_link_health.cpp`; the severed-port tail of the box case "Silent link faults reads, frames restore them (issue #237)" moved to the new hangup case. Pty fake evidence only; not checked on the rig.
- **A WandererRotator Mini whose USB cable is pulled reads Connected false at once**, WandererAstro rotator driver, issue #1989, decision 0009 rules 2 to 5. A command written to a pulled adapter threw `DriverException` "Serial write failed" with `Connected` still true, and a pull during a move read as silence until the move budget ran out and then as the "check DC power" fault. Now `EIO`, `ENXIO` or `ENODEV` on a command write or a move monitor read loses the link at once: `Connected` false, operations `NotConnected`, `DeviceState` empty, the port released, the move ended without the missing-report fault, and `get_link_fault()` (new on this driver) gives the reason until the next Connect or Disconnect. There is no silence latch or staleness bound, since the rotator says nothing between moves, so an idle pull is found by the next command. A Connect over a lost link reopens without a Disconnect. Tests: five `[link-lost]` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_wandererastro_rotator.cpp`. Pty fake evidence only; not checked on the rig.

### Changed

- **The WandererRotator auto-detect logs every raw serial node it skips, with the reason**, WandererAstro rotator, issue #2212 (part of #2211). The raw `/dev/ttyUSB0` to `ttyUSB9` pass dropped a node without a word when its USB descriptor could not be read or was not a CH340/CH341 adapter. Each such node now gets one DEBUG line, `WandererRotator scan skipped /dev/ttyUSBn: USB descriptor unreadable` or `... not a CH340/CH341 device (vendor <id>, product "<product>")`, so a log shows whether a second node existed and why it was not probed. Missing nodes and nodes already probed through `/dev/serial/by-id` stay quiet, and the set and order of probed ports is unchanged. Test: `WandererRotator auto-detect names why it skips a raw node (#2212)`.
