### Changed

- **Celestron telescope serves its position and status through `util::PolledState`**, Celestron driver, issue #2493. The driver no longer holds its own snapshot, poll slot, fault latch or loss flag; the fault latch (three failed status polls) and the 30 s loss rule (decision 0009) come from the module, and a pulled serial cable still drops `Connected` at once through `PolledState::lose_link()`. A control-path read at the end of a slew, Park or FindHome writes through the frame and no longer renews its measured time (`Celestron PolledState - a sync and a slew end read back the written state before the next poll`). A location read that times out in the poll now counts as a failed poll (`Celestron PolledState - a site read that times out fails the tick and counts toward the loss`); a FindHome completion check that gets no answer stops the mount on its third failure and also counts as a failed poll, so it never clears a latched fault (`Celestron PolledState - a FindHome tick that reaches nothing keeps a latched fault`, `Celestron FindHome - invalid home-status replies persist through Slewing and AtHome`). A latched fault's text is now the module's fixed text, without the handset's reply. Decision 0010 now matches the code: control-path reads write through, and the roster row reads `PolledState`.
