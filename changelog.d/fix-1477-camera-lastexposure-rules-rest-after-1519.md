### Fixed

- **Camera `LastExposureDuration` and `LastExposureStartTime`**, issue #1477 (ZWO, QHY, SVBONY, Player One and the iOptron camera that reuses it, GPhoto, iOptron iGuider; ToupTek and Altair are not changed here). Before any successful image both now raise `InvalidOperation` (0x40B), where ICameraV4 names it, instead of `ValueNotSet` (test `Contract sweep tier 2 - <camera> - LastExposure before any image is InvalidOperation` for each camera, and the `LastExposure values follow successful images only` case in each driver test). A failed exposure no longer publishes them: ZWO after the retries fail, QHY when the frame is not delivered, SVBONY when the frame read fails, GPhoto when the capture throws, iGuider when the stream stalls. They were set when the exposure started, so a failed first exposure reported its requested duration and start time. They are committed only when the image is acquired (`util::LastExposure`), a later failure keeps an earlier success, and only a new success replaces it. SVBONY stamps the start time in the worker after the deferred `FrameSpeedMode` write, which takes about a second on hardware, not in `StartExposure` (test `LastExposureStartTime is stamped after the deferred FrameSpeedMode write`). GPhoto reports the shutter speed actually set, so a 0.1 s request on a camera whose nearest choice is `1/200` reports 0.005 s (test `LastExposureDuration is the shutter speed actually set`); a bulb exposure reports the requested duration the driver holds (test `LastExposureDuration of a bulb exposure is the held duration`), and `PercentCompleted` keeps the requested duration. The ToupTek and Altair rows follow the open ToupTek rewrite. While disconnected the two getters raise `NotConnected` (the same sweep case checks it before connecting). A GPhoto `AbortExposure` that lands while the frame decodes discards the image and publishes nothing (test `an abort during the decode publishes no LastExposure values`).
