### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher link-loss verdict closing a new session**, Sky-Watcher driver, issue #1707. A status poll that judged the old link lost (a stale fault, or the serial node gone) could latch "lost", stamp the outage or close the session a relink had just opened, and could report the new session as not connected. The stale verdict is re-checked under the health lock before it latches, the close only runs while that latch still stands, the serial node-gone verdict is dropped when a relink has replaced its session, and `link_alive()` then judges the current session. Covered by "SkyWatcher link loss - a stale verdict does not latch a session relinked after it", "SkyWatcher link loss - a latched loss does not close a session relinked before the close" and "SkyWatcher link loss - serial: a node-gone verdict does not stamp or close a session relinked after it" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_skywatcher_link_loss.cpp`.
