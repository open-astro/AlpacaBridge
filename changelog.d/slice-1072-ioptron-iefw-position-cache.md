### Changed

- **iOptron iEFW Position cache and lock order**, iOptron iEFW filter wheel, issue #1072. `Position` is served from a 100 ms status cache, so two reads inside the window cost one `:WP#` (`two Position reads inside the TTL cost one :WP#`), and a read after `set_position` goes to the wheel (`a Position read after set_position goes to the wire`). `-1` while the wheel moves passes through and is never held (`Position reads -1 while moving and the move end is seen at once`). Three failed reads in a row latch a "communications compromised" error that a good read clears, and a reconnect clears the count (`failed Position reads are not served and latch a link fault`, `a reconnect clears the Position link-health count`). `get_names` no longer waits behind a `:WP#` refill or the connect handshake (`get_names does not wait behind a slow Position refill`, `get_names does not wait behind a held connect`), and concurrent `set_position` calls leave `Position` on the wheel's slot (`concurrent set_position calls leave Position on the wheel slot`). A refused move drops the cached frame (`a refused move invalidates the cached Position`), and a `FocusOffsets` write made during a connect is kept instead of replaced by the wheel's stored offsets (`a FocusOffsets write during connect is kept`). Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_ioptron_filterwheel.cpp`.
