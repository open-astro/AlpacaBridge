### Changed

- **QHY camera: the exposure runs in a `util::CaptureSession`, and no thread is detached any more**, issue #1736. The session owns CameraState, ImageReady, the image, PercentCompleted, LastExposure* and the watchdog; the driver keeps the SDK sequence (settings on the StartExposure thread, then arm, settle and download on one body thread) and extends the watchdog for a slow readout mode with `CaptureControl::extend_deadline()`. AbortExposure and a disconnect call `cancel_exposure` first, then join the body with no time bound (decision 0020 rule 5): when the cancel does not interrupt `GetQHYCCDSingleFrame`, they wait for that one call instead of detaching the thread after 2 s. Client-visible: a second StartExposure while one exposes is `InvalidOperation` (it used to supersede the running exposure), and an AbortExposure no longer clears a kept exposure failure, which the next StartExposure or a reconnect clears (decision 0020 rule 3). ConformU not re-run; a run on a real QHY camera is a follow-up.
