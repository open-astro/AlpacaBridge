### Fixed
- **ZWO AM Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore, issue #2408): the stop sent when a GOTO runs past its 15 min bound is now checked and sent again, up to three stops, the way AbortSlew's is. A link lost during a Park or a FindHome is now stopped on the next connect, before anything else is written. A redundant `Connected=true` no longer writes the site and the time to the mount while the driver moves it.

### Breaking changes
- **ZWO AM Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore, issue #2408): an `e3` (mount busy) reply to a GOTO is now an error that Slewing raises. The driver no longer stops the mount and sends the GOTO again. `SyncToCoordinates` and `SyncToTarget` are refused with InvalidValue when they would move the reported position by more than 5 degrees. **After upgrading:** retry a GOTO refused as busy once the mount is idle, and slew near the target before syncing, or use a plate solve close to the current position.
