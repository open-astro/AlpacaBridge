### Added (tests)

- **Switch member tests over the existing fakes**, Gemini PDH, WandererAstro box and ToupTek thermal switch drivers, issue #1762. Connected cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_gemini_pdh_switch.cpp`, `test_wandererastro_box_switch.cpp` and `test_touptek_thermal_switch.cpp` call `GetSwitchDescription`, `SetSwitchName`, `StateChangeComplete`, `CanWrite`, `CanAsync`, `MinSwitchValue`, `MaxSwitchValue` and `SwitchStep` on in-range ids, and all of them except `MaxSwitchValue` and `SwitchStep` on out-of-range ids. For Gemini PDH and WandererAstro they also call `SetAsync` and `SetAsyncValue` and check that `SetSwitch` and `SetSwitchValue` on a read-only id raise `NotImplemented` and leave the value unchanged. The ToupTek thermal driver has no read-only id and its cases do not call `SetAsync` or `SetAsyncValue`. Tests only; no driver changes.
