### Added
- **remotealpaca: Telescope of an upstream Alpaca server** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, web UI, issue #2710, slice 5 of #1753): the "Remote Alpaca" vendor now also serves the Telescope device of another Alpaca server as a device of this server, with the same four settings (upstream host, port, device number, poll period) and the same Connect, snapshot and link rules as the other proxied devices. The position, `Slewing`, `Tracking`, `AtPark`, `AtHome`, `SideOfPier`, `IsPulseGuiding`, `UTCDate` and `SiderealTime` answer from the poll's snapshot (twelve reads per poll; the clocks advance by the snapshot's age); the capabilities, axis rates, site, optics, rates and targets are read once at Connect and written through by this server's writes. The asynchronous slews, Park, FindHome, MoveAxis, PulseGuide, the syncs and every write are forwarded in caller order, and a slew reads `Slewing` true at once. `AbortSlew` and `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` are forwarded even while the link is faulted. A `MoveAxis` this server sent is stopped after 60 s with no new `MoveAxis` (a verified stop; `Slewing` raises the outcome), and a Connect after a link lost while this server's slew or `MoveAxis` ran stops a mount that still slews before any other command. The synchronous slews are deprecated in ITelescopeV4 and answer `NotImplemented` (`CanSlew` and `CanSlewAltAz` read false); an upstream older than ITelescopeV3 is refused at Connect. Built under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_REMOTEALPACA`; fake evidence only, ConformU against the simulators is a rig step on a bare mount.
