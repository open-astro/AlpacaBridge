### Added

- **OnStep: tracking rates, rate offsets, the mount's guide rate and SyncToAltAz** (OnStep driver, issue #2214): on OnStepX, `TrackingRates` are sidereal, lunar, solar and King, and `TrackingRate` reads the rate letter of the polled `:GU#` status (`(` lunar, `O` solar, `k` King, none sidereal) and sets with `:TQ#`/`:TL#`/`:TS#`/`:TK#`. The firmware writes those letters only while tracking compensation is off, so with it on the driver serves the last rate it knew (its own writes, the last letter, and `:GT#`, read at connect and on the slow poll tick while the mount tracks). `RightAscensionRate` and `DeclinationRate` are settable (`CanSet*` true, `:SXTR`/`:SXTD`, x 15 and x 0.9972695663 for the firmware's arcseconds per sidereal second); a request beyond the firmware's silent +/-1800 clamp is `InvalidValue`, reads are 0 and writes `InvalidOperation` while `TrackingRate` is not sidereal, a refusal throws with the `:GE#` reason, and the offsets are read again at connect, after a write and when the polled frame's rate, compensation, tracking, parked or at-home flags change (OnStepX zeroes them on a home reset). `GuideRate` is the mount's pulse-guide rate, `:GX90#` times 15.0410686 arcseconds per second, read at connect and when the pulse-guide digit of `:GU#` changes, on OnStep 4.x too; `CanSetGuideRates` stays false because `:R0#`-`:R2#` write the controller's non-volatile memory. `SyncToAltAz` is supported (`CanSyncAltAz` true): the Alt/Az slew's conversion and validation order, then the `SyncToCoordinates` path, also while tracking. Turning Tracking on (the `Tracking` write and the driver's restore after a GOTO) sends `:Te#` on OnStepX, because `:ST60.164275#` sets the rate back to sidereal and would cancel a lunar, solar or King selection. With no `:GU#` answer since the connect, `TrackingRate` and the rate offsets throw `DriverException` like `Tracking`; changing `TrackingRate` while an offset is set zeroes both offsets first (`:SXTR,0.000000#`/`:SXTD,0.000000#`), because the mount keeps applying them on a non-sidereal rate. OnStep 4.x keeps sidereal only, no rate offsets and `:ST60.164275#`. Getters read driver state only.

### Fixed

- **OnStep: AlignmentMode refuses like the other frame getters** (OnStep driver, issue #2214): connected, it now throws `DriverException` with no `:GU#` status since the connect and throws the fault on a latched fault, and throws it for a status without a mount-type letter (no supported firmware sends one), instead of answering GermanPolar or the last letter (decisions 0010 rule 8, 0009). Disconnected still answers GermanPolar.

### Added (tests)

- **OnStep rate cases** (issue #2214): `[rates]` cases in `test_onstep_telescope_snapshot.cpp` against the scripted OnStepX: a frame letter per rate, the compensation frame with and without `:GT#`, the `TrackingRate` write, its write-through, offset read-back and offset zeroing, the no-status throw, a `t` compensation frame, Tracking on keeping the rate (`:Te#`), OnStep 4.x staying sidereal with `:ST60.164275#`, offset unit conversion both ways, the range and sidereal refusals, the `:GE#` reason, the offsets read again on a flag change, the guide rate following `:GX90#` and the `:GU#` digit, and `SyncToAltAz` (sync while tracking, validation order). The `:ST60` counts in the existing tracking-restore cases are `:Te#`.
