### Fixed
- **A wrong verb on a log endpoint now answers HTTP 405 with `Allow`** (AlpacaHTTP router, issue #2484): `loglevel`, `logs`, `logfiles` and `logfiles/<name>` (and their unversioned aliases) answered a verb they do not accept with HTTP 200 and an Alpaca error. They now answer 405 like the other AlpacaBridge-only management paths, with `Allow: GET, POST, PUT` for `loglevel`, `GET` for `logs` and `GET, DELETE` for both `logfiles` forms. A cross-origin `PUT /management/v1/logfiles` is now 405 too, as `docs/wifi-api.md` says. Every 405 from an AlpacaBridge-only path now carries `InvalidOperation` (0x40B): `configuredevice`, `removedevice`, `updatedevice`, `shutdown`, `restart` and `synctime` sent `InvalidValue` (0x401). Covered by `test_routing`.
