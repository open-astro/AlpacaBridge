### Added
- **remotealpaca: ObservingConditions of an upstream Alpaca server** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, web UI, issue #1753): the new vendor "Remote Alpaca" serves the ObservingConditions device of another Alpaca server (the ASCOM Alpaca Simulators first) as a device of this server, with this server's own device number and `UniqueID`. The operator types the upstream host, port and device number, and a poll period of 1 to 60 seconds (2 by default). Getters answer from a snapshot of the upstream that the poll keeps (decision 0010) and never wait on the upstream; once connected, `Name` is "Remote Alpaca" and the upstream's Name without its " (...)" qualifiers or anything after a comma (decision 0024), and `Description` is the upstream's, with the upstream's full Name added when the two differ; the upstream's `ErrorNumber` and `ErrorMessage` pass through unchanged with its host in front; Connect and Disconnect are forwarded, and a sensor the upstream lacks is `NotImplemented`. Three failed polls in a row latch a link fault and the link is lost at `max(30 s, 3 poll periods)`; a refused connection loses it at once; only the client's Connect relinks (decision 0009). An upstream that is this server is refused when it is configured and again at every Connect (decision 0023 rule 4). Built under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_REMOTEALPACA`; fake evidence only, ConformU against the simulators is a rig step.

### Changed
- **util::HttpClient: a refused connection has its own type** (AlpacaCore, issue #1753): the libcurl client throws `util::HttpConnectRefused` (still a `DriverException`) when the connect is refused (`ECONNREFUSED`), so a caller can take it as a lost link; an unreachable host, a timeout and a dropped connection are unchanged. `RemoteAlpacaClient` maps it to `RemoteConnectRefused`. WeeWX is unchanged: it still counts every failed fetch toward its latch.
