### Fixed

- **Celestron NexStar: Name, Connected, RightAscension and the other getters no longer wait behind a mount exchange** (Celestron, issue #1437): the whole NexStar driver now exchanges with the mount under the protocol wrapper's per-link lock with the driver state lock free: connect and disconnect (under a transition lock no getter takes), the FindHome dispatch and completion check, slew, park, sync, abort, MoveAxis, pulse guide, `set_tracking`, `set_guide_rate`, site and time writes and `CommandString`. A getter returns at once instead of waiting one exchange timeout. The safety stop still goes out on the third failed FindHome check. Covered by the `[iolock]` cases in `test_celestron_telescope.cpp`.
