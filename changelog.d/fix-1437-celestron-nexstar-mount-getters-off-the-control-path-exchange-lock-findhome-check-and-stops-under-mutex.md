### Fixed

- **Celestron NexStar: Name, Connected, RightAscension and the other getters no longer wait behind a mount exchange** (Celestron, issue #1437): the whole NexStar driver now exchanges with the mount under the protocol wrapper's per-link lock with the driver state lock free: connect and disconnect (under a transition lock no getter takes), the FindHome dispatch and completion check, slew, park, sync, abort, MoveAxis, pulse guide, `set_tracking`, `set_guide_rate`, site and time writes and `CommandString`. A getter returns at once instead of waiting one exchange timeout. The safety stop still goes out on the third failed FindHome check, and a failure stop (FindHome poll, Slewing poll) is skipped when a new motion command has taken the mount first, so it cannot cut that motion short ("the failure stops are skipped when a new goto took the mount first"). A connect that fails after the port opened closes it again and leaves Connected false ("a failed time write closes the link and leaves Connected false"). Covered by the `[iolock]` cases in `test_celestron_telescope.cpp`.
