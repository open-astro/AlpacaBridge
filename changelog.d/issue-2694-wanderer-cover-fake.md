- **WandererAstro WandererCover in the fake ConformU run**, WandererAstro cover calibrator, issue #2694 (part of #2078). Covered by the new `FakeSerialStreamer` cover case in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_wandererastro_covercalibrator.cpp`. The cover row of `scripts/conformu_fake_runs.tsv` is `run`, with its report at `AlpacaCore/conformu/WandererAstro/Fake/Fake-covercalibrator-fake_serial_streamer-Linux-arm64.txt`, and the `ConformU fake` workflow now names the WandererAstro sources and `fake_serial_streamer.h` in its paths, so a WandererAstro change runs the vendor's rows.
