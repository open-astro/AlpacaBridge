### Fixed

- **`SetCCDTemperature` refuses NaN and infinity first on every cooled camera**, ZWO, QHY, Player One (iCAM) and ToupTek (Altair) drivers; SVBONY already did, issue #2487. A setpoint that is NaN, +infinity or -infinity now answers `InvalidValue` before the connection check, so a disconnected camera no longer answers `NotConnected` for it (ZWO, Player One, ToupTek) and QHY no longer lets a NaN pass its bounds; a finite value while disconnected still answers `NotConnected`. The ranges the SDKs report stay checked after the connection check; no range is invented. The check is `util::check_ccd_setpoint` in `camera_frame_request.h` (unit cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_camera_frame_request.cpp`); the sweep probes `set_set_ccd_temperature(NaN)`, `(+inf)` and `(-inf)` in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_contract_sweep.cpp` run them on every camera entry. The order is in the ASCOM contract topic file, "Camera argument order".
