### Changed

- **ZWO camera and dew heater switch share one open through the shared open table**, ZWO camera driver and dew heater switch, issue #1667 (part 1 of 2; the replug fix follows). The camera SDK wrapper no longer counts opens per id: `vendor::zwo::SharedOpenTable`, which the EAF has used since issue #1500, hands each holder a link, runs every per-device call under it, and does `ASIOpenCamera` and `ASIInitCamera` together on the first open. A holder's close never closes a handle another holder or a later Connect opened (test "a stale close does not close another holder's open"), an open of a held id whose serial changed closes the old handle and reopens ("an open of a held id whose serial changed closes it and reopens"), and the last holder closes ("one real open, closed by the last holder"). A close that fails no longer leaves the id marked open, so the next open runs `ASIInitCamera` again ("a close that throws still lets the next open initialise the camera", "A close that throws still drops the entry"). The image download runs without the shared lock, and a loss during it closes the handle only when the transfer returns (the `call_unserialised` cases in `test_zwo_shared_open_table.cpp`). A Connect while a lost handle is still closing under a download answers InvalidOperation ("A loss closes the id only after the unserialised call returns"). No change to `Connected` or to error mapping yet.
