### Fixed

- **A ZWO camera unplugged and plugged back in works again after one Connect**, ZWO camera driver, issue #1667 (the dew heater half was not reproduced on the rig and stays open). `ASI_ERROR_CAMERA_REMOVED`, `ASI_ERROR_CAMERA_CLOSED` or `ASI_ERROR_INVALID_ID` on a camera call used to leave `Connected` true: an exposure spent its retries and failed with error 1280, and a temperature read could not see the removal. One such error, from any camera call (a status or temperature read included), now makes `Connected` read false, `DeviceState` empty and every operational member throw `NotConnected` with "ZWO camera <serial> was removed from USB; Connect again when it is back", which `get_link_fault()` also reports until the next Connect or Disconnect; the loss is logged once at WARN with the serial, and the exposure retry stops at the first error without re-triggering (tests "a pulled cable during an exposure loses the link at once", "a removal error on a temperature read loses the link"). A Disconnect shows `Connected` false at once even while a retry is stuck in an SDK call ("Disconnect reads false while a retry burst is stuck in the SDK"), and a Disconnect after a loss completes. The next Connect opens the camera by the serial read at the first connect, kept in memory, not in the configuration, whatever SDK id it has now, and two cameras whose ids swapped while disconnected are told apart ("Disconnect after a loss completes and Connect reopens by serial on the new id", "two cameras swapped while disconnected are told apart by serial"). With the dew heater switch on the same camera, the camera's loss refuses the switch's next read and write with `NotConnected` before the SDK, a stale close from the switch never closes the handle the camera reopened, and a loss the switch meets first stops the camera's next call the same way ("the switch gets NotConnected before the SDK is touched", "a stale close from the switch does not close the new handle", "the camera gets NotConnected before the SDK is touched"); the switch itself still latches no loss. Fake SDK evidence only: the wrapper's removal-code mapping is not yet checked on the rig.
- **A Disconnect during a ZWO camera pulse guide turns the guide output off at once**, ZWO camera driver, issue #1667. The turn-off ran on a detached thread that slept out the pulse, so a Disconnect left the ST4 output on until then and the thread outlived the driver. It now runs in an `AsyncOperation` slot: Disconnect and destroying the driver send the turn-off before the close and leave no timer ("Disconnect mid-pulse turns the guide off at once", "destroying the driver mid-pulse turns the guide off and leaves no timer"); a reconnect after a replug leaves no timer from the old open ("a pulse from before a replug never turns off the guide on the new handle"); a pulse on another axis still ends each pulse at its own time ("a pulse on another axis does not cut the running pulse short").

### Changed

- **ZWO camera and dew heater switch share one open through the shared open table**, ZWO camera driver and dew heater switch, issue #1667. The camera SDK wrapper no longer counts opens per id: `vendor::zwo::SharedOpenTable`, which the EAF has used since issue #1500, hands each holder a link, runs every per-device call under it, and does `ASIOpenCamera` and `ASIInitCamera` together on the first open. A holder's close never closes a handle another holder or a later Connect opened (test "a stale close does not close another holder's open"), an open of a held id whose serial changed closes the old handle and reopens ("an open of a held id whose serial changed closes it and reopens"), and the last holder closes ("one real open, closed by the last holder"). A close that fails no longer leaves the id marked open, so the next open runs `ASIInitCamera` again ("a close that throws still lets the next open initialise the camera", "A close that throws still drops the entry"). The image download runs without the shared lock, and a loss during it closes the handle only when the transfer returns (the `call_unserialised` cases in `test_zwo_shared_open_table.cpp`). A Connect while a lost handle is still closing under a download answers InvalidOperation ("A loss closes the id only after the unserialised call returns").
