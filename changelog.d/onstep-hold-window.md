### Changed

- **OnStep telescope: the slew and pulse-guide deadlines are `util::HoldWindow`s** (AlpacaCore, issue #1835, decision 0022; `onstep_telescope_driver.cpp`): the 2 s slew grace and the pulse-guide end time were raw `steady_clock` time points. The slew window is armed before the `:MS#` goes out and cleared on a refused or failed `:MS#`, AbortSlew, connect, disconnect and the GOTO's end ("Slewing is true as soon as SlewToCoordinatesAsync returns", "AbortSlew inside the hold clears Slewing at once", "a reconnect inside the hold leaves Slewing false"). A pulse's window is armed before its `:Mg` command and runs on the task clock ("a pulse keeps its axis while virtual time has not reached its end"). Hold lengths are unchanged.

### Fixed

- **OnStep: a shorter pulse on one axis no longer ends the other axis's `IsPulseGuiding`** (#1835): one deadline served both axes, so a North pulse sent during a longer East one made `IsPulseGuiding` read false while the East pulse still ran. Each axis now has its own window ("a shorter pulse on one axis leaves the other axis's longer pulse guiding"); a shorter pulse on the other axis keeps the longer pulse's end tick ("a shorter pulse on the other axis keeps the longer pulse's end tick").
- **OnStep: a short GOTO now reports landed when the mount stops, not at 2 s** (#1835): landing was held back until the 2 s slew window ended. It is now a reading taken after the `:MS#` answer that shows no GOTO, and it clears the window ("a short goto reports landed before the 2 s hold ends"). A reading from before the answer never ends the slew or sends the tracking restore ("a reading taken before the :MS# answer does not end the slew"). The synchronous slew's wait ends on the same rule.
- **OnStep: a poll tick's publish no longer drops a newer sample** (#1835): a tick that published dropped every other sample in flight, newer ones included, such as the pulse-end tick's frame or the synchronous slew's end read, so `IsPulseGuiding` could stay true up to about 1 s longer and Alt/Az be up to 2 s older. Publishes are now ordered by sample: an older sample than the held frame's is skipped ("a newer sample is not dropped by an older tick's publish").
