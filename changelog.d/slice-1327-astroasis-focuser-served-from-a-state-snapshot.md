### Changed

- **Astroasis Oasis focuser: `Position`, `IsMoving`, `Temperature` and `DeviceState` are served from a state snapshot** (AlpacaCore, issue #1327; `astroasis_focuser_driver.cpp`): one poll body started after connect reads the `0x32` status every 100 ms and the getters answer from memory. The 100 ms status cache is gone: a getter never touches the HID device, where the old cache refilled on a read after its expiry (`test_astroasis_focuser.cpp`, `getters between poll ticks cause no device calls`: 150 getter calls after a real pause longer than 100 ms make zero device calls, and the values follow the device after the next tick). `Move` and `Halt` write through, so `IsMoving` reads true right after a `Move` and false right after a `Halt` with no poll in between (`Move and Halt write through to the frame`), and a poll that overlaps a write drops its frame (`a poll that overlaps a write drops its frame`). A frame older than 200 ms is still served; once the poll has gone unanswered for 30 s the link counts as lost: `Connected` reads false, the getters throw `NotConnected`, the HID handle is released and `Connected=true` reconnects (`a link silent past the staleness bound drops Connected`). Three failed polls in a row latch a "communications compromised" `DriverException` on reads and `Move` while `Connected` stays true, `Halt` is never refused, and the first good poll restores service (`failed polls are not served once the link latches`). The latched fault text is reported by `get_link_fault()`, and the `DeviceState` `TimeStamp` is when the frame was measured. A `create_astroasis_focuser` overload takes the `TaskClock` the poll waits on. A removed hidraw node is found by the 30 s bound, not at once. ConformU not re-run (fake-only validation, no hardware).
