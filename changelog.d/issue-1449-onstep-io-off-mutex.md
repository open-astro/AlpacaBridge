### Fixed
- **OnStep telescope: getters no longer wait behind a serial exchange** (AlpacaCore, issue #1449): every exchange with the mount now runs with the driver state lock free, under a per-command operation lock in the same order as the Sky-Watcher slice (`transition_mutex_`, then `io_mutex_`, then `mutex_` as a leaf). `Slewing`, the position getters, `Tracking`, `AtPark`, `AtHome`, `SideOfPier`, `UTCDate`, `SiderealTime`, the site getters and `DeviceState` answer from the driver's state and its 2 s caches while a Park, FindHome (and their background part), SetPark, Unpark, a Tracking write, a slew, a sync, a UTCDate or site write or the MoveAxis re-send is on the wire; a getter whose cache is stale still asks the mount, and waits for the wire for that one read. The stops before a Park or FindHome, AbortSlew's, `MoveAxis(axis, 0)`'s, the refused re-send's and the relink's share one helper and no longer hold the state lock. While a connect is on the wire a getter reads `NotConnected` at once instead of waiting for it. Park, FindHome, slews, syncs and the stops behave as before.

### Changed (docs)
- **OnStep instructions and decision 0016** (docs, issue #1449): the lock order and the `StateLock`/`Wire` rule are written down in `onstep.instructions.md`, and decision 0016 no longer lists OnStep as left out of the #295 slicing.

### Added (tests)
- **OnStep getter timing** (issue #1449): `test_onstep_getter_locks.cpp` times twenty-one getters against ten commands, the Park and FindHome background part, the MoveAxis re-send tick and a connect on the wire (each under 100 ms; replies held 300 ms), and reads getters during a disconnect's close; a `[stress]` case races Park, FindHome, AbortSlew, MoveAxis and a reconnect against the getters under TSan.
