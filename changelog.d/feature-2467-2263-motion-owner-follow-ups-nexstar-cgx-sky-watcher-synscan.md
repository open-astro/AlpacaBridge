### Fixed

- **SynScan PulseGuide beside a MoveAxis on the other axis**, SynScan driver, issue #2467. A PulseGuide is accepted while a MoveAxis drives the other axis, as the #2263 motion-claim table says: each axis is its own motor command, and the pulse's end stops or hands back to tracking only its own axis, so the jog keeps running. A MoveAxis on the pulse's own axis still refuses it with `InvalidOperation` (`test_synscan_motion_owner.cpp`).
- **Sky-Watcher superseded pulse dispatch logged at INFO**, Sky-Watcher driver, issue #2467. A PulseGuide whose dispatch loses its axis to a newer motion command is an expected outcome, so it is logged at INFO instead of as a WARN dispatch failure. Tests also show that a MoveAxis on the other axis does not supersede the per-axis stop-waits of Tracking false, DeclinationRate (zero and duty rates) and an RA duty rate (`test_skywatcher_motion_owner.cpp`).
- **NexStar blocking slew keeps a running pulse when its pier read fails**, Celestron NexStar driver, issue #2467. A blocking SlewToCoordinates reads the pier side before it claims the mount, as the async slew does, so a failed read refuses the slew and leaves a running PulseGuide alone instead of ending it. Tests also pin that PulseGuide is accepted again after a Park completes and Unpark runs, and that a refused pulse chain start stops its axis and closes the pulse (`test_celestron_nexstar_motion_owner.cpp`).
- **CGX ambiguous-pulse refusal names its cause**, Celestron CGX driver, issue #2467. A PulseGuide on an axis whose running pulse lost its first acknowledgement is refused with `InvalidOperation` and a text that says so, instead of the generic overlap message. A pulse body superseded before its guide-active poll or its end-of-pulse position read sends that read nothing, and a superseding command's wait for the guide packet ends within 3.05 s. Tests also pin that a kept pulse failure refuses a Dec pulse too with nothing sent, that a FindHome or Park that superseded a slew and then failed its start leaves the slew's Tracking restore unsent, and that a retarget whose GOTO is refused restores Tracking (`test_celestron_cgx_motion_owner.cpp`).
