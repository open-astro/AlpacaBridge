### Added (tests)

- **WeeWX fetcher seam for the stress suite** (AlpacaCore, issue #1070): `create_weewx_observingconditions` has an overload taking a `WeeWxFetcher`, and the existing overload delegates to it with the libcurl fetch, so production is unchanged.
