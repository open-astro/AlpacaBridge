### Changed (CI)
- **Coverage build hardening** (CI, follow-up to the #1308 review): `.github/scripts/coverage-compilers.sh` also records the sha256 of the compiler proper (`cc1`, `cc1plus`), so a toolchain update that changes only the backend starts a fresh cache, and quotes the compiler path in the wrapper. The coverage summary step fails when the AlpacaCore and AlpacaHTTP line totals do not add up to the combined total, which would mean gcovr stopped applying `--filter` to the JSON tracefile. The `clang-tidy` job zeroes ccache's statistics before its build and warns when fewer than half its compiles hit `build-vendors`' cache.
