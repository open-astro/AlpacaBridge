### Changed

- **SynScan accepts site, time and Sync writes right after AbortSlew** (SynScan telescope, issue #2586): AbortSlew stops the mount and Slewing reads false at once, but the cancelled GOTO or Park body could still be winding down for a moment, and until it returned SiteLatitude, SiteLongitude, UTCDate and SyncToCoordinates were refused with "cannot be written while the mount is moving". A stopped body no longer counts as motion (`AsyncOperation::live()`), so those writes are accepted as soon as AbortSlew returns; the stopped body sends nothing more. A write during a live GOTO, Park, landing or MoveAxis is still refused. The other mount drivers with this refusal (OnStep, ZWO AM, Celestron NexStar) clear their motion state inside AbortSlew and had no such window; Bisque refuses none of these writes; Sky-Watcher is left to a follow-up.
