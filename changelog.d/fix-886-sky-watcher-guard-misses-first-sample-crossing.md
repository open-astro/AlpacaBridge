### Fixed
- **Sky-Watcher limit guard no longer misses a crossing before its first sample** (AlpacaCore, issue #886): a `MoveAxis` or tracking start from rest now takes the guard's baseline position before the first motion command goes out, so a mount that is already past the altitude floor or meridian limit by the guard's first sample is stopped instead of having that outside position accepted as the baseline. A mount that starts outside the limit can still be driven back in. Covered by `SkyWatcher limits - a floor crossed before the guard's first sample is stopped` (MoveAxis) and `SkyWatcher limits - a meridian limit crossed before tracking's first sample is stopped` (tracking).
