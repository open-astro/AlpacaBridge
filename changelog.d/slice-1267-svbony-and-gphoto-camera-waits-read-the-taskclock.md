### Changed

- **SVBONY and gphoto camera waits read the task clock** (SVBONY, gphoto, issue #1267): the SVBONY pulse-guide end, exposure watchdog deadline and readout wait deadline, and the gphoto exposure watchdog deadline, bulb hold deadline and bulb file wait deadline now read an injected `util::TaskClock` (real by default), so tests drive them with `FakeTaskClock::advance()` instead of sleeping. The `create_svbony_camera(int, int, SVBSDK&)` and `create_gphoto_camera(int, int, GPhotoSDK&, RawDecoder&)` test factories take an optional clock; production factories, exposure start timestamps (`system_clock`) and the SDK call timeouts are unchanged. Covered by the `[taskclock]` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_svbony_fake_sdk.cpp` and `AlpacaCore/tests/test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp`. ConformU not re-run.
