### Fixed
- **SynScan logs a pulse stop that fails after a cancel** (SynScan, issue #1725): when a pulse is cancelled by a replacement pulse, AbortSlew, a disconnect or driver destruction, the best-effort stop of the axis swallowed its failure, so a dead link left the axis possibly moving with nothing logged. The failed stop is now logged at ERROR and the axis marked as possibly moving (`Slewing` true until AbortSlew, MoveAxis(0) or a disconnect), as the refused-start path already did. Teardown still completes and the error is not stored for the next command. Covered by "a cancelled pulse whose stop fails logs the failure" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_synscan_telescope.cpp`.
