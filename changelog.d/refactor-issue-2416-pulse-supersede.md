### Changed
- **Sky-Watcher and SynScan: a slew, Park or FindHome supersedes a running pulse instead of joining it** (Sky-Watcher and SynScan telescope drivers, issue #2416): decision 0006 rule 3 as the maintainer settled it on #2263 (one rule on every mount). Sky-Watcher's goto, Park and FindHome supersede both pulses inside `claim_slew_slot_locked()` and no longer cancel and join them (`stop_pulse_ops()`); a PulseGuide on an axis with a running pulse supersedes it (its sequence plus a claim on the axis, which ends a dispatch stop-wait the old pulse is parked in) instead of `cancel_all_and_join()`. SynScan's SlewToCoordinates, SlewToCoordinatesAsync and Park no longer call `join_pulse_bodies()`: after every check that can refuse them they supersede each running pulse and send the plain stop it owed themselves, with no wire turn, before their GOTO (`supersede_pulses_for_slew_locked()`), so a refused Park now leaves a pulse running. A superseded pulse body sends its axis nothing more on either driver, and a slew start the slot refuses after superseding a pulse stops the mount (and on SynScan restores tracking), as for a slew body in flight. The stop paths (AbortSlew, `MoveAxis(axis, 0)`, Sky-Watcher's SyncToCoordinates, disconnect and the destructor) still cancel and join. Decision 0006's K3 entry and four stale lines are reworded.
