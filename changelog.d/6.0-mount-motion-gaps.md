### Breaking changes
- **Sky-Watcher direct: a failed or timed-out goto now leaves the drive stopped** (Sky-Watcher direct driver, issue #2407): the driver stops both axes when a goto fails, so Tracking reads false afterwards and a retried goto is refused until Tracking is on again. **After upgrading:** if a goto fails, turn Tracking back on before you retry it (most clients do this before a slew).

### Fixed
- **A SynScan GOTO that runs past its 120 s deadline now stops the mount** (SynScan driver, issue #2412): before, the blocking slew, the precise async slew and the landing re-GOTOs threw "Slew timed out" and sent no stop, so the mount kept driving after the client was told the slew failed. The driver now cancels the GOTO, stops both axes, restores tracking, and keeps the timeout error for Slewing until the next motion command or a reconnect. If the stop itself fails, the error says the mount may still be moving.
- **A Sky-Watcher direct goto that fails or times out now stops the mount** (Sky-Watcher direct driver, issue #2407): when a goto (SlewToCoordinates, SlewToTarget or their Async forms) timed out after 180 s, or its dispatch or landing failed, the driver reported Slewing false but left the motors running. It now stops both axes first and still reports the goto's error. A newer MoveAxis, AbortSlew or slew keeps control of the axes. For Tracking after the stop, see Breaking changes.
- **Bisque: AtHome now goes false when the mount moves** (Bisque driver, issue #2461): after FindHome, TheSkyX mounts reported AtHome true until the next reconnect, even after a slew, MoveAxis, Park, PulseGuide or turning Tracking on. Any of these now resets AtHome, as ASCOM requires, so a sequencer no longer skips homing because of a stale true.
- **ZWO AM: a GuideRate of 0 is refused, and GuideRate reports what the mount runs** (ZWO AM driver, issue #2462): a rate of 0 is refused with InvalidValue before the connection check, instead of being stored but never sent (which made every later PulseGuide do nothing). After the x15 fallback the mount's `:Ggr` readback is cached, and a readback that does not match the request fails the write with DriverException.
- **ZWO AM: Sync takes its pointing offset from a position read after the sync** (ZWO AM driver, issue #2492): the offset could use the poll's cached position from before the sync command, and so add the sync correction a second time.
