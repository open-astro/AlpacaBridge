### Added

- **Sky-Watcher direct: optional per-axis goto backlash compensation** (issue #437). New `backlashStepsRA`, `backlashStepsDec` (microsteps, 0-100000) and `backlashEnabledRA`, `backlashEnabledDec` settings, 0 and off by default, with web UI fields; a saved config without them loads and behaves as before (`test_skywatcher_backlash.cpp`, the catalog case). When enabled, every goto finishes from the same side of the gear slack: RA in the tracking direction, Dec in increasing counts. A goto that would arrive from the other side goes the backlash steps past the target, then comes back onto the same target in a slow final leg at a speed derived from the board's `:s` worm count and `:a` steps per revolution; a goto already arriving from that side, or with compensation off or 0 steps, sends the same commands as before (asserted over the fake mount's command stream, in both hemispheres). The position register is never rewritten, so with tracking or guide pulses reversing the axis between gotos the reported position stays within one gear gap of the shaft at every landing (asserted over a fake gear gap). An `AbortSlew`, a `MoveAxis` or a newer goto during the first leg drops the final leg, and a landing wait that times out sends none. The final leg runs while tracking is stopped, so its time shows up as a small RA lag at landing. Only gotos are compensated: guide pulses, `MoveAxis` and the rate properties are not, because autoguiders measure and correct backlash themselves. A board that does not report a worm count (Wave 100i, EQ-AL55i) is left uncompensated with a warning.
