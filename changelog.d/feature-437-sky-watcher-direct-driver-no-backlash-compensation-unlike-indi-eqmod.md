### Added

- **Sky-Watcher direct: optional per-axis goto backlash compensation** (issue #437). New `backlashStepsRA`, `backlashStepsDec` (microsteps, 0-100000) and `backlashEnabledRA`, `backlashEnabledDec` settings, 0 and off by default, with web UI fields; a saved config without them loads and behaves as before (`test_skywatcher_backlash.cpp`, the catalog case). When enabled, a goto that reverses the axis's last direction of travel first drives the backlash steps in the new direction at a speed derived from the board's `:s` worm count and `:a` steps per revolution, then makes the unchanged move, so the mount lands on the same target; a goto that does not reverse, or with compensation off or 0 steps, sends the same commands as before (asserted over the fake mount's command stream). Only gotos are compensated: guide pulses, `MoveAxis` and the rate properties are not, because autoguiders measure and correct backlash themselves. A take-up that is aborted (`AbortSlew`), superseded by a newer goto or timed out puts the position register back on its pre-take-up count, so no offset is left behind (`test_skywatcher_backlash.cpp`, the abort, timeout and second-goto cases). A board that does not report a worm count (Wave 100i, EQ-AL55i) is left uncompensated with a warning.
