### Fixed

- **The iOptron mount, iEAF/iAFS2/3, iEFW and iFR drop `Connected` on `EBADF` too** (issue #2262 PR 3, decision 0009 point 2a). Their serial writes and reads now go through the shared `util::SerialLink` verdict, so `EBADF` on the port's fd is a lost link at once, as `EIO`, `ENXIO` and `ENODEV` already were (`Connected` false, operations `NotConnected`, the port released; for the iFR both the focuser and the rotator). Before, a write that met an unusable fd failed with `DriverException` and `Connected` stayed true. The fault texts keep their vendor names ("iEAF serial link lost (errno 9)", "iOptron mount serial link lost (write): Bad file descriptor"), and an `EBADF` logs one ERROR line naming the port, since it means a driver bug. Over TCP the mount is unchanged.
