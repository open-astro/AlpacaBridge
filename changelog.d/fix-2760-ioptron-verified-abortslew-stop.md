### Fixed

- **iOptron AbortSlew, the 300 s slew cap stop and the MoveAxis dead-man stop are now verified**, iOptron driver, issue #2760. After the first `:Q#` a stop check reads `:GLS#` and `:GEP#` every 250 ms on the task clock: the stop is verified when the status shows no motion and two position reads at least 500 ms apart agree within 30 arcsec, and `:Q#` with `:qR#` and `:qD#` is sent again when a round ends unverified, 3 `:Q#` in all within 3 s. A mount that still moves after that makes Slewing raise "AbortSlew: the mount still reports motion after 3 stops" until the next command or a reconnect (shared `util::run_verified_stop`). A new GOTO, Park or FindHome, a MoveAxis and a disconnect end the check. Covered by the `[goto-safety]` cases in `test_ioptron_abort_slew.cpp` and `test_ioptron_telescope.cpp` and the `[stress]` case in `test_ioptron_concurrency_stress.cpp`.
