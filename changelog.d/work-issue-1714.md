### Fixed

- **HTTP send deadline and per-address connection cap**, AlpacaHTTP, issue #1714. A response stalled for 30 s without the client reading, or not fully sent within 120 s plus the time its size needs at 256 KiB/s (so a large image frame is not cut off while the client keeps reading), now closes its connection (`socket_send_allv` waits with `poll(POLLOUT)` for the shorter of the time left and the stall bound), so a client that stops reading no longer holds a worker; covered by the "never reads", "send stall", "slow reader" and "scales with body size" cases in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_server_socket.cpp`. New `http.max_connections_per_ip` (default 32, 0 = off, env `ALPACAHTTP_MAX_CONNECTIONS_PER_IP`) closes a connection over the per-address limit at accept with no response, logged at most once a minute; the third connection from one address is refused while another address is served in `test_server_socket.cpp`, and the key and variable are parsed in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_config.cpp`.

- **HTTP send-limit tests**, AlpacaHTTP, issue #1714. Count body bytes received with response headers in the send-deadline and stall cases; the successful scaled-deadline download now requires exactly Content-Length bytes, rejecting even a one-byte truncation.

- **HTTP resource-budget documentation**, issue #1714. ADR 0007 now describes the size-scaled routed-response allowance, the fixed allowance for short transport errors, and the shared 30 s no-progress bound.
