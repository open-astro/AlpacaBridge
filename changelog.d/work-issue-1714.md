### Fixed

- **HTTP send deadline and per-address connection cap**, AlpacaHTTP, issue #1714. A response not fully sent within 120 s or the time its size takes at 64 KiB/s, whichever is longer (a larger finite allowance for large images), or stalled for 30 s without a positive send, now closes its connection (`socket_send_allv` waits with `poll(POLLOUT)` until the earlier response or no-progress deadline, renewing the latter only on a positive write), so a client that stops reading no longer holds a worker; covered by the "never reads", "send stall", "slow reader" and "scales with the body" cases in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_server_socket.cpp`. New `http.max_connections_per_ip` (default 32, 0 = off, env `ALPACAHTTP_MAX_CONNECTIONS_PER_IP`) closes a connection over the per-address limit at accept with no response, logged at most once a minute; the third connection from one address is refused while another address is served in `test_server_socket.cpp`, and the key and variable are parsed in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_config.cpp`.

- **HTTP send retry coverage**, AlpacaHTTP, issue #1714: retained expiry/EINTR regressions, exact header/body accounting and below-floor cutoff coverage alongside the complete size-scaled download case; nonpositive test stall overrides retain response expiry while permitting writes. The above-floor reader has pacing margin for short socket reads and still outlasts its base deadline.

- **Mutation evidence gate**, issue #1714: accept production headers under `AlpacaHTTP/include/` as mutation targets, matching the HTTP send helper covered by the isolated socket-send tests.
