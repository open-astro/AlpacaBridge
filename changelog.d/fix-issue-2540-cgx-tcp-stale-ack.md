### Fixed
- **A late ACK from a recovered WiFi re-send no longer answers the next identical CGX write** (Celestron CGX telescope over SkyPortal WiFi, issue #2540): when a lost reply to a read-only query or a tracking-rate write was recovered by a re-send, the other copy's reply could still be on its way, and one that landed after the next identical request went out was taken as its reply, so the driver believed a rate write the mount never had. The exchange now reads those replies off for up to 300 ms after the last copy went out before it returns. A new test also pins the Dec-before-RA order of the driver's control reads of the sky position.
