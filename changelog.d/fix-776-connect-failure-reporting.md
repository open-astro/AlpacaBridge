### Breaking changes
- **Connect failures report a DriverException, and Connecting raises a failed connect** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #776): a client that matched `ErrorNumber` 0x407 (NotConnected) on a failed `PUT Connected=true` now gets 0x500 (DriverException) with the same message; a client that polled `GET Connecting` after a failed `Connect()` now gets a DriverException instead of `false`, until its next `Connect` or `Disconnect`; and `PUT Connected=true` can now take up to 60 s to answer instead of replying success at 8 s. Upgrade step: treat 0x500 from `Connected=true` and an error from `Connecting` as "the connect failed", and give `PUT Connected=true` a timeout above 60 s.

### Fixed
- **Connect failures are reported as ASCOM specifies, for every device type** (AlpacaHTTP, AlpacaCore, issue #776): a synchronous `connect()` that throws NotConnected is also reported as a DriverException and its client registration is dropped (`router.cpp` PUT connected catch, `test_routing.cpp` "sync connect failure" block); a failed `PUT Connected=true` was reported as NotConnected (0x407), which ASCOM `Connected` forbids ("Do not use a NotConnectedException here"); it is now a DriverException carrying the driver's reason (`AlpacaHTTP/src/http/router.cpp`, `test_routing.cpp` #358 block). After a failed Platform 7 `Connect()`, `GET Connecting` returned `false` with no error, so the failure was invisible through the completion property; it now raises the driver's reason as a DriverException on every read until the client's next accepted Connect or Disconnect, while a failure the client already superseded with a Disconnect is not raised (`AsyncConnectable::connecting_error()`, `test_async_connectable.cpp`). `LastConnectError` on the management API is unchanged. `PUT Connected=true` replied success after 8 s with the connect still running, so a connect that then failed was reported as a success; it now waits for the connect to finish, bounded at 60 s (above the ~17 s QHYCFW3 boot and the first-connect scans), and a connect still running at that limit is a DriverException and is withdrawn when no other client holds the device (`test_routing.cpp` #130 block, 9.5 s and capped cases). Other devices and every GET stay served meanwhile; only that device's connect and disconnect requests queue behind it, as before. For the client-visible consequences, see Breaking changes.
