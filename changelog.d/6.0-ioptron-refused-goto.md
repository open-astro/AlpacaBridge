### Breaking changes
- **iOptron: an out-of-range guide rate is refused instead of clamped** (AlpacaCore, issue #2257): a GuideRate write outside 0.01-0.90x sidereal (RA) or 0.10-0.99x (Dec), or not greater than 0, now throws InvalidValue and sends nothing, connected or not; a 1.0x write used to be clamped to 0.90x and read back as 1.0x. **After upgrading:** write a guide rate inside those ranges.
- **iOptron: a GoTo the mount refuses now fails instead of moving the mount counterweight-up** (AlpacaCore, issue #2395): every release since 3.0.0 re-sent a GoTo the mount refused on `:MS1#` (target below its altitude limit or past its mechanical or meridian limits) as the counterweight-up `:MS2#`, and during polar alignment this turned a user's HAE29C into its tripod. A target that only worked that way now gets an error and the mount does not move. **After upgrading:** reach such a target from the other side of the meridian with your app's meridian flip, or change the limit in the hand controller once you have checked the clearance; also check the hand controller's altitude limit and meridian treatment, because 3.0.0 to 4.2.0 could leave the altitude limit at -89 degrees.
- **iOptron: a GoTo the mount does not answer now fails** (AlpacaCore, issue #2396): a `:MS1#` with no reply, or an unknown one, was taken as accepted, so over Wi-Fi a lost reply looked like a finished slew at the wrong place. It is now accepted only when the mount's status shows it slewing; otherwise the driver sends a stop and the slew fails, and a very short GoTo that finished before its reply timed out fails the same way. **After upgrading:** if a GoTo fails with "gave no reply to the GOTO", check the Wi-Fi or USB link to the mount and send the GoTo again.
- **iOptron: a synchronous GoTo that stops far from its target now fails** (AlpacaCore, issue #2396): SlewToCoordinates, SlewToTarget and SlewToAltAz that settle more than 600 arcsec from the target (RA measured on the sky) throw instead of returning as landed. **After upgrading:** treat the error as a missed slew; check the mount's limits and alignment and slew again.

### Fixed
- **iOptron: a refused GoTo is an error and is never re-sent as a counterweight-up `:MS2#` slew** (AlpacaCore, issue #2395): every GoTo initiator (SlewToCoordinates, SlewToTarget, SlewToAltAz and their async forms) sends one `:MS1#`; a synchronous slew throws "Slew rejected by mount" and an async one makes Slewing raise "failed: mount rejected the target"; for what this changes for users, see Breaking changes.
- **iOptron: no reply to a GoTo is not acceptance** (AlpacaCore, issue #2396): on USB and Wi-Fi alike, a `:MS1#` with no reply or an unknown one is confirmed by one status read (not when the mount was already moving before the GoTo, when that status may be the older motion), or the mount is sent `:Q#` and the slew fails; see Breaking changes.
- **iOptron: a synchronous GoTo that did not land is not reported as landed** (AlpacaCore, issue #2396): a far miss, a position that cannot be read three times in a row, or the 300 s bound in the landing wait now raise instead of ending the slew, and the mount is sent `:Q#` first, so a mount still moving is stopped (the error says so, or says the mount may still be moving when the stop could not be sent); see Breaking changes.
- **iOptron AbortSlew sends `:Q#` even when the status read before it fails** (AlpacaCore, issue #2395): the parked check runs after the stop, and InvalidWhileParked is raised only from a status read that succeeded; before, a failed read on a flaky link threw without stopping the mount.
- **iOptron: a reconnect over a lost link stops the GoTo or MoveAxis that was running** (AlpacaCore, issue #2396): `:Q#` is the first command after the port reopens, so a jog running when the link dropped is no longer forgotten.
- **iOptron GuideRate reads the mount's guide rate** (AlpacaCore, issue #2434): GuideRateRightAscension and GuideRateDeclination reported 0.5x sidereal until a client wrote a rate, whatever the mount ran. The driver now reads `:AG#` once when it connects; if that read fails the getters raise instead of inventing 0.5x, and the next GuideRateRightAscension or GuideRateDeclination write reads `:AG#` again so the other axis keeps the mount's rate. After a write they report the rounded rate that was sent.
