### Changed

- **QHY tests that passed with their behaviour removed**, QHY drivers, issue #1762. The filter wheel, CFW3 and Q-Focuser "State machine" cases now read `DeviceState` from a connected driver, so a connected state carrying a `Connected` entry fails them (`test_qhy_filterwheel.cpp`, `test_qhy_cfw3_filterwheel.cpp`, `test_qhy_focuser.cpp`). The CFW3 disconnect-during-move case moves three slots (2400 ms) against a 1000 ms bound, the goto-vs-disconnect race waits for the goto to reach the wire on every odd round and requires 12 such rounds, the severed-link case requires `Connected=true` to refuse with `NotConnected`, and the reconnect-mid-move case requires the first reconnect to succeed and the firmware to read `20181114`. A new CFW3 case, `sync disconnect during sync connect is not dropped`, fails when the `transition_mutex_` lock is removed (#528). `QHYSeamFixture::destroys_driver_first()` pins the fake, decorator, driver declaration order without ASan. Test-only; no driver change.
