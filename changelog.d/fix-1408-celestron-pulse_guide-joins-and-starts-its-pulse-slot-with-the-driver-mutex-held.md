### Fixed

- **Celestron PulseGuide no longer stalls the driver while it joins the previous pulse**, Celestron telescope driver, issue #1408. A second `PulseGuide` on an axis cancels and joins that axis's chunk chain, and starts the new one, with no driver mutex held, so `IsPulseGuiding` and the other getters answer while a chained chunk is still waiting on the mount. A refused chain start now stops the axis the first chunk had started and clears its pulse state before the error reaches the client. Covered by `Celestron Telescope Driver - PulseGuide joins the previous pulse without holding the driver mutex` in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_celestron_telescope.cpp`. The refused-start stop is covered by `Celestron Telescope Driver - a refused PulseGuide chain start stops the axis and clears the pulse` in the same file, which asserts the first guide chunk, the axis stop and `IsPulseGuiding` false.
