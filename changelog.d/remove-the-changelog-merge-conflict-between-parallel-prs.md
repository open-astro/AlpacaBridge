### Changed
- **Changelog entries are per-PR fragment files instead of edits to `CHANGELOG.md`** (tooling): a PR now adds `changelog.d/<branch-slug>.md` (format in `changelog.d/README.md`), so parallel PRs no longer conflict on the shared UNRELEASED section. `scripts/changelog_fragments.py` validates fragments (`--check`, run by the `docs-drift` job and pre-flight), proposes the version (`--bump`) and, at release, writes the dated section, collapses the previous release, merges a legacy UNRELEASED section and deletes the fragments (`--release`). `scripts/changelog_to_deb.py` includes the fragments in the synthesized stanza, and `/commit`, `/submit-pr`, `/bump-release`, `AGENTS.md` and `docs/development.md` describe the new flow. A fragment name with capitals is refused with a message that shows the lowercased name.
