### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher mount commands refuse a faulted link** (Sky-Watcher, issue #882): while the link-fault latch is set, the `Tracking`, `TrackingRate` and rate-offset setters, `MoveAxis`, `PulseGuide`, the slew, sync, park, unpark, find-home and abort commands, and the raw `Command*` calls throw the same "communications compromised" `DriverException` as the position reads, and send nothing to the mount. Before, they ran into raw transport timeouts. `Slewing`, `Tracking`, `TrackingRate`, `RightAscensionRate`, `DeclinationRate`, `IsPulseGuiding`, `AtHome`, `AtPark` and `SideOfPier` refuse while faulted instead of answering from commanded state; guide rate, axis rates and `Can*` keep answering. `Connected` stays true and service returns on the first good reply. Covered, apart from the latitude write, by "SkyWatcher serial - a faulted link refuses Slewing and every wire write without sending".
