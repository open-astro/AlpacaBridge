### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher mount commands refuse a faulted link** (Sky-Watcher, issue #882): while the link-fault latch is set, the `Tracking`, `TrackingRate` and rate-offset setters, `MoveAxis`, `PulseGuide`, the slew, sync, park, unpark, find-home and abort commands, a `SiteLatitude` write that crosses the equator while tracking (which leaves the stored site unchanged), and the raw `Command*` calls throw the same "communications compromised" `DriverException` as the position reads, and send nothing to the mount; a sub-floor `RightAscensionRate` or `DeclinationRate` starts no new duty-cycle burst while the link is faulted (bursts resume on the first good reply) or after the board restarted (until the client reconnects). Before, they ran into raw transport timeouts. `Slewing`, `Tracking`, `TrackingRate`, `RightAscensionRate`, `DeclinationRate`, `IsPulseGuiding`, `AtHome`, `AtPark` and `SideOfPier` refuse while faulted instead of answering from commanded state; guide rate, axis rates and `Can*` keep answering. `Connected` stays true and service returns on the first good reply. Covered by "SkyWatcher serial - a faulted link refuses Slewing and every wire write without sending" "SkyWatcher serial - a faulted link refuses a hemisphere-crossing SiteLatitude write", "SkyWatcher serial - a faulted link starts no sub-floor rate burst until it recovers" and "SkyWatcher serial - a board that restarted under a recovered link starts no sub-floor rate burst".
- **Sky-Watcher `TrackingRate`, `RightAscensionRate` and `DeclinationRate` answer `NotConnected` (0x407) while disconnected** (Sky-Watcher, issue #882): they had no connection check, so they returned the driver-side value on a disconnected mount, and once a faulted link was then lost they answered "communications compromised" while `Connected` read false. They now check the connection first, like `Tracking` and `AtPark` (issue #656). A serial link lost while faulted also no longer reads `Connected` true for a moment during the close: the link is marked down before the port is closed. The State-machine and Target-persistence tests' disconnected expectations changed from a value to `NotConnected`. Covered by "SkyWatcher serial - state getters on a faulted link that is then lost report NotConnected".
