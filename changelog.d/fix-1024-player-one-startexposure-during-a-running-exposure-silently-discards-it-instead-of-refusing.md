### Fixed
- **Player One and iCAM `StartExposure` during an exposure is refused** (AlpacaCore, issue #1024): the driver now throws `InvalidOperation` ("Camera is finishing a previous exposure; try again shortly", the QHY wording) while an exposure or its download is in progress, instead of silently discarding the running frame and starting a new one (test `StartExposure during an exposure is refused`; the camera is left untouched, no SDK stop or start). An idle camera, and one whose last exposure finished, failed or was aborted, still starts (test `StartExposure after abort or a finished frame succeeds`). **After upgrading:** a client that restarted an exposure without `AbortExposure` now gets `InvalidOperation` and must call `AbortExposure` (or wait for the frame) first.
