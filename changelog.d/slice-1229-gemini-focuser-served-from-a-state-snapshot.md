### Changed

- **Gemini focuser: `Position`, `IsMoving` and `Temperature` are served from a state snapshot** (AlpacaCore, issue #1229; `gemini_focuser_driver.cpp`): one poll body started after connect reads the focuser every 250 ms and the three getters answer from memory, so a client polling them no longer costs a serial round trip per call (`test_gemini_focuser_snapshot.cpp`: 150 getter calls between two ticks put zero commands on the wire, and the values follow the device after the next tick). `Move` and `Halt` still reach the device and write through, so `IsMoving` reads true right after a successful `Move` and false right after `Halt`. Three failed polls in a row latch a link fault: the getters and `Move` throw a `DriverException` "communications compromised" while `Connected` stays true, `Halt` is never refused, and the first good poll restores service (`failed polls latch a link fault and a good poll clears it`). A failed temperature read leaves only `Temperature` unavailable and is retried after 30 s (`a temperature probe that fails keeps Position and backs off`). `MaxStep`, `MaxIncrement` and `TempComp` stay live reads. A `create_gemini_focuser` overload takes the `TaskClock` the poll waits on. ConformU not re-run (fake-only validation, no hardware).
