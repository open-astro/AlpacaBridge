### Fixed

- **A wrong verb on `wifi/*`, `update/*`, `/api` and `buildinfo` is now HTTP 405 with `Allow`**, AlpacaHTTP management API, issue #2648. A verb a known `wifi/<sub>` or `update/<sub>` sub-path does not take (a `PUT` on `wifi/status`, a `GET` on `update/check`) used to answer HTTP 200 with `ErrorNumber` 1025, and `/api` and `/management/v1/buildinfo` answered `POST`, `PUT`, `DELETE` and `PATCH` as a `GET`. They now answer like the other AlpacaBridge-only paths: 405, the sub-path's own `Allow` (`/api` and `buildinfo`: `GET`) and `ErrorNumber` 1036 (`InvalidOperation`). An unknown sub-path keeps HTTP 200, `ErrorNumber` 1025 and its "Unknown wifi|update endpoint or method" text, every right-verb answer is unchanged, and the cross-origin guard still runs first. Covered by the sub-path and `/api`/`buildinfo` probes in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_route_table.cpp` (section G) and `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp`.
