### Added
- **remotealpaca: CoverCalibrator of an upstream Alpaca server** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, web UI, issue #2704, slice 5 of #1753): the "Remote Alpaca" vendor now also serves the CoverCalibrator device of another Alpaca server as a device of this server, with the same four settings (upstream host, port, device number, poll period) and the same Connect, snapshot and link rules as the other proxied devices. `CoverState`, `CoverMoving`, `CalibratorState`, `CalibratorChanging` and `Brightness` answer from the poll's snapshot; `MaxBrightness`, and whether the cover and the calibrator are there (`NotPresent`), are read once at Connect. A part the upstream does not have answers `NotPresent` and its commands `NotImplemented`, and an ICoverCalibratorV1 upstream's missing `CoverMoving` and `CalibratorChanging` answer `NotImplemented` without being called. `OpenCover` and `CloseCover` read `CoverState` `Moving` at once, and `CalibratorOn` and `CalibratorOff` read `CalibratorState` `NotReady` until the next poll; `HaltCover` is forwarded even while the link is faulted. While the link is faulted `CoverState` and `CalibratorState` read `Unknown`, and the other snapshot getters and every other command throw `DriverException`. Built under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_REMOTEALPACA`; fake evidence only, ConformU against the simulators is a rig step.
