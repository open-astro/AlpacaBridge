### Changed
- **Player One camera waits and deadlines read the task clock** (AlpacaCore, issue #1279): the pulse-guide timer, the exposure completion deadline and the 50 ms image-readiness poll now go through `util::TaskClock`, so tests advance a `FakeTaskClock` instead of sleeping. `create_playerone_camera(device, index, sdk, completion_grace, clock)` takes the clock as a trailing parameter that defaults to the real one; the server's behaviour is unchanged. Tests: `Player One Camera Driver - pulse guide timer waits on the task clock`, `Player One Camera Driver - exposure deadline reads the task clock` and `Player One Camera Driver - readiness poll sleeps on the task clock`.
