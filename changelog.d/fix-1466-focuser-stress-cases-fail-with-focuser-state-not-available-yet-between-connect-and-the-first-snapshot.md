### Fixed

- **Focuser getters after Connect**, Celestron, Gemini, Astroasis and QHY focuser drivers, issue #1466. A focuser whose connect-time state polls were both lost no longer connects with an empty snapshot: Connect retries the first poll once and then fails with `DriverException` ("<device> focuser did not answer the first state poll"), closing the port, so `Position` and `IsMoving` never throw "Focuser state not available yet" once `Connected` reads true. Covered by the "a getter after a successful connect never throws DriverException" cases in `test_celestron_focuser.cpp`, `test_gemini_focuser_snapshot.cpp`, `test_astroasis_focuser.cpp` and `test_qhy_focuser_snapshot.cpp`. A getter that overlaps a disconnect and reconnect reports `NotConnected` instead of "Focuser state not available yet" (covered by the four `concurrent connect/disconnect/operate stress` cases), and a refused connect clears the cached firmware.
