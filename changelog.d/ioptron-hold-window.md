### Changed

- **iOptron telescope: the slew and pulse-guide deadlines are `util::HoldWindow`s** (AlpacaCore, issue #1831, decision 0022; `ioptron_telescope_driver.cpp`): the 5 s Slewing override and the four pulse position deadlines were raw `steady_clock` time points, and IsPulseGuiding's end was a raw nanosecond count. The slew window is armed before the `:MS1#`, `:MH#`, `:MP1#`, `:MP0#` or GOTO trim pulse goes out and cleared on AbortSlew, MoveAxis, a rejected or failed dispatch, connect and disconnect; a Park, Unpark, FindHome or trim whose command fails takes its window back ("a Park whose command fails leaves Slewing false", "Slewing is true as soon as SlewToCoordinatesAsync returns", "AbortSlew inside the hold clears Slewing at once", "a reconnect inside the hold leaves Slewing false"). The pulse windows are armed before the `:Z` command and run on the task clock ("a pulse keeps its axis while virtual time has not reached its end"). Hold lengths are unchanged. Landing still waits on the 5 s window until rig evidence shows the mount reports a GOTO at once, so the 0022 row is `partial`.

### Fixed

- **iOptron: a synchronous slew whose dispatch fails no longer reads Slewing true for 5 s** (#1831): a failed `:SRA#`, `:Sd#` or `:MS1#` exchange threw out of SlewToCoordinates or SlewToAltAz with the window still armed. The dispatch now clears it ("a synchronous SlewToCoordinates whose dispatch fails leaves Slewing false").
- **iOptron: a PulseGuide whose command fails no longer leaves its windows open** (#1831): RA or Dec stayed held, the first read served a predicted position, and IsPulseGuiding kept an earlier pulse's end, for a pulse that never went out. A failed pulse now clears every pulse window ("a PulseGuide whose command fails leaves no pulse window").
