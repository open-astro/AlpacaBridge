### Fixed

- **A healthy link is no longer declared lost when a client write lands in every poll**, `util::PolledState` (the Astroasis, Celestron, Gemini and QHY focusers, the Celestron CGX and SynScan telescopes), issue #2747. A write-through drops the poll's frame, so `measured_at` stayed put and `served()` threw `NotConnected` at 30 s (`util::kLinkStalenessBound`) although the device answered every probe. The loss check now also counts the time of the last probe that answered; freshness (`stale`, `max_age`) is unchanged, and a probe that keeps failing still loses the link at 30 s. Covered by the `PolledState - a write landing in every poll does not latch link loss on a healthy device` and `PolledState - a probe failing for the full loss bound still loses the link` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_polled_state.cpp`.
