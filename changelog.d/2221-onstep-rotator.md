### Added

- **OnStep: a Rotator device on the controller's shared link** (OnStep driver, web UI, issue #2221): an OnStepX controller's rotator can be added as an **OnStep** Rotator device, with the telescope's connection fields (Auto-Detect with the mount index, Serial, Network). It shares the controller's one serial port or TCP connection with the telescope and the focusers, connects and disconnects on its own, and a lost link reaches every device on it (decision 0009). Connect refuses OnStep 4.x ("requires OnStepX firmware") and a controller built without a rotator ("OnStepX reports no rotator", from `:GX98#`). `MechanicalPosition` is the firmware's angle (`:rG#`) in 0-360, `Move`, `MoveAbsolute` and `MoveMechanical` go to the angle inside the firmware's travel (`:rI#`/`:rM#`; a target outside it is `InvalidValue` with nothing sent) with `:rS`, whose refusal reports its `:GE#` reason, `Halt` sends `:rQ#`, `IsMoving` follows the polled `:rT#`, and `StepSize` is `:rD#`. A move while the firmware's derotation is on is refused with `InvalidOperation`; the derotator commands are never sent. `Sync` keeps its offset across restarts, as the other rotators do (decision 0008). `CanReverse` is true, as IRotatorV4 requires: `Reverse` is the driver's own mirror of the angle, since the firmware's `:rR#` reverses only its derotator. Fake-tested only; the ConformU Rotator check on an OnStepX rig is tracked in #2126.

### Changed

- **Rotator DeviceState TimeStamp** (issue #2221): `RotatorDriver` gains the `device_state_age()` hook the focuser and telescope bases have (decision 0010), so a rotator that serves its state from a snapshot stamps `DeviceState` with the time it was measured. The existing rotators do not use it, and their output is unchanged.

### Added (tests)
