### Changed

- **GPhoto camera: the exposure runs in a `util::CaptureSession`**, issue #1982. The session owns CameraState, ImageReady, the image, PercentCompleted, LastExposure*, the watchdog and the kept failure; the driver keeps its SDK sequence (shutter choice, native, bulb or mirror lock-up capture, decode) and deletes its exposure thread, lifecycle mutex and hand-rolled state. LastExposureDuration stays the actual length (the native shutter speed set, or the held bulb time), now passed to the session (decision 0020 rule 7). Client-visible: a StartExposure while one exposes is `InvalidOperation` (it used to stop the running exposure and start over), and so is one while a body that a stop or the watchdog ended is still inside libgphoto2; an idle AbortExposure or StopExposure no longer discards a finished frame or clears a kept failure, which the next StartExposure or a reconnect clears; the watchdog failure reads "GPhoto: the exposure did not complete before its deadline"; SetGain is also refused while such a body is returning; and BinX, BinY, NumX, NumY, StartX and StartY are `InvalidOperation` while an exposure runs (#2259). Fake evidence only; a Canon bulb and mirror lock-up check on a rig is pending.
