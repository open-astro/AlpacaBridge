### Fixed

- **Celestron motion initiators no longer wait for the old slew**, Celestron telescope driver, issue #1406. A new slew, Park, Unpark, FindHome, MoveAxis or AbortSlew used to cancel and join the slew body in flight, so it waited for that body's serial exchange and tail; it now supersedes the body and returns, and the body leaves every flag, target, error and the learned RA offset alone once it is superseded. Every motion command goes through a send gate, so a superseded body's GOTO cannot reach the mount after the stop of the command that replaced it. An initiator that sends a command of its own still waits for the one exchange in flight, because the protocol wrapper serialises exchanges. Covered by the `[initiator-supersede]` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_celestron_async_park.cpp` (for example `Celestron initiator supersede - MoveAxis does not join the old slew body`, `Celestron initiator supersede - a superseded slew body does not learn the RA offset` and `Celestron initiator supersede - a queued goto never reaches the mount after AbortSlew`).
