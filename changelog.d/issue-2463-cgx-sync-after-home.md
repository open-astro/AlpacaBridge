### Changed (docs)

- **Celestron CGX docs say to slew near the target before a Sync, and give the Dec reversal take-up its recorded wording** (docs, issue #2463): a Sync more than 5 degrees on the sky from the current position is refused with `InvalidValue`, and after FindHome the mount points at the pole, so the CGX instructions, `docs/celestron-cgx-usb-motion.md` and `SUPPORTED-DRIVERS.md` now say to slew near the target first, then Sync. The `SUPPORTED-DRIVERS.md` CGX qualification limits now describe the Dec guide take-up as the CGX instructions record it (either reversal direction, +0.5 to +0.8", mechanical, RA unaffected) and tell users to use the guider's Dec backlash compensation or one-direction Dec guiding. A comment in `supervise_home_search()` notes that the 180 s home deadline bounds the RA still-wait.
