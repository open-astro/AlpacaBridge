### Fixed (tests)

- **SynScan failed-restore pulse cases no longer race the pulse's stop body** (AlpacaCore tests). "SynScan pulse guide - the next RA pulse restores tracking after a failed restore" failed on CI (PR #2298, shard 1) because its clock steps met the status poll, which is always parked on the fake clock, and could pass the whole IsPulseGuiding window before the pulse's stop body had started its wait; the body then never ran. The same shape let the RA-pulse branch of "SynScan Tracking - a failed Tracking=false after a failed restore keeps tracking off" pass without the pulse ending. Both now start the pulse with `start_ra_pulse_parked`, which takes the stop body's thread from the pulse spawn seam and waits until that thread is parked on the clock (`FakeTaskClock::wait_for_waiter_thread`, new), then step until the pulse's ending command reaches the handset.
