### Fixed

- **SVBONY camera `Name` and identity getters during Connect and Disconnect**, SVBONY camera driver, issue #1842. Connect no longer holds the driver `mutex_` across the SDK enumerate, open, control warm-up and setup handshake, and Disconnect no longer holds it across the SDK stop and close, so `Name`, `SensorName`, `CameraXSize`, `CanPulseGuide` and the other capability getters answer while the camera opens or closes instead of waiting for it. The connect works in locals and publishes the camera's identity, capabilities and ROI under the lock at the end, so a connect that fails after the open no longer leaves the camera's capabilities behind; the name refresh skips the SDK while a connect or disconnect is in flight and does not overwrite what a connect published. Covered by four cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_svbony_camera.cpp`, which hold `open_camera`, `close_camera` or `enumerate_cameras` on a latch; per [decision 0016](docs/decisions/0016-getters-never-wait-on-device-io.md).
