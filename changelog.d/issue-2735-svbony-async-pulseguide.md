### Fixed
- **SVBONY camera: PulseGuide returns at once** (AlpacaCore, issue #2735): `SVBPulseGuide` returns only after the pulse, and PulseGuide ran it on the request thread, so every pulse took as long as the pulse and a 2 s pulse missed ASCOM's 1 s response target. PulseGuide now puts the pulse on a queue and returns at once, and one background body sends the queued pulses in the order the client asked for them, as on the ZWO mount. IsPulseGuiding reads true until the last queued pulse ends, a fifth pulse while four are pending is refused with InvalidOperation, a failed pulse is raised by IsPulseGuiding until the next PulseGuide or a reconnect, and a Disconnect waits for the pulse in flight and drops the queued ones. Checked on the fake SDK only: no SVBONY camera with pulse guiding has a ConformU report.

### Added (tests)
- **SVBONY async PulseGuide cases** (issue #2735): PulseGuide returns before the pulse ends on the fake SDK's clock, a Disconnect joins the pulse body, queued pulses go out once each in request order from one body, a fifth pulse is refused, a pulse queued behind a link loss raises the loss text, and a failed pulse is kept; the fake ConformU SVBONY camera row now runs, with its report, and the `ConformU fake` workflow runs it on a pull request that touches the SVBONY driver or its fake.
