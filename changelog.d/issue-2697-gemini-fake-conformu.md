### Changed (CI)

- **Gemini Cover Lite and Automatic FlatPanel v2 in the fake ConformU run** (Gemini cover calibrators, issue #2697, part of #2078): the test fake `FakeGeminiFlatPanel` (`AlpacaCore/tests/fake_gemini_flatpanel.h`) answered only as the Motorized Flat Panel V3 (Pro) firmware, so the Cover Lite and v2 drivers had no fake that speaks their protocol. It now takes a `GeminiPanelFirmware` (Pro by default, so every existing test is unchanged). The Rev2 firmware answers `>H#` with `*HGeminiFlatPanel#`, `>V#` with `*V408#`, `>S#` in the Rev2 layout `*S19<motor><light><cover>#` and an open or close with the exact `*OOpened#` / `*CClosed#` acks. The Lite firmware answers `*HGeminiFlatPanelLite#`, `*V206#` and `>S#` as `*S<light>11#`, and has no cover motor. `fake_gemini_flatpanel_rev2.h` and `fake_gemini_flatpanel_lite.h` name the fake in each firmware, because the roster and the run list key a row on its fake header. Both drivers have a `kFakeConnectableRoster` row, a tier-2 contract sweep host, a `fake_conformu_server` host and a `run` row in `scripts/conformu_fake_runs.tsv`, with reports at `AlpacaCore/conformu/Gemini/Fake/Fake-covercalibrator-fake_gemini_flatpanel_rev2-Linux-arm64.txt` and `...-fake_gemini_flatpanel_lite-Linux-arm64.txt`. The v2 ConformU factory holds each cover ack for 12 s, as the real panel's 12.6 s travel does. New cases: "FakeGeminiFlatPanelLite answers as the Cover Lite" in `test_gemini_flatpanel.cpp` and "FakeGeminiFlatPanelRev2 answers as the v2 panel" in `test_gemini_flatpanel_v2.cpp`.
