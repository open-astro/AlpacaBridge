### Changed

- **The SynScan hand-controller fake (`FakeSynScanHandset`) has a ConformU mode that moves like a mount** (issue #2692, slice 6 of #2078). Built from a `FakeMountMotionModel::Config`, it answers from the shared motion model: a GOTO reports slewing and the target only after its travel time (`L`, `e`), a Sync re-labels the reported position, tracking follows the sky and stopped tracking lets RA drift east, and variable- and fixed-rate `P` moves move their axis (an RA move replaces tracking). The site (`w`/`W`) and clock (`h`/`H`) reads and writes are kept. The fake ConformU server hosts it (`make_synscan_telescope`, `FAKE_CONFORMU_SYNSCAN`) and the row is `run`: the x64 ConformU 4.6.0-rc.1 run passes with no issue (the report is an x64 placeholder until the arm64 job's replaces it). Test-only: no driver code changes. Tests: `FakeSynScanHandset ConformU mode` in `test_synscan_telescope.cpp`.
- **The Celestron NexStar hand-controller fake (`FakeCelestronHandset`) has a ConformU mode that moves like a mount** (issue #2692). The same shape as the SynScan fake: built from a `FakeMountMotionModel::Config`, a hand-controller or AUX GOTO takes its travel time (`L`, `MC_SLEW_DONE`, `e`), a Sync re-labels the position, tracking follows the sky, and AUX guide pulses (`MTR_AUX_GUIDE`) and rate moves move their axis. A GOTO leaves the RA motor stopped until the next `T` write (`goto_stops_tracking`, the CGX-L fw 7.18 behaviour the driver's `restore_tracking_after_slew` answers). The fake ConformU server hosts it (`make_nexstar_telescope`); an x64 ConformU run raised 11 issues, so the row stays `pending` with its reason: the driver does not refuse `Tracking = true` while parked (a `[!shouldfail]` case in `test_celestron_telescope.cpp` pins it; the fix is in a file another change holds), and its 27 arc second CGX-L slew bias against this exact-landing fake. Tests: `FakeCelestronHandset ConformU mode` in `test_celestron_telescope.cpp`.
