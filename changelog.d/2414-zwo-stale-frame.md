### Fixed
- **ZWO camera: a frame that began before StartExposure is no longer served** (AlpacaCore, issue #2414): an ASI120MM Mini started soon after the previous download hands back a frame the camera began on its own, so about a third of 1 s guide frames were ready 0.92-0.95 s after `StartExposure` and a guider measured an error it had already corrected. The driver now checks when the SDK reports the frame ready. A frame ready earlier than the exposure allows (minus 20 ms and 0.1 % for timing jitter) is dropped without being downloaded, and the exposure is taken again; `LastExposureStartTime` then reports when that retake started. These retakes share the existing two retries and the issue #2187 retry budget, so they cannot loop; if they run out, `ImageReady` raises the failure and the client retakes the frame. Covered by the `[stale-frame]` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_fake_sdk.cpp`.
