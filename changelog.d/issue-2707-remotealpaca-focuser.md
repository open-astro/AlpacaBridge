### Added
- **remotealpaca: Focuser of an upstream Alpaca server** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, web UI, issue #2707, slice 5 of #1753): the "Remote Alpaca" vendor now also serves the Focuser device of another Alpaca server as a device of this server, with the same four settings (upstream host, port, device number, poll period) and the same Connect, snapshot and link rules as the other proxied devices. `IsMoving`, `Position`, `Temperature` and `TempComp` answer from the poll's snapshot; `Absolute`, `MaxStep`, `MaxIncrement`, `StepSize` and `TempCompAvailable` are read once at Connect and keep answering while the link is faulted. A member the upstream lacks answers the upstream's own `NotImplemented`. `Move` clamps an absolute target to `0..MaxStep`, is forwarded in the command slot and reads `IsMoving` true at once (an absolute Move always, since the polled `Position` can be a poll period old; a relative step of 0 leaves the polled `IsMoving`); `Halt` is forwarded even while the link is faulted and reaches the upstream after an earlier `Move`; the `TempComp` write is forwarded and written through. While the link is faulted the frame getters, `Move` and the `TempComp` write throw `DriverException`. Built under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_REMOTEALPACA`; fake evidence only, ConformU against the simulators is a rig step.
