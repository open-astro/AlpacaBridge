### Changed

- **The iOptron, SynScan and Sky-Watcher 60 s MoveAxis cap runs on the shared motion guard** (issue #2651, first part; the other mount drivers follow): each axis has a slot body, `util::run_motion_deadline()`, and the three drivers use `util::kMaxMoveAxisDuration` as their 60 s. A cap stop the mount ignores ends after the guard's three stops and Slewing raises `util::kStopNotVerifiedText`; iOptron stops and verifies each axis alone (`:qR#`/`:qD#`). Tests: the `[goto-safety]` MoveAxis cap cases in `test_ioptron_telescope.cpp`, `test_synscan_async_slew_failure.cpp` and `test_skywatcher_async_slew_failure.cpp`, and the `run_motion_deadline` cases in `test_motion_guards.cpp`.
