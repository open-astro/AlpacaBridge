### Fixed
- **Sky-Watcher direct: Tracking=false during an East/West guide pulse stays off** (`skywatcher_telescope_driver.cpp`, issue #770): the pulse's end-of-pulse restore put the RA drive step period back and sent `:J` without checking `Tracking`, so RA ran at sidereal rate while `Tracking` read false. The restore now checks `Tracking` first and only stops the axis when it was switched off, the same rule as the MoveAxis(0) restore. A long pulse (1.5 s or more) checks the restored rate with `IsPulseGuiding` still true; `Tracking=false` during that check, or during the matching check right after a West pulse starts, no longer lets its resend of `:I`+`:J` restart RA.
