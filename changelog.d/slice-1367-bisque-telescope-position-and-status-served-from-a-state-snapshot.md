### Changed

- **Bisque position, tracking, pier side and `DeviceState` are served from a polled snapshot, and `TimeStamp` is when the poll measured** (Bisque, issue #1367): one status poll (1 s, on the task clock) runs for the life of a connection and the getters answer from its frame with no TheSkyX round trip, so a read stays under the ConformU fast-response target on a slow link (`test_bisque_telescope_snapshot.cpp`, "getters and DeviceState make no TheSkyX transaction at 100 ms per transaction"); the driver's own 2 s position cache (`kPositionCacheTtl`, the `cached_*` values and their timestamps) is gone. `SetTracking`, a slew start, an abort, a sync and the end of a pulse guide (the position is measured at pulse end) read back at once and a poll that sampled before the write cannot undo it ("a setter's value is read back at once and a stale poll does not undo it", "a sync, a slew start and an abort are read back at once", "the position after a pulse guide is measured at pulse end, not a poll later", "the slew Tracking gate writes a successful live read through to the frame"); an async slew ends with `Slewing` false and the landed position in one step, and a failed `IsSlewComplete` poll still reaches the client as the `Slewing` error ("an async slew ends with Slewing false and the landed position in one step", "async slew - completion poll errors are surfaced and valid replies are preserved"); `DeviceState` `TimeStamp` is the frame's measurement time ("DeviceState TimeStamp is when the poll measured"). A TheSkyX that stops answering latches a fault after 3 failed ticks (the getters refuse with "communications compromised"), and past the 30 s link-loss bound of decision 0009 the link is lost (`NotConnected`, `Connected` false, empty `DeviceState`) until the client's `Connected=true` relinks ("past the staleness bound DeviceState is empty with no TimeStamp and a relink recovers", "a reply clears a latched fault without a reconnect"). Fake TheSkyX only, ConformU not re-run.
