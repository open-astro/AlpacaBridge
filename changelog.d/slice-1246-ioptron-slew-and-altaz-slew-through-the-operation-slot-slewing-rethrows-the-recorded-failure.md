### Changed
- **iOptron async slews run on the operation slot** (iOptron telescope driver), issue #1246: `SlewToCoordinatesAsync` and `SlewToAltAzAsync` now send their GOTO from the body of a `util::AsyncOperation` slot instead of a hand-joined `slew_dispatch_thread_`, as the Celestron and SynScan drivers do. A GOTO the mount rejects after the initiator returned still makes `Slewing` throw until `AbortSlew` or a new initiator clears it, for both the RA/Dec and the AltAz form (`test_ioptron_telescope.cpp` `[slot]` cases; the clearing is pinned by `test_ioptron_async_slew_failure.cpp`); a refused slot thread throws `DriverException` and restores the earlier failure.
