### Fixed (tests)

- **The Celestron CGX USB stress cases no longer flake under CPU load**, issue #2661. An auto-completed home in the fake CGX mount answers "searching" to only one `MC_LEVEL_DONE` read. In the stress storm, a Park or FindHome can supersede a home that is still starting, and the superseded body's supervision read can then come first and take that one read. The new start's check then saw "done" for its whole 500 ms window and raised "Dec reports home done with no home search running". A real search reads searching for seconds, so every reader sees it. The fake gains `set_auto_home_searching_reads()` (default 1, so no other case changes), and the four USB stress cases in `test_celestron_cgx_concurrency_stress.cpp` set it to three: the new start's check and the superseded body's read, with one to spare. Beside 12 CPU hogs the lifecycle case failed 5 to 14 times in 100 runs (four at a time) with one searching read, and 0 times in 200 runs with three. Covered by "Fake Celestron CGX mount - an auto-completed home answers searching to as many reads as asked" in `test_celestron_cgx_protocol.cpp`. Test-only; the driver is unchanged.
