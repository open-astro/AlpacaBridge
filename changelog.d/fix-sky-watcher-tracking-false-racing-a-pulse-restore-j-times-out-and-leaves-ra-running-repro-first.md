### Fixed
- **Sky-Watcher: Tracking=false sent as an RA guide pulse ends no longer times out and leaves RA running** (AlpacaCore): the pulse task's restore ":I"+":J" could land after the setter's ":K", restart RA, and the setter then threw "Timed out waiting for axis 1 to stop" with Tracking still true. The setter now marks tracking-off as pending before its stop-wait, and the pulse task stops RA again when it sees it (`stop_ra_if_tracking_off_locked`, `tracking_off_pending_`), and every other pulse-end path (reversing restore, tracking-on-mid-pulse re-apply, Dec rate-offset re-apply) reads the same state through `tracking_effectively_on_locked()`; a second concurrent Tracking=false no longer clears it (counter, not flag). Covered by the new case "Tracking=false between a pulse restore :I and :J leaves RA stopped" in `test_skywatcher_async.cpp`, which parks the task with a test-only hook and a braking ramp. Also covered by four more cases in the same file (reversing pulse, pulse dispatched with tracking off, Dec pulse with a rate offset, second concurrent Tracking=false).
