### Changed

- **Sky-Watcher position, status getters and `DeviceState` are served from a polled snapshot, and `TimeStamp` is when the poll measured** (SkyWatcher, issue #1011): one status poll runs for the life of a connection (100 ms, on the task clock) and the getters answer from its frame with no board round trip, so a read stays under the ConformU fast-response target on a slow link (`test_skywatcher_snapshot.cpp`, "getters and DeviceState make no device transaction at 100 ms per transaction"), and Park, FindHome, SlewToCoordinatesAsync and MoveAxis return without a position read ("asynchronous initiators make no position read"); a client write reads back at once and a poll that sampled before a rate setter anchored the model cannot undo it ("a poll that sampled before a rate setter anchored cannot undo the anchor"); the live limit guard reads the frame while it is under 200 ms old and keeps its direct read while it is stale ("a stale frame is not served to the guard and it reads the board"); a silent mount is now noticed by the poll while idle, a latched fault refuses the getters, and past the 30 s link-loss bound of decision 0009 the link is lost (`NotConnected`, `Connected` false, empty `DeviceState`, relink stops surviving motion), UDP included ("UDP link pulled past the window, then relinked: both axes stop"); a new protected `TelescopeDriver::device_state_age()` hook (default none) lets a driver stamp `TimeStamp` with its measurement time ("DeviceState TimeStamp is when the poll measured")
