### Changed

- **The CGX state-frame writers are pinned and the frame's age is tested**, tests and tooling, issue #2035. Each control-path write drops the poll in flight and keeps the frame's measured time (decision 0010 rules 2 and 3), so a writer in a tight loop would starve the poll into a false link loss. New docs-drift check 26 fails when a caller of `write_frame()`, `read_sky_position()` or `publish_guide_end_frame()` in the CGX driver is added or removed without updating its pinned list. The stress case `Celestron CGX USB telescope - the state frame stays fresh through the arrival-refinement loop` runs the full arrival refinement ten times a second for 4 s on the real poll and checks that the served frame stays under 2 s old and the link is never lost. Decision 0010 is unchanged; ConformU not re-run.
