### Breaking changes
- **Saved SynScan and QHY devices with a fractional number or a boolean in a whole-number field no longer load** (AlpacaCore and AlpacaHTTP, device catalog): SynScan and QHY moved into the device catalog after 5.0.0, and the catalog types their whole-number fields strictly, so a saved value such as `9600.5` or `true` that 5.0.0 truncated to an integer is now refused, and the device is listed as failed to load. The fields: SynScan `baudRate`, `tcpPort`, `mountIndex` and `responseTimeoutMs`; QHY `cameraIndex`, `filterwheelIndex`, `focuserIndex`, `maxStep`, `speed`, `holdIhold` and `holdIrun`. `configuredevice` refuses the same values. **After upgrading:** re-save the device with a whole number (a float with no fractional part, such as `9600.0`, is still accepted).
