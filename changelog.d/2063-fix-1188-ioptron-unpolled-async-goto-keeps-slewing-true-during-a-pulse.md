### Fixed

- **iOptron PulseGuide after an unpolled async GOTO**, iOptron telescope driver, issue #2063. When a client never reads `Slewing` after a `SlewToCoordinatesAsync`, `PulseGuide` now asks the mount before the pulse starts and, finding it stopped, no longer reports `Slewing` true for the pulse; a later `MoveAxis` no longer records the finished GOTO as driver motion. A stop seen before the GOTO goes out (while its async body waits) no longer hides that GOTO: `Slewing` stays true for a pulse during it. The GOTO settle trim is unchanged for a GOTO the client polls, and no longer fires while the client's pulse window is open. Covered in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_ioptron_telescope.cpp` by `iOptron Telescope Driver - Slewing stays false for a PulseGuide after an unpolled async GOTO finished`, `iOptron Telescope Driver - Slewing stays false for a PulseGuide after an unpolled GOTO and a fresh status`, `iOptron Telescope Driver - MoveAxis after an unpolled finished GOTO leaves no driver motion`, `iOptron Telescope Driver - Slewing stays true for a PulseGuide during a GOTO sent after a held body` and, for the trim, `iOptron Telescope Driver - no settle trim during a PulseGuide after an unpolled async GOTO`.
