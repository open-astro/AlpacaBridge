### Added (tests)
- **Telescope unit cases that passed with their behaviour removed now fail** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1858): `EquatorialSystem` is pinned per driver (Topocentric for iOptron and Bisque, J2000 for Celestron and SynScan) instead of an OR over every value, and `AlignmentMode` is pinned to GermanPolar for iOptron and Bisque. The ZWO Auto connect on missing hardware now requires the connect to throw and the driver to stay disconnected (it skips when a ZWO mount is reachable), the ZWO pulse-guide race queues three pulses and requires that no queued `:Mg` reaches the mount after the redundant `Connected=true`, and two cases are renamed to what they check: the Sky-Watcher "Target persistence" case is "Target and site members while disconnected", and the ZWO "ConnectionType::Auto and enumeration" case is "ConnectionType::Auto and ZWODeviceInfo defaults" (its scan never found a mount, so the loop over it pinned nothing).
- **The Celestron, iOptron and GPhoto `[stress]` registrations connect and reach their async threads** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1859): the Celestron fake handset answers the alignment, tracking and GOTO commands, and the iOptron storm and destruction cases run over `FakeIoptronMount`, so each requires the connect and an accepted slew (and on Celestron a pulse-guide chunk chain) before it storms or destroys, and checks that a Disconnect joined the slot bodies. The Celestron storm no longer tolerates `DriverException`, which only the old canned replies produced. A new `SlotBodyTracker` in `concurrency_stress.h` counts the bodies a slot starts and runs through the drivers' `set_*_spawn_for_testing` seams. The GPhoto storm requires a real connect before and after.
- **ZWO ASIAIR Pro and Plus switch drivers get connected `[stress]` registrations and ISwitchV3 coverage** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #1862): the libgpiod and `ioctl()` stubs move out of the two test files into one shared `tests/zwo_asiair_stubs.h` / `.cpp` (the stub chip now refuses a second claim and a write through a released request, and the ioctl stub records each port's last level); `test_zwo_asiair_concurrency_stress.cpp` adds a connected storm and a destruction-mid-operation case per driver, and each driver gets a case that calls every ISwitchV3 member while connected and checks the levels left at disconnect. `check_stress_registration.py` no longer lists the pair as masked.
