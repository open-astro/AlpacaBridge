### Fixed

- **A failed gphoto exposure is now reported to the client** (GPhoto, issue #1064, issue #1204): when an exposure failed (a camera that powered itself off between exposures, a refused bulb press, a failed download or decode), `StartExposure` had been accepted, the failure went to the driver log only, and `ImageReady` stayed false forever. Now `ImageReady` and `ImageArray` throw `DriverException` with `Exposure failed: <libgphoto2 text>`, while `CameraState` stays Idle so the next `StartExposure` can run; the failure clears on the next `StartExposure`, on `AbortExposure`/`StopExposure` and on disconnect. When the failure is the camera missing from the USB bus, the driver also closes the dead handle and reports `Connected` false, so the client reconnects (reconnecting looks the camera up again); a stop in progress is not counted as a failure. The driver does not re-open the session by itself, because a body that powered off is not on the bus to re-open. Reproduced on a Canon EOS 70D with a one-minute auto power-off; the fix is covered by the fake SDK only. Pinned in `test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp` by `GPhoto camera fake - failed capture reports the SDK error through ImageReady and stays idle`, `GPhoto camera fake - failed bulb press reports the SDK error through ImageReady and stays idle`, `GPhoto camera fake - abort after a failed exposure clears the reported failure`, `GPhoto camera fake - camera gone from the USB bus mid-exposure reports a disconnect` and `GPhoto camera fake - camera gone while an abort closes the shutter keeps the camera connected`.
