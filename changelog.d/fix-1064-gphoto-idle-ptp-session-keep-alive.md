### Changed

- **A gphoto camera whose USB session timed out while idle reconnects on the next exposure** (GPhoto, issue #1064): when the first call of an exposure (the shutter-speed write) fails because the camera's PTP session is gone, such as after the body powered off or timed the session out between frames, the driver closes and reopens the session once (looking the camera up again, since a body that powered off returns on a new USB port), rewrites the RAW format and ISO, and retries that write; no retry happens once the capture has started. If the reopen fails, the exposure ends with no image and the camera reports disconnected, so the client reconnects. Untested on hardware. Pinned by `GPhoto camera fake - exposure reopens a lost session once and captures` (open, close and write counts) and `GPhoto camera fake - failed session reopen ends the exposure without a capture`, `GPhoto camera fake - reopen finds the camera on a new port` and `GPhoto camera fake - failed retried write after reopen keeps the new session closable` in `test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp`.
