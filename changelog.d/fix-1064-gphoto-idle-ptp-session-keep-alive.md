### Fixed

- **A gphoto camera that powered itself off now reports the failed exposure instead of looking connected** (GPhoto, issue #1064): when a body's auto power-off took it off the USB bus between exposures, `StartExposure` was accepted, the exposure then failed in the driver log only, and `Connected` stayed true with `ImageReady` false forever. A failed exposure now sets `CameraState` to Error and `ImageArray` reports `Exposure failed: <reason>` until the next `StartExposure`, `AbortExposure` or disconnect. When the failure is the camera missing from the USB bus, the driver also closes the dead handle and reports `Connected` false, so the client reconnects (reconnecting looks the camera up again). The driver does not re-open the session by itself, because a body that powered off is not on the bus to re-open. Reproduced on a Canon EOS 70D with a one-minute auto power-off; the fix is covered by the fake only. Pinned by `GPhoto camera fake - camera gone from the USB bus mid-exposure reports Error then disconnects` and `GPhoto camera fake - failed capture latches CameraState Error and keeps the camera connected` in `test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp`.
