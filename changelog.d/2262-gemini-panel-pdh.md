### Fixed

- **The Gemini flat panels and the Gemini Power & Data Hub drop `Connected` on `EBADF` too** (issue #2262 PR 2, decision 0009 point 2a). Both wrappers now judge a failed read or write with the shared `util::SerialLink` verdict, as the Gemini focuser does since #2262 PR 1: `EIO`, `ENXIO`, `ENODEV` and now `EBADF` on the port's fd are a lost link at once (`Connected` false, operations `NotConnected`, the port released, `get_link_fault()` "Serial link lost (errno 9)" until the next Connect or Disconnect). Before, an unusable fd left the flat panel failing every command with `DriverException` and the hub's reader retrying for ever, both with `Connected` still true. An `EBADF` logs one ERROR line naming the port, since it means a driver bug. Every other link-loss behaviour is unchanged.
