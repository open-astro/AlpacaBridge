### Added
- **remotealpaca: Switch of an upstream Alpaca server** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, web UI, issue #2709, slice 5 of #1753): the "Remote Alpaca" vendor now also serves the Switch device of another Alpaca server as a device of this server, with the same four settings (upstream host, port, device number, poll period) and the same Connect, snapshot and link rules as the other proxied devices. `MaxSwitch` and each switch's name, description, range, step, `CanWrite` and `CanAsync` are read once at Connect and keep answering while the link is faulted; an upstream with more than 64 switches is refused at Connect. `GetSwitch`, `GetSwitchValue` and (for an asynchronous switch) `StateChangeComplete` answer from the poll's snapshot. The writes are forwarded in the command slot in the order the clients sent them and read back at once; `SetAsync` and `SetAsyncValue` are forwarded to an ISwitchV3 upstream and are `MethodNotImplemented` for an older one. While the link is faulted the switch values and every write throw `DriverException`. Built under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_REMOTEALPACA`; fake evidence only, ConformU against the simulators is a rig step.
