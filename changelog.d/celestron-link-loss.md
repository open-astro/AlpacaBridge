### Fixed

- **A Celestron NexStar mount whose serial cable is pulled reads Connected false at once and reconnects with one Connect**, Celestron NexStar telescope driver, issue #1723, decision 0009 rule 2. A hangup on a reply read used to throw a `DriverException` "Celestron serial link lost (hangup)", and `EIO`/`ENXIO`/`ENODEV` on a command write a `DriverException` "Failed to send command", while `Connected` stayed true and the dead port stayed claimed until the status poll's 30 s link-loss bound ran out; a hangup in a fire-and-forget command's reply drain, or in a poll's slow-field or site read, was swallowed. The wrapper now releases the port at once, `Connected` reads false, after the loss every member that talks to the mount throws `NotConnected` (the call that meets the pull may report its own failure first; no Slewing, AtPark or tracking value from before the pull is served), `DeviceState` is empty, the status poll ends in that tick, a slew, Park or pulse-guide body ends on its own next reads, and `get_link_fault()` gives the loss reason until the next Connect or Disconnect. Nothing reopens the port until the client's next Connect, which reopens it without a Disconnect first; a pull during the connect's queries fails that Connect with `NotConnected`. Over TCP (a Wi-Fi bridge) a hangup is unchanged: a failed exchange for the fault latch and the 30 s bound. No loss time is stamped (no #521 motion-on-relink consumer). Not breaking: calls on a pulled mount already failed before this change, so nothing that worked stops working; they now fail with `NotConnected` instead of `DriverException`. `PtyResponder` (tests) gains `unplug()` and `unplug_on()`. Tests: the twelve `Celestron Link - ...` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_celestron_telescope.cpp`. Pty fake evidence only; not checked on the rig.
