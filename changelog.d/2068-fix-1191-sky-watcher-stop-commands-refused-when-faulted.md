### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher stops are sent while the link is faulted**, Sky-Watcher driver, issue #2068. `AbortSlew`, `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` and `Tracking=false` were refused with "communications compromised" while a link fault was latched, so a stop the board would obey was never sent. They now send their stop frames best effort: an acked stop succeeds, a stop whose reply fails throws the fault after it was sent. The motion-limit guard keeps a failed stop owed and resends it on later ticks until it is acked or the link is lost. Starts (non-zero `MoveAxis`, slews, `Tracking=true`, `PulseGuide`, Park, FindHome) stay refused, and a lost link still sends nothing. Covered by the `[stop-while-faulted]` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_skywatcher_link_loss.cpp`.
