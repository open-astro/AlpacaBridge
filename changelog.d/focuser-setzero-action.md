### Added
- **`SetZero` action on the ZWO EAF, ToupTek AAF and iOptron iEAF focusers** (AlpacaCore, for OpenAstro Ara's Settings › Focuser "Set zero"): the three drivers list `SetZero` in `SupportedActions`, and `Action("SetZero", "")` (name matched case-insensitively) makes the current position 0 without moving the motor and returns an empty string. It throws `NotConnected` while disconnected and `InvalidOperation` while the focuser is moving; the moving check and the zero run in one hold of the driver's (or, on the iEAF, the wrapper's) I/O lock, so a Move from the same driver cannot land between them. ZWO calls `EAFResetPostion(id, 0)` (also refused while the status cache's fault is latched, as Move is), ToupTek sends `Toupcam_AAF(SETZERO, 0)`, and the iEAF sends `:FZ#` after a `:FI#` that reports the motor idle. The iOptron iFR focuser is unchanged and still has no actions.

### Added (tests)
- **Focuser SetZero cases**: one case each in `test_zwo_focuser.cpp`, `test_touptek_focuser.cpp` and `test_ioptron_ieaf_focuser.cpp` (idle focuser zeroed with no move sent, moving focuser refused with its position kept, disconnected `NotConnected`, case-insensitive name), and the three focusers' `contract_sweep.h` entries now list `SetZero`. `FakeZWOEAFSDK` gains `reset_position` and `FakeToupTekSDK` models SETZERO and gains `set_aaf_moving`.
