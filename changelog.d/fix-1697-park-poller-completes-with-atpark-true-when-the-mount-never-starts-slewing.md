### Fixed

- **Park no longer reports `AtPark` when the mount never moved**, Celestron and SynScan drivers, issue #1697. When the handset acked the park GOTO but the mount stayed more than 600 arcseconds from the saved park position, Park used to finish with `AtPark` true and no error. It now fails: the next `Slewing` read throws a DriverException naming the miss and the 600 arcsec limit, and `AtPark` stays false. A mount already at the park position still parks. SynScan sends the park GOTO once more before it fails, as a slew does for the handset's far misses (#1105); "SynScan - a park GOTO the handset ends 49 arcmin off is repeated once and parks" and "SynScan - a park that misses by 49 arcmin twice fails" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_synscan_async_park.cpp` cover it, and a park whose landing read throws fails at once without the repeat ("SynScan - a park whose position check cannot read the mount fails without a repeat GOTO"). An Alt-Az mount is checked against its saved azimuth and altitude ("SynScan - an alt-az park the mount does not reach fails and one it reaches parks"). Covered by "Celestron case A/B" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_celestron_async_park.cpp` and "SynScan case A/B" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_synscan_async_park.cpp`.
