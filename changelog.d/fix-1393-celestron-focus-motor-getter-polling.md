### Fixed
- **Celestron focus motor getters no longer do serial I/O** (Celestron focuser driver), issue #1393: `Position`, `IsMoving` and DeviceState read the state snapshot only. The connect retries its first poll once, so a getter has an answer when Connect returns (`Celestron Focuser Driver - a getter right after a connect whose first poll was lost has an answer`). If both connect polls are lost, getters answer "state not available yet" and add no AUX exchange, so a stalled link trips the link-fault latch at the poll cadence (`Celestron Focuser Driver - getters on a stalled link add no AUX exchanges and never trip the latch`). A `Position` or `MaxStep` call that straddles a disconnect and reconnect no longer answers "not calibrated": `MaxStep` reports NotConnected, and `Position` reports NotConnected or, when the new session has published no frame yet, "state not available yet" (`Celestron focuser - getters across reconnects never serve a prior session's frame`). A `CalibrationStatus` call that straddles one reports NotConnected and no longer answers `NotCalibrated`.
