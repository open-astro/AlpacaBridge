### Fixed
- **Celestron focus motor getters no longer poll the wire repeatedly** (Celestron focuser driver), issue #1393: a getter with no frame held (the connect's first poll was lost) runs inline polls only until one fails, then answers "state not available yet" until the poll publishes, so a stalled link trips the link-fault latch at the poll cadence (`Celestron Focuser Driver - getters on a stalled link add no AUX exchanges and never trip the latch`). A poll that finds the port closed after a disconnect is no longer counted as a link failure or logged as "communications compromised" (`Celestron focuser - a getter racing a disconnect logs no link fault`), and a `Position` or `MaxStep` call that straddles a disconnect and reconnect reports NotConnected instead of "not calibrated" (`Celestron focuser - getters across reconnects never serve a prior session's frame`).
