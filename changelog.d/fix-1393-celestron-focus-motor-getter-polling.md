### Fixed
- **Celestron focus motor getters no longer do serial I/O** (Celestron focuser driver), issue #1393: `Position`, `IsMoving` and DeviceState read the state snapshot only. The connect retries its first poll once, so a getter has an answer when Connect returns (`Celestron Focuser Driver - a getter right after a connect whose first poll was lost has an answer`). If both connect polls are lost, getters answer "state not available yet" and add no AUX exchange, so a stalled link trips the link-fault latch at the poll cadence (`Celestron Focuser Driver - getters on a stalled link add no AUX exchanges and never trip the latch`). A poll that finds the port closed after a disconnect is not counted as a link failure or logged as "communications compromised" (`Celestron focuser - a getter racing a disconnect logs no link fault`). A `Position` or `MaxStep` call that straddles a disconnect and reconnect reports NotConnected instead of "not calibrated" (`Celestron focuser - getters across reconnects never serve a prior session's frame`).
