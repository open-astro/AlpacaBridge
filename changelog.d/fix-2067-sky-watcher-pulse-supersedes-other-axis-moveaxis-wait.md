### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher MoveAxis no longer fails when a pulse starts on the other axis**, Sky-Watcher driver, issue #2067. A pulse on one axis that starts while a `MoveAxis` on the other axis is waiting for its axis to stop no longer makes that `MoveAxis` throw "Motion superseded before dispatch"; the wait follows the other axis's command, as a pulse's wait already did. A newer command on the same axis still supersedes it. Covered by `SkyWatcher async - a pulse on the other axis does not supersede a MoveAxis stop-wait (#2067)` in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_skywatcher_async.cpp`, both orders. A `MoveAxis` that a pulse superseded on its own axis no longer empties that pulse's slot when it exits, so the pulse still follows a later command on the other axis. Covered by `SkyWatcher async - a superseded MoveAxis leaves the superseding pulse's slot to follow (#2067)`.
