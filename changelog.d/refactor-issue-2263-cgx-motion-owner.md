### Changed

- **Celestron CGX: a new slew, Park or FindHome replaces one in flight, and a pulse guide replaces only the pulse on its own axis** (AlpacaCore, issue #2263): a Park or FindHome sent while a slew, Park or FindHome runs, and a SlewToCoordinatesAsync or SlewToTargetAsync sent while a slew runs, takes both axes from it, where it used to be refused with `InvalidOperation` ("Celestron CGX is already moving"); the replaced operation sends the mount no stop. A slew sent during a FindHome, a Park or the homing leg of a meridian-flip slew is still refused, because homing drops the pointing reference; during a flip the refusal now says to wait for it or AbortSlew, not to run FindHome. A PulseGuide on one axis now runs beside a pulse on the other axis, and replaces a pulse on its own axis without waiting for it, where both used to be refused; a zero-duration PulseGuide during a pulse returns at once and leaves that pulse running. A PulseGuide during a slew, Park or FindHome is refused as before, and so is one on an axis whose running pulse lost its first acknowledgement. AbortSlew, Unpark and disconnect stop motion as before. The driver keeps these rules in the shared per-axis motion owner (`util::MotionOwner`).
