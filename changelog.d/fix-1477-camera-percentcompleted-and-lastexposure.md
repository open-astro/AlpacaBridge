### Fixed

- **Camera `PercentCompleted` and cooler members**, issue #1477. `PercentCompleted` applies to every camera driver (ZWO, QHY, ToupTek and Altair, SVBONY, Player One and the iOptron camera that reuses it, GPhoto, iOptron iGuider); the cooler changes are in the ZWO, QHY, SVBONY, Player One and GPhoto drivers. `PercentCompleted` returned 0 while idle and 100 after a frame (ZWO also after a failed exposure); ICameraV4 says it must raise `InvalidOperation` in every state except those where a device may report progress, and these drivers only ever report Exposing. It now raises `InvalidOperation` (0x40B) unless `CameraState` is `Exposing`, in one place (`CameraDriver::get_percent_completed_checked()`, used by the GET route and by `DeviceState`), and a lost link or latched fault still surfaces first. `DeviceState` therefore omits `PercentCompleted` while the camera is not Exposing and carries it as an integer while Exposing. `CoolerOn` (get and set) and `CoolerPower` now raise `PropertyNotImplemented` (0x400) on a camera without a cooler instead of answering `false` or `0.0`, and a Player One SDK failure reading `CoolerPower` is reported as a `DriverException` instead of `0.0`. Covered by `Camera PercentCompleted ...` and `Camera DeviceState carries PercentCompleted ...` in `test_device_state.cpp`, the `GET PercentCompleted` case in `test_routing.cpp`, and the `PercentCompleted only while Exposing` and cooler cases in `test_zwo_camera.cpp`, `test_qhy_camera.cpp`, `test_svbony_camera.cpp`, `test_playerone_camera.cpp`, `test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp` and `test_ioptron_iguider_camera.cpp`. The ToupTek and Altair cooler members and `LastExposure*` are not changed here.
