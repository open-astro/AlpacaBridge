### Fixed

- **Bisque (TheSkyX) Telescope Driver: Park and FindHome return at once** (AlpacaCore, issue #2809). ITelescopeV4 makes both asynchronous; they used to wait for the mount (ConformU measured Park at 12 s and FindHome at 21 s against its 1 s target). Each now runs its IsParked poll or TheSkyX's home loop on an operation slot, Slewing reads true until it ends, and AtPark or AtHome report the result. A Park that times out or a FindHome that fails keeps its error, which AtPark, AtHome and Slewing raise until the next motion command or a reconnect. Fake-only evidence; not tested on hardware.
