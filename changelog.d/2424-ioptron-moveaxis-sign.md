### Changed
- **iOptron MoveAxis direction is now written down, and stays as it was** (AlpacaCore, issue #2424): a positive `MoveAxis` rate on the primary axis sends `:mw#`, which iOptron labels "RA+". It turns the tube east and the reported RightAscension rises, the same as Sky-Watcher, OnStep and Bisque, so NINA's "East" polar-alignment turn already goes east. The old code comment said "west", which was wrong; the command was right. Three sources back this: iOptron's RS-232 v3.10 labels, the HAE16/HAE29C GOTO trim (`:ZQ`, "RA-", lowers RA), and INDI's CEM120 finding. A positive secondary rate is meant to raise Declination on both pier sides; the driver picks `:mn#` or `:ms#` from the pier side, as before. No behaviour changes, so keep NINA's "Primary Reversed" off. Neither axis has had a rig check yet, and the southern hemisphere is untested. The rule is in `ascom-contract.instructions.md` ("iOptron MoveAxis signs").

### Added (tests)
- **iOptron MoveAxis sign cases** (issue #2424): two `[moveaxis]` cases in `test_ioptron_telescope.cpp` check the arrow command for a positive and a negative rate on each axis, on pier sides 0 and 1, and that the reported RightAscension or Declination then moves the right way. `FakeIoptronMount` gains `set_pier_side()` and steps its position on `:mw#`, `:me#`, `:mn#` and `:ms#`.
