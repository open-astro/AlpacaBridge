### Fixed

- **ToupTek and Altair camera getters no longer wait for the connect**, ToupTek camera driver, issue #1844. The SDK open and the camera configuration now run under a transition lock instead of the driver state lock, so `Name` and `Connected` answer during a slow connect, and the disconnect closes the handle after it has released the state lock. `Name` no longer asks the SDK, so it does not wait for another ToupTek camera's connect either. `Name`, `ReadoutModes`, `CameraXSize`, `SensorName` and the other identity and capability getters serve the values read at start-up or at the last connect, also after a disconnect, and a camera plugged in after start shows `ToupTek Camera` (or `Altair Camera`) until its first connect. Covered by `ToupTek camera - identity getters and Connected do not wait for a connect held in the SDK open (#1844)`, `ToupTek camera - a disconnect sent during a held SDK open leaves the camera disconnected (#1844)`, `ToupTek camera - Name and Connected do not wait for a disconnect held in the SDK close (#1844)`, `ToupTek camera - Name does not wait for a second ToupTek camera held in the SDK open (#1844)` and `ToupTek camera - identity getters keep the start-up values after a connect and disconnect (#1844)` in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_touptek_fake_sdk.cpp`.
