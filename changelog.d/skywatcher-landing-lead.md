### Fixed
- **Sky-Watcher direct: slews no longer land 6-18" short in RA** (AlpacaCore, issue #1019): every goto aims ahead by its estimated duration, and the driver restarted tracking as soon as the landing was within 8" of where the sky would be one resume latency later, so whatever the duration estimate ran long stayed as a fixed RA offset. On an EQM-35 Pro, whose gotos cost far less than the 0.5 s floor of the overhead estimate, every slew landed 6-8" short, and ConformU 4.5.1 failed `SlewToTargetAsync` at 17.0" on 5.0.0-beta.1: a refinement re-goto targeted the counts the mount already held, the board never moved, and the 2 s start grace was counted as goto overhead. A landing ahead of the sky is now held, axis stopped, until the sky is one resume latency away (at most 5 s); only a landing that is behind, off in Dec, or further ahead than that gets another goto, and the loop checks once more after its last goto instead of restarting tracking unchecked. On the EQM-35 Pro fake the landing error after one warm-up slew falls from -6.6 to -8.2" to under 3". On the EQM-35 Pro itself, ConformU 4.5.1 now passes the full telescope suite with every slew within 1" of RA (`SlewToTargetAsync` -0.7", where 5.0.0-beta.1 failed twice at -17.0"); the report replaces `AlpacaCore/conformu/SkyWatcher/EQM-35 Pro/Linux-arm64.txt`.
