### Fixed
- **iOptron Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore): a mount reply that arrived after its timeout, which happens over Wi-Fi when a lost packet is resent, was read as the answer to the next command and left the link one reply behind. Reported positions then jumped by degrees while the mount stood still, and status reads failed until the fault latched ("Invalid status response from mount"). The next command now waits briefly for a late reply and discards it, stale bytes are discarded before every Wi-Fi command as they already were over USB, and a position reply of the wrong shape is refused instead of parsed. Seen on an HAE29C over Wi-Fi with 6.0.0-beta.2.
- **iOptron Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore): Declination stays within -90 to 90 degrees after a Sync. The Sync offset was added unbounded, so after a plate-solve Sync the mount at home reported Dec 90.71. A Sync whose position read is not a position reply now fails instead of storing an offset from it.
