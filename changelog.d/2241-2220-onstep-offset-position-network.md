### Added (tests)

- **OnStep rate-offset position cases** (issue #2241): `[offset-model]` cases in `test_onstep_telescope_snapshot.cpp`, whose fake OnStepX now moves `:GRa#`/`:GDe#` with the virtual clock in 0.5 arcsec steps at the `:SXTR`/`:SXTD` offsets: ConformU's two-reads-10-s-apart method at 40 and 0.05 arcsec/s on both axes from 0, 250, 500 and 750 ms after a poll, the SI-to-sidereal conversion, the guard on a stalled axis at both rates, the 30 minute hold, a stalled link, re-anchoring on a rate write, a `TrackingRate` change, an offset change the poll finds and a sync, the RA wrap and Dec clamp, no model while slewing, parked, not tracking, pulse guiding, moving an axis, homing, parking or on OnStep 4.x, and `DeviceState` serving the getters' value; and the driver's own commands while an offset runs: a PulseGuide, a MoveAxis and its stop, a GOTO, a Park or FindHome, AbortSlew and a `Tracking` write, a poll tick that publishes after a write that zeroes the offsets, a rate write on a stale frame, and an offset found at the lunar rate.

### Fixed

- **OnStep: RightAscension and Declination follow a rate offset between polls** (OnStep driver, issue #2241): while `RightAscensionRate` or `DeclinationRate` was set, the position was the frame the 1 s status poll last read, so it lagged the mount by up to one second of the offset (up to 40 arcsec at 40 arcsec/s) and, at the smallest offsets, showed the mount's 0.5 arcsec steps only when one happened to land; clients that measure the rate from two reads, ConformU's extended rate-offset tests among them, got the wrong answer. On OnStepX, while an offset is set at the sidereal rate and the mount is tracking and not slewing, guiding, moving an axis, homing, parking or parked, the two getters and `DeviceState` now serve the commanded position (the last anchor plus the offset times the sidereal time since it), with no extra traffic to the mount. Each poll checks it against the mount and re-anchors on the measured position when either axis is more than 2 arcsec off, and after 30 minutes anyway; a rate write, a `TrackingRate` change that clears the offsets and an offset change the poll reads re-anchor first, so the position never jumps; a sync anchors at the synced position; and the model stops 3 s after the last good poll, when the last frame is served as it is. `DeviceState`'s `TimeStamp` stays the measurement time. OnStep 4.x, and every other state, is unchanged. Checked against the fake only; the bare-mount ConformU run on the TeSeek HM-17 is still to come.
