### Changed

- **One Sky-Watcher vendor entry in the web UI**, issue #1818. The add-device `Vendor` list shows one `Sky-Watcher` entry for the SynScan hand-controller and EQMOD direct drivers, and a `Connection` choice (`Hand controller (SynScan)` default, `Direct cable (EQMOD)`) under it picks the driver; it appears only when two drivers of the device type share a catalog `vendorLabel`, and saved devices keep their vendor key. Covered by `AlpacaHTTP/tests/web/sky_watcher_vendor.test.js`. `GET /management/v1/devicecatalog` now serves `vendorLabel` when a descriptor sets one (`AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_catalog_json.cpp`, case 6b). Both telescope drivers carry the label `Sky-Watcher`, so the not-enabled refusal reads `Sky-Watcher support not enabled ...` (`AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp`), the direct driver's `Description` and `DriverInfo` read `Sky-Watcher EQMOD Direct Mount Driver` and `AlpacaCore EQMOD Direct Driver v0.1` (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_skywatcher_telescope.cpp`), and the ConformU reports of both drivers live under `AlpacaCore/conformu/Sky-Watcher/`.
