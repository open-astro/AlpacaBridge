### Changed
- **SVBONY camera exposure runs in a capture session**, SVBONY camera driver, issue #1980. The exposure thread, its lifecycle mutex, active flags and deadline are replaced by `util::CaptureSession` (decision 0020): the Exposure register write, the deferred ROI and `FrameSpeedMode` writes, the readback checks, `SVBStartVideoCapture`, the 500 ms `SVBGetVideoData` reads and `SVBStopVideoCapture` run in the session body, and the exposure getters read the session. What a client can see:
  - After an AbortExposure whose SDK stop failed, `CameraState` reads Idle, not Error, and `PercentCompleted` raises `InvalidOperation` (not Exposing); `ImageReady` and `ImageArray` raise the stop's message (`SVBONY capture stop was not confirmed; ...`) until the next `StartExposure` or a reconnect. A later successful stop (an AbortExposure retry) no longer clears that message; StartExposure and the settings writers still refuse with `DriverException` until the stop is confirmed.
  - An AbortExposure with nothing exposing no longer clears a failed exposure's message (decision 0020 rule 3); it still retries an SDK stop the camera could not confirm.
  - A StartExposure while an exposure is in progress raises `InvalidOperation` and the running exposure still delivers its frame; it used to stop the running exposure and start a new one.
  - The watchdog message is `SVBONY: the exposure did not complete before its deadline` (was `SVBONY camera exposure exceeded its completion deadline`); the deadline is still the exposure plus 15 s on the task clock.
  - An SDK error while writing the exposure register no longer raises from `StartExposure`: the write runs in the body, so it ends the exposure and `ImageReady` and `ImageArray` raise it as `DriverException`.
  - A StartExposure that passed its checks before a Disconnect raises `NotConnected` and runs no exposure.

### Fixed
- **The SVBONY camera drops `Connected` when the SDK reports it removed**, SVBONY camera driver, issue #1986. `SVB_ERROR_CAMERA_REMOVED` or `SVB_ERROR_CAMERA_CLOSED` from a control read or write, a pulse guide or any SDK call of the exposure is a lost link at once (decision 0009): `Connected` reads false, members throw `NotConnected` with the SDK error text, which `get_link_fault()` (the management listing) also reports until the next Connect or Disconnect, `DeviceState` is empty, one warning names the camera serial, an exposure in flight fails with that text and makes no further frame read, and the dead camera handle is closed. `SVB_ERROR_INVALID_ID`, a timeout or any other SDK error keeps the link as before. Only the client reconnects: a Connect opens the camera afresh, and a Disconnect of a lost camera completes, also when the SDK close itself reports the camera removed (it used to raise that error). Tested on the fake SDK only: whether a real USB cable pull makes the SVBONY SDK return a removal code is not yet known (decision 0009 row `partial`).

### Added (tests)
- **SVBONY capture-session and link-loss cases** (issues #1980, #1986): eight `[capture-session]` cases (kept failure until the next StartExposure, reconnect clears it, StartExposure refused while exposing, unconfirmed stop reads Idle, PercentCompleted on the task clock, LastExposureDuration is the clamped register, StartExposure after a reconnect, a StartExposure that raced a Disconnect) and eleven `[link]` cases (removal on a control read, a control write, a pulse guide and during an exposure; only a removal code is a loss; the driver's own NotConnected is not; Connect after a loss; LinkFault kept until Connect or Disconnect; Disconnect of a lost camera; a Disconnect whose own close reports the camera removed; a Disconnect queued against a Connect) in `test_svbony_camera.cpp`. `FakeSVBSDK` gains `fail_next_with()`, `fail_with_removal()`, `remove_during_held_read()` and a `before_call` hook on `stop_video_capture`.
