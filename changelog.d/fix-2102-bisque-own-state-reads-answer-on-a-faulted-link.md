### Fixed

- **Bisque faulted-link reads and writes**, Bisque telescope driver, issue #2102. While the position link is faulted, every operational read (`AtHome`, `AtPark`, `Slewing`, `IsPulseGuiding`, `TargetRightAscension`, `TargetDeclination`, `DeclinationRate`, `RightAscensionRate`, `TrackingRate`, `GuideRate`) and every write or command (`Tracking`, `TrackingRate`, `GuideRate`, `TargetRightAscension`, `TargetDeclination`, `FindHome`, `Park`, `UnPark`, `SetPark`, `PulseGuide`, `MoveAxis`, the slew and sync methods) now throws a driver error ("communications compromised") and sends nothing to TheSkyX, as `Tracking` already did on read; a refused write keeps the stored value. `TrackingRate`, `GuideRate` and the target and guide-rate writes throw `NotConnected` while disconnected. `AbortSlew` still tries to stop the mount on a fault. Covered by "Bisque snapshot - own-state reads refuse on a faulted link and TrackingRate needs a connection", "Bisque snapshot - writes refuse on a faulted link, send nothing and keep the stored value" (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_bisque_telescope_snapshot.cpp`) and the `get_tracking_rate` probe in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_contract_sweep.cpp`.
