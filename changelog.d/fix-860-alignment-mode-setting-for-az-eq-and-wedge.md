### Added
- **SynScan and Celestron: Alignment Mode setting for AZ-EQ mounts and forks on a wedge** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, issue #860): a new per-device `alignmentMode` setting (`auto`, `altaz` or `equatorial`, Alignment Mode in the device form) tells the driver the geometry of a mount whose handset does not report it. On a SynScan AZ-EQ5 or AZ-EQ6 (model IDs 5 and 6), `altaz` reports AlignmentMode Alt-Az, tracks in Alt-Az mode and parks on azimuth and altitude; `equatorial` reports German equatorial, tracks in mode 2 (3 south of the equator) and parks on the hour angle. On a Celestron fork model, `equatorial` reports Polar and selects equatorial tracking. `auto` or no setting keeps the earlier behaviour: SynScan AZ-EQ refuses AlignmentMode and Tracking=true, Celestron forks report Alt-Az. Mounts whose geometry the model ID fixes ignore the setting. The device save keeps a valid value and drops any other value. This is the per-device alignment setting the SynScan `Tracking=true` breaking entry (issue #774) points to: an AZ-EQ5 or AZ-EQ6 owner who needs tracking sets Alignment Mode to Alt-Az or Equatorial instead of switching to the Sky-Watcher direct driver.
