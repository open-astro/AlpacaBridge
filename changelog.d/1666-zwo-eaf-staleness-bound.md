### Fixed

- **A ZWO EAF focuser that stops answering reads Connected false after 30 s instead of staying faulted for ever**, ZWO EAF driver, issue #1666, decision 0009 rule 2(b). An EAF whose status reads kept failing with an error other than a removal latched "communications compromised" after three reads and then kept `Connected` true for as long as the service ran. A fault that has stood for 30 s (`util::kLinkStalenessBound`, on the driver's task clock) is now a lost link, found by the next failed `IsMoving`, `Position`, `Temperature` or `DeviceState` read: `Connected` reads false, every member throws `NotConnected`, `DeviceState` is empty and the driver closes its open of the unit. Nothing reopens it until the client's next Connect. A good read before the bound still clears the fault with no reconnect, and the next fault is timed from its own latch. The driver now reports `get_link_fault()` (the web UI's link fault): the latched fault, or after a loss the loss reason, kept until the next Connect or Disconnect. While the fault is latched, a `Move` is refused with the fault text (`DriverException`, decision 0009 rule 1) instead of reaching the unit; `Halt` still does. A removal error still loses the link at once, as since #1500. Only an EAF whose status reads have already failed for 30 s is affected, so nothing that worked stops working. Tests: four staleness cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_focuser.cpp` on a fake task clock, and a `[stress]` case in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_concurrency_stress.cpp` that races staleness losses against connects and reads. Fake SDK evidence only; not checked on the rig.
