### Fixed

- **A WeeWX feed that stops answering now faults, then reads Connected false, instead of serving its last reading for ever**, WeeWX ObservingConditions driver, issue #1984, decision 0009. A failed poll fetch (a refused, dropped or timed-out connection, an HTTP status of 400 or more, or a body that does not parse) was logged at WARN and the driver went on serving the last good values with `Connected` true for as long as the service ran. Each failed poll now counts as one failed exchange: after three in a row the feed is faulted (`Connected` stays true; the sensor reads and `TimeSinceLastUpdate` refuse with `DriverException` and the fault text, which the web UI shows as the link fault, and `DeviceState` is the empty list), and the first good poll or `Refresh` clears it with no reconnect. A fault that stands for `max(30 s, 2 x poll interval)` on the task clock is a lost feed: `Connected` reads false, every member throws `NotConnected`, `DeviceState` is empty and the poll stops. With the default 15-minute interval that is the third failed poll (45 minutes after the last good reading) for the fault and the fifth for the loss. Nothing reconnects on its own: the client's next Connect fetches the feed again and fails as before if it is still down. A failed `Refresh` still reports its own error and does not count. Only a feed whose polls have already failed three times in a row is affected, so a station that answers sees no change. Tests: nine new cases and a rewritten stall case in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_weewx_observingconditions.cpp` on a fake task clock, and a `[stress]` case in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_weewx_concurrency_stress.cpp` that races staleness losses against connects, disconnects and reads. Fake feed evidence only; not checked against a real station.
