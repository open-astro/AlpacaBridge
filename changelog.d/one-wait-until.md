### Changed (tests)
- **One predicate wait for the tests** (AlpacaCore tests, issue #2687): `wait_until(pred, bound)` in `AlpacaCore/tests/driver_rig.h` replaces the 29 local copies (`wait_until`, `eventually`, `wait_for`, `spin_until` and `wait_until_true`, including the one in `fake_mount_server.h`). It polls every 1 ms with a 20 s default bound. Cases that assert something must not happen keep their own short bound. Callers that passed an `int` millisecond bound now pass a `std::chrono` bound. A new check in `scripts/check_docs_drift.py` (check 32) fails a test file that defines its own copy again.
