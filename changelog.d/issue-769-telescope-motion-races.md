### Fixed
- **Celestron and SynScan telescope motion ownership** (AlpacaCore, issue #769): aborts and replacement motions fence stale tasks; pulse-guide chains are independent per axis, and `MoveAxis` only cancels a pulse on the same axis. `FindHome` and `Unpark` join the serialized Celestron motion handoff; Unpark is serialized in SynScan too. SynScan restores the requested tracking mode after AbortSlew stops an active RA pulse.
- **Sky-Watcher synchronous slew cancellation**: a `SlewToCoordinates` superseded during its wait now reports `InvalidOperation` instead of `DriverException` or returning as if the slew completed.
- **Celestron and SynScan AbortSlew** signals cancellation, stops active pulse tasks, and sends hardware stops before joining the async slew worker; the worker is also reaped if a stop throws. A GOTO already in flight still must complete or reach the wrapper's configured response timeout before the serialized stop command can be sent.
