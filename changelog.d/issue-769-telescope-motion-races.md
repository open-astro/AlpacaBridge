### Fixed
- **Celestron and SynScan telescope motion ownership** (AlpacaCore, issue #769): aborts and replacement motions fence stale tasks; pulse-guide chains are independent per axis, and `MoveAxis` only cancels a pulse on the same axis. `FindHome` and `Unpark` join the serialized Celestron motion handoff; Unpark is serialized in SynScan too. SynScan AbortSlew re-sends the requested tracking mode after its stops whenever tracking is on (also after aborting a GOTO, Park or MoveAxis), so the mount keeps tracking when `Tracking` reads true. SynScan `PulseGuide` and `MoveAxis`, and Celestron `MoveAxis`, now check their arguments before the connection, so a bad argument on a disconnected driver reports `InvalidValue` instead of `NotConnected`.
- **Sky-Watcher motion ownership**: a synchronous `SlewToCoordinates` superseded during its wait (AbortSlew, Park, FindHome, PulseGuide, MoveAxis, `Tracking=false`) now reports `InvalidOperation` instead of `DriverException` or returning as if the slew completed; the GOTO itself may still finish. A `FindHome` refused during Park no longer cancels the park. A reconnect invalidates `MoveAxis` stop-waits from the previous session, so they cannot send commands into the new one. Park, FindHome, async slews and `MoveAxis` clear stale goto and tracking-restore state when they take over.
- **Celestron and SynScan AbortSlew** signals cancellation, stops active pulse tasks, and sends hardware stops before joining the async slew worker; the worker is also reaped if a stop throws. A GOTO already in flight still must complete or reach the wrapper's configured response timeout before the serialized stop command can be sent.
