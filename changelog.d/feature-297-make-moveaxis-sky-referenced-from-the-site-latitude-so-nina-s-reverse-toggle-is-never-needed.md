### Changed

- **Sky-Watcher `MoveAxis` is now sky-referenced** (Sky-Watcher direct driver, issue #297): a positive rate on the primary axis moves the tube east (the reported RightAscension rises) and a positive rate on the secondary axis moves it north (the reported Declination rises), in both hemispheres and on both pier sides, matching `indi-eqmod`; the site latitude and the Dec branch pick the axis direction. Goto, tracking, guiding and the rate magnitude are unchanged. **After upgrading, turn off NINA's "Primary Reversed" and any secondary reverse option for this driver**, because the driver now handles the direction itself. Tests: `test_skywatcher_pointing.cpp`, "MoveAxis is sky-referenced on the primary axis, both hemispheres (#297)" and "... on the secondary axis, both hemispheres and pier sides (#297)".
