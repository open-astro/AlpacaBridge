### Fixed

- **OnStep: `Connected` no longer waits for a connect in progress** (issue #910): `Connected` took the driver lock that `Connected=true` holds for the whole connect (port open, handshake, site and time sync, cache warm-up, and an auto-detect scan when no port is set), so `DeviceState`, which checks `Connected` first, waited until the connect finished, and so did a `Connected` read that landed just before the connect showed as in progress (the router checks `Connecting` before its other `Connected` reads). It is now read lock-free, as on SynScan, and still reads false until the connect has finished. Tests: `test_onstep_telescope.cpp` "OnStep Telescope Driver - Connected answers while a connect is in flight" and `test_onstep_link_loss.cpp` "OnStep link loss - Connected stays false through the relink's warm-up".
