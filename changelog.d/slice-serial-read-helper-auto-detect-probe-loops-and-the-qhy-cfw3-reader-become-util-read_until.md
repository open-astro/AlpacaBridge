### Changed

- **Serial probe and QHY CFW3 reads on `util::read_until`**, Gemini focuser and flat panel, iOptron mount, iEAF and iEFW, Celestron, SynScan, ZWO mount, Sky-Watcher, WandererAstro and QHY CFW3 wrappers, issue #1630. Each auto-detect probe loop and the CFW3 `read_reply` now use one `read_until` loop instead of a hand-written byte loop (the SynScan handset and Wanderer probes loop once per token). A probe accepts and refuses the same replies as before; a timeout, overflow or hangup still means "not this device". A WandererBox connect now fails at once when the port hangs up or a read errors while it waits for the first frame, where it used to retry until its deadline (test: `Connect fails at once when the port hangs up mid-wait`). Covered by the existing serial-ownership, probe and CFW3 tests, which pass unchanged.
