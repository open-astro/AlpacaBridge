### Changed

- **Serial probe and QHY CFW3 reads on `util::read_until`**, Gemini focuser and flat panel, iOptron mount, iEAF and iEFW, Celestron, SynScan, ZWO mount, Sky-Watcher, WandererAstro and QHY CFW3 wrappers, issue #1630. Each auto-detect probe loop and the CFW3 `read_reply` now use one `read_until` loop instead of a hand-written byte loop (the SynScan handset and Wanderer probes loop once per token). A probe accepts and refuses the same replies as before: it judges the bytes it got (the Gemini focuser, iEAF, iEFW, Sky-Watcher and ZWO mount probes parse a partial reply as before, the Wanderer probes drop an overflowed token and keep listening) and a hangup means "not this device" (the Wanderer cover and filter wheel probes still back off 50 ms and listen on until their deadline). A WandererBox connect now fails at once when the port hangs up or a read errors while it waits for the first frame, and a WandererRotator connect fails at once when the port hangs up mid-handshake, where both used to retry until their deadline (tests: `Connect fails at once when the port hangs up mid-wait`, `Connect fails at once when the port hangs up mid-handshake`). Covered by the existing serial-ownership, probe and CFW3 tests, which pass unchanged.
