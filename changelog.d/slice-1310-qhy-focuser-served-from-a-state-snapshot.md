### Changed

- **QHY Q-Focuser: `Position`, `IsMoving`, `Temperature` and `DeviceState` are served from a state snapshot** (AlpacaCore, issue #1310; `qhy_focuser_driver.cpp`): one poll body started after connect reads position and telemetry every 250 ms and the getters answer from memory. The 100 ms position cache and the 1 s telemetry cache are gone: a getter never touches the wire, where the old caches refilled on a read after their expiry (`test_qhy_focuser_snapshot.cpp`: 150 getter calls, after a real pause longer than 100 ms, put zero commands on the wire, and the values follow the device after the next tick). The `DeviceState` `TimeStamp` is when the frame was measured (`DeviceState TimeStamp is when the frame was measured`; `FocuserDriver` gains a `device_state_age()` hook, as the telescope base has). `Move` and `Halt` write through, so `IsMoving` reads true right after a `Move` and false right after `Halt`. A frame older than 500 ms is still served; once the poll has gone unanswered for 30 s the link counts as lost: `Connected` reads false, the getters throw `NotConnected`, the port is released and `Connected=true` reconnects (`a stale frame is served until the link-loss bound, then Connected drops`). Three failed polls in a row latch a "communications compromised" `DriverException` on reads and `Move` while `Connected` stays true, `Halt` is never refused, and the first good poll restores service (`three failed polls latch a fault that the next good poll clears`). An unplugged port is now seen by the next poll rather than by the getter itself (the tests allow up to 1.5 s). The latched fault text is reported by `get_link_fault()`, and kept after the link is lost. A `create_qhy_focuser` overload takes the `TaskClock` the poll waits on. ConformU not re-run (fake-only validation, no hardware).
