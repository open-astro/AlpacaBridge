### Fixed

- **ZWO mount connect now requires a verified AM identity**, ZWO telescope driver, issue #1523. `Connected=true` refuses a device whose `:GVP` reply is not a ZWO AM model (`AM`, digits, at most one capital letter: AM3, AM5, AM5N, AM7), or that does not answer. The probe is tried twice, then the connect throws `NotConnected` with a reason that quotes the reply, and nothing is written to the device (no site, time, guide-rate or tracking-rate traffic). The driver cleans up and an explicit retry against a device that answers `AM5#` connects. A failed first telemetry sample after a verified identity still publishes `Connected=true`. Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_telescope.cpp` ("A non-ZWO :GVP reply refuses the connect and writes nothing", "A silent device refuses the connect with a readable reason", "A failed first sample after a verified identity still connects", "A refused connect can be retried against a device that answers AM5").
