### Changed
- **QHY camera, filter wheel and Q-Focuser moved into the device catalog** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, issue #938): one schema (`qhy_schema.cpp`, every build) and one factory (`qhy_catalog.cpp`, only under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_QHY`) for the three device types replace the router's three register arms, three sanitize branches and four driver includes. The vendors-OFF text stays "QHY support not enabled. Rebuild with -DALPACACORE_ENABLE_QHY=ON" (test `QHY vendors-OFF refusal text`). The API still refuses a camera or integrated wheel with neither `cameraIndex` nor `cameraId`, an unknown `wheelType`, a CFW3 `connectionType` other than `auto`/`serial` and a CFW3 serial wheel with no `portPath`, with the old texts (tests `Builtin catalog - QHY camera and integrated wheel need a camera index or id` and `Builtin catalog - QHY CFW3 wheel normalize refuses a bad wheelType, connectionType or endpoint`). The CFW3 wheel and the Q-Focuser still construct without a serial scan (the scan runs at connect, #659): the factory calls `create_qhy_cfw3_filterwheel_by_index` / `create_qhy_focuser_by_index`, never a `resolve_*` function, and refuses a saved CFW3 serial wheel with no port instead of falling through to the probe that DTR-resets every CP210x. `GET /management/v1/devicecatalog` now lists the three `qhy` descriptors. The Q-Focuser `maxStep`, `speed`, `holdIhold`, `holdIrun` ranges and `temperatureSource` values are now checked per field, so the API refusal text is the catalog's ("speed is out of range (min 1) (max 8)", test `Builtin catalog - QHY Q-Focuser settings are range checked per field`) and a saved out-of-range value falls back to its default instead of keeping the device from loading. A saved filter wheel or camera entry keeps every field the device type declares whatever its `wheelType` or `connectionType` (for example an integrated wheel keeps a stale `portPath`), and a saved CFW3 wheel with no port logs "config normalized: ..." instead of "Skipping persisted device: ...". `scripts/check_layering.py`'s AlpacaHTTP vendor-include baseline drops from 29 to 25. A fractional or boolean index or setting is now refused as the wrong type; for saved entries, see Breaking changes.
