### Fixed

- **Target coordinates now report "not connected" while disconnected** (OnStep, iOptron, Celestron NexStar and Bisque telescope drivers, issue #2075): reading `TargetRightAscension` or `TargetDeclination` before connecting, with no target set, returned "value not set" (0x402) instead of "not connected" (0x407), so a client could not tell a disconnected mount from a connected one with no target. Both reads now answer "not connected" first, as every other telescope read does, and still answer "value not set" once connected with no target. `SlewToTarget`, `SlewToTargetAsync` and `SyncToTarget` had the same order in the OnStep, Celestron NexStar, Bisque, SynScan and SkyWatcher drivers (a disconnected call answered "value not set" while no target was set) and now answer "not connected" first too. OnStep, iOptron and Celestron also refuse the read with their usual "communications compromised" error while the mount link is faulted, as their other commanded-state reads do. The contract sweep now probes both reads and the three target-consuming members on every telescope driver while disconnected (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_contract_sweep.cpp`); a new iOptron test pins the refusal on a latched fault; fake-based tests only, no mount or ConformU run.
