### Fixed

- **GPhoto camera `Name` and identity getters during Connect and Disconnect**, GPhoto camera driver, issue #1845. Connect no longer holds the driver `mutex_` across the libgphoto2 autodetect, open and connect-time widget reads and writes, and Disconnect no longer holds it across the close, so `Name`, `SensorName`, `SensorType` and the other getters answer while the camera opens or closes instead of waiting for it. A new `transition_mutex_` serialises the sync `Connected` transitions instead: a sync `Connected=false` sent during the one-time sensor-geometry priming capture now waits for that capture to finish (it used to return at once and be applied afterwards); the camera still ends disconnected. The connect reads the widgets into locals and publishes them under the lock at the end, and the name refresh no longer runs `gp_camera_autodetect` while a connect or disconnect is in flight or overwrites what a connect published. Covered by five cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp`, which hold `open_camera`, a widget read, the priming capture, `close_camera` or `enumerate_cameras` on a latch; per [decision 0016](docs/decisions/0016-getters-never-wait-on-device-io.md).
