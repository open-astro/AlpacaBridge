### Fixed
- **A ZWO EFW that reports its properties only once it is open connects again**, ZWO EFW filter wheel, issue #1668. ZWO's EFW procedure opens a wheel before `EFWGetProperty`, and a wheel on the rig refused that read until it was open. Since the enumeration began skipping any slot whose property read failed (#1703), such a wheel was not listed and every Connect failed with "No ZWO EFW wheels detected"; 5.0.2 connected it. The enumeration now lists the slot by its id and the connect reads the property after the open; a slot whose read says the wheel is removed or the id is invalid is still skipped, and the EAF and CAA enumerations are unchanged (test "a wheel that refuses its property before the open is listed by its id").
- **A ZWO EFW filter wheel or CAA rotator removed from USB now loses the link**, ZWO EFW and CAA drivers, issue #1668. After a replug the drivers kept the old SDK handle: `Connected` stayed true and every call failed (`EFWGetPosition: EFW_ERROR_REMOVED`, `CAAGetDegree: CAA_ERROR_REMOVED`) until the service restarted. Now one removal code from any call on the unit makes `Connected` read false, `DeviceState` empty and every operational member throw `NotConnected` with "ZWO EFW <serial> was removed from USB; Connect again when it is back" (or "ZWO CAA ..."), which `get_link_fault()` also reports until the next Connect or Disconnect; the loss is logged once at WARN with the serial and the last position or angle. A client Connect after the replug reopens the same unit by the serial read at the first connect, even under another SDK id, and two units swapped while disconnected are told apart. Nothing reopens on its own. Checked on a rig on 2026-11-03 with a 7-slot EFW: the cable pull dropped the link at the next `Position` read, and one Connect after the replug rebound the wheel by serial without moving it (tests "a wheel removed from USB reads Connected false at once", "a replugged wheel is reopened by one Connect under its new id", "two wheels swapped while disconnected are told apart by serial", and the three CAA cases of the same names in `test_zwo_efw_caa_link.cpp`).
- **A ZWO EFW or CAA status fault that lasts 30 s is a lost link**, ZWO EFW and CAA drivers, issue #1668, decision 0009 rules 1 and 2b. Three failed status reads already latched a "communications compromised" fault; it now also refuses a move (the CAA's Halt still runs), and a fault standing for `util::kLinkStalenessBound` (30 s) makes the next failed read a lost link, as for the ZWO EAF (tests "a Position fault standing past the staleness bound loses the link", "a latched Position fault refuses a move", "a status fault standing past the staleness bound loses the link", "a latched status fault refuses a move but not a Halt").
