### Fixed

- **A Gemini power hub whose link is lost reads Connected false and reconnects with one Connect**, Gemini Power & Data Hubs Advanced 3 switch, issue #1987, decision 0009. A pulled USB cable (a hangup on the reader's read, or `EIO`/`ENXIO`/`ENODEV` on a `>G#` poll or a set command) used to leave `Connected` true for ever with the dead port claimed: reads latched the #237 fault and threw `DriverException`, and a write threw `DriverException` "Serial write failed". The wrapper now clears the cached status and firmware, releases the port and stops its reader at once; `Connected` reads false, every member throws `NotConnected`, `DeviceState` is empty, and a write to the pulled adapter throws `NotConnected`. A hub that stops answering with the cable in still latches the fault after three unanswered polls (about 6 s, `Connected` true, `DriverException`), and a fault that stands for `util::kLinkStalenessBound` (30 s, stamped on the task clock) is a lost link the same way, so a silent hub reads `Connected` false about 36 s after its last frame. `get_link_fault()` keeps the loss reason ("Serial link lost (errno N)", or the fault text the bound ended) until the next Connect or Disconnect; the fault text is now "<reason> (3 consecutive failures)" from `util::PolledLinkHealth` instead of "no status frame for N consecutive polls (S s)". A hangup during the connect fails it with `NotConnected` "Gemini power hub link lost during connect" and keeps no reason. Nothing reopens the port on its own: the client's Connect over the lost link reopens it without a Disconnect first, and its Disconnect completes. `create_gemini_pdh_switch()` and `create_gemini_pdh_switch_by_index()` take a trailing `util::TaskClock&` (the real clock by default).
- **The Gemini focuser reads MaxStep once at connect**, Gemini Automatic Astro Focuser Pro, issue #1840, decision 0016 rule 3. `MaxStep` and `MaxIncrement` sent `:08#` on every read, under the wrapper lock and behind any exchange in flight, and `Move` read it live for its clamp. The connect now reads it once (inside the connect's 1 s exchange budget) and publishes it; `MaxStep`, `MaxIncrement` and the `Move` clamp serve that value and never touch the wire. A connect whose `:08#` read fails is refused with `DriverException` "Gemini focuser did not report its maximum position" and releases the port.

### Added (tests)

- **Gemini power hub link-loss cases** (issue #1987): nine `[link-lost]` cases in `test_gemini_pdh_switch.cpp` over `FakeGeminiPdh` and `FakeTaskClock` (a pulled cable while streaming, a write to a pulled adapter, a fault past the staleness bound, a frame before the bound, firmware never beside a lost link, a pull during the handshake, a silent handshake that is not a loss, the kept reason until a Disconnect, Connect over a lost link); they replace "Dead serial link (EIO) faults the link instead of serving stale status", which asserted `Connected` stayed true.
- **Gemini focuser MaxStep cases** (issue #1840): three cases in `test_gemini_focuser.cpp` (one `:08#` per connect, the `Move` clamp uses the published limit, a failed read refuses the connect); `FakeGeminiFocuser` gains `set_max_position()` and `set_garbage_max_position()`.
