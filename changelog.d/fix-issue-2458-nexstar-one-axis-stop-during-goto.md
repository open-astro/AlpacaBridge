### Fixed

- **NexStar MoveAxis stop during a GOTO stops both axes**, Celestron NexStar telescope driver, issue #2458. A `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` sent while a slew or FindHome runs now cancels the GOTO and sends rate 0 to both axes, as `AbortSlew` does, instead of stopping only its own axis while `Slewing` read false and the GOTO kept driving the other axis. A stop that fails throws `DriverException` and `Slewing` raises the failure instead of reading false. With no GOTO running, a stop still stops only its own axis, so a jog on the other axis keeps running. Covered in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_celestron_async_park.cpp` by `Celestron MoveAxis - a one-axis stop during an async slew stops both axes (#2458)`, `Celestron MoveAxis - a one-axis stop with no slew running leaves a jog on the other axis alone (#2458)` and `Celestron MoveAxis - a failed one-axis stop during an async slew never reads Slewing false (#2458)`.
