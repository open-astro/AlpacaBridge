### Fixed

- **ASI120 and other USB 2.0 ZWO cameras no longer fail and retry long exposures started soon after the previous download** (ZWO camera driver, issue #1349): when a client started an exposure longer than about 3 s between 0.3 and 0.45 s after the previous image download, the camera ended it with `ASI_EXP_FAILED` after the full exposure time and the driver's retry ran it again, so a 10 s exposure took about 20 s. A USB 2.0 camera now waits until 600 ms have passed since the last download before it starts an exposure longer than 3 s. Exposures of 3 s or less, such as a guiding loop, start at once as before, and USB 3.0 cameras and the first exposure after a connect do not wait. Covered by the `ZWO camera clock - USB 2.0 start waits 600 ms after the previous download` and `ZWO camera clock - USB 2.0 exposure of 3 s or less does not wait after a download` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_camera_clock.cpp`.
