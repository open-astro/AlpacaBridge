### Fixed

- **ASI120 and other USB 2.0 ZWO cameras no longer fail and retry every exposure after the first** (ZWO camera driver, issue #1349): when a client started the next exposure right after the image download, the camera ended it with `ASI_EXP_FAILED` after the full exposure time and the driver's retry ran it again, so a 10 s exposure took about 20 s. A USB 2.0 camera now waits until 600 ms have passed since the last download before it starts the next exposure; USB 3.0 cameras and the first exposure after a connect do not wait. Covered by the `ZWO camera clock - USB 2.0 start waits 600 ms after the previous download` case in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_camera_clock.cpp`.
