### Fixed
- **ZWO cameras keep their device number and UniqueID across restarts** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): a saved camera entry now binds the camera by serial number instead of SDK enumeration order, so a reboot that swaps the order no longer hands a client the other camera. The first start records `serialNumber` and `cameraName` in the entry (`cameraIndex` and `cameraId` stay as hints, never a fallback to a camera with a different serial); a camera whose serial is gone fails connect with "ZWO camera serial <sn> not found". A body whose serial cannot be read binds by `cameraName`, skipping a serial another entry claims, and reports a stored `ZWO_UID_<16 hex>` UniqueID instead of `ZWO_<device number>`; existing entries get one on the first start after the upgrade. Pinned by the cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_camera_identity.cpp` and `ZWO Camera Driver - UniqueID follows the config entry, not the device number`.
- **ZWO camera identity survives a web UI edit and refuses control bytes** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): editing a ZWO camera in the web form removes and re-adds it, so the form now resends the learned `serialNumber`, `cameraName` and `uniqueId` while `cameraIndex` and `cameraId` are unchanged (`AlpacaHTTP/web/app.js` `zwoCameraIdentityFields`). An entry with only an id or index hint no longer binds a camera whose serial another entry claims. `configuredevice` refuses a `serialNumber` that is not hex and a `cameraName` or `uniqueId` with control bytes as `InvalidValue`; a stored entry with such a value drops it with a warning. With a `cameraName` set, connect opens only cameras of that model to read serials, so other bodies are left alone. An entry with no `cameraName` opens each connected ZWO camera once to read its serial; while another program holds a camera that open fails, so the entry binds by model name plus `cameraIndex` like a serial-less body and reports a stored `ZWO_UID_...` UniqueID; once the serial can be read at a later start (or when the entry is saved again), it binds by serial and the UniqueID changes to `ZWO_SN_<serial>`; until then the UniqueID reads `ZWO_SN_<serial>` while the camera is connected and the stored `ZWO_UID_...` while it is not (not checked on hardware with a guider running).
- **ZWO camera edit identity no longer leaks into the next Add Device, and duplicates are refused** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): the form resends the learned identity only in edit mode and clears it on reset (`zwoCameraIdentityFields`, `resetDeviceForm` in `AlpacaHTTP/web/app.js`), and `configuredevice` refuses a `serialNumber` or `uniqueId` another ZWO camera entry holds as `InvalidValue`, pinned by the `zwo camera refuses a serial or uniqueId another entry holds (#914)` case in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp`.
- **The learned ZWO camera identity write-back is pinned by a test** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): the `zwo camera without a serial learns a UniqueID on the API and persisted paths (#914)` case in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp` checks that a generated `ZWO_UID_` is stored by `configuredevice` and by the start-up load, and is the same after a second start. Known limit: an entry with only an index hint learns the serial of whatever camera sits at that index (`AlpacaHTTP/src/http/router.cpp`).
- **Stored ZWO camera entries with a bad or duplicate identity still load** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): the `zwo camera stored entries with a bad or duplicate identity stay listed (#914)` case in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp` loads a file with a non-hex serial and two entries sharing a serial and UniqueID, and requires all three cameras to be listed (`AlpacaHTTP/src/http/router.cpp`, the `ConfigSource::Persisted` arms).
- **Duplicated stored ZWO camera identity is relearned per field and persisted** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): a stored entry whose `uniqueId` duplicates another gets a new `ZWO_UID_` that is written back (it no longer changes every start), and an entry pair sharing both serial and `uniqueId` has each field cleared on its own: the first entry drops the shared serial (written back) and the second keeps it (`AlpacaHTTP/src/http/router.cpp`, pinned by `zwo camera stored entries with a bad or duplicate identity stay listed (#914)` in `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp`).
- **A ZWO camera re-configured after a failed load keeps its stored UniqueID** (AlpacaHTTP, issue #914): `configuredevice` without `uniqueId` for a number whose stored row did not load no longer drops the stored `ZWO_UID_`; the write-back compares against the supplied value (`AlpacaHTTP/src/http/router.cpp`, pinned in the stored-entries case of `AlpacaHTTP/tests/test_routing.cpp`).
