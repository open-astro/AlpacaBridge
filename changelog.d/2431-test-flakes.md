### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher rate writes made as a pulse ends are no longer dropped** (Sky-Watcher driver, issue #2431): a pulse runs its end-of-pulse restore, drops the state lock, and then releases its axis. A `DeclinationRate` or `RightAscensionRate` write in that gap saw the axis busy and stored the rate for the pulse's restore, which had already run, so the mount kept the old Dec offset or RA drive rate until the next rate write or tracking change. The pulse now applies such a write when it releases the axis, also when it moves RA out of the duty-cycled or stopped drive, returns Dec to the rate the restore had applied, or lands while the restore itself waits for Dec to stop. A pulse that a MoveAxis took over in that gap leaves the axis to the MoveAxis, also when the MoveAxis takes the axis while the release waits for it to stop: the old pulse no longer writes its rate over the MoveAxis rate, which froze the reported position while the axis slewed. The RA drive and the duty-cycle worker follow the same rule after a stop-wait. The flaky "an RA duty stop during a Dec pulse's dispatch stop-wait does not drop the pulse (#1706)" case hit this window on a loaded CI runner. A new `set_pulse_release_hook()` test seam parks a pulse in the window for the new cases.
- **QHY: a Disconnect during a StartExposure no longer leaves a brief DriverException** (QHY camera driver, issue #2431): a Disconnect that began while a StartExposure held its reservation found no exposure to stop. The exposure then started, its body saw the disconnect and returned with no image, and ImageReady and ImageArray raised "the exposure ended without an image" until the disconnect finished. The disconnect now ends such a start before it runs, and that StartExposure throws `NotConnected`. The disconnect, the driver's shutdown and AbortExposure now record the abort before they look for a running exposure, as the other camera drivers do: a start that published its exposure between the two steps was missed, and an AbortExposure in that gap was lost. This was the intermittent "QHY camera - concurrent connect/disconnect/exposure stress" failure.

### Fixed (tests)

- **Four flaky cases wait on the event they check** (issue #2431): the Sky-Watcher "#1706" duty-stop case also lost its DeclinationRate write when the pulse ended before the write ran (Dec was then still busy with the MoveAxis jog the pulse stopped, so the write waited for that jog's end, as designed); it now steps the clock until the duty worker it joins has stopped and waits for the write while the pulse still owns Dec. "SynScan PulseGuide - a second pulse on an axis supersedes the first without a join or a stop (#2263)" now waits for the replacement pulse's end command on the handset log, because IsPulseGuiding turns false when the pulse window closes, before the woken body sends that command. The iGuider fake V4L2 camera now takes one frame interval for a dropped frame, as uvcvideo does, so "Dropped frames do not cut the first-frame wait short" no longer sees a frozen clock on a host with a coarse `steady_clock`. "Celestron CGX USB Telescope Driver - disconnect waits only for the active pulse packet" now waits for a guide-status read made after its second pulse: the first half's disconnect had already read the guide status, so the wait passed at once and the Home it then started could run before the pulse body's resync and fail on the dropped reply's timeout.
