### Changed

- **remotealpaca Switch: fewer upstream calls per poll, and a WARN for a slow poll** (remotealpaca Switch driver, issue #2796): a two-state switch (`MaxSwitchValue` one `SwitchStep` above `MinSwitchValue`) now gets its `GetSwitch` from its `GetSwitchValue` (true at the maximum, false at the minimum, as ASCOM defines on and off), so the poll asks the upstream one call for it, not two. A multi-state switch, a value between the ends, or a range or step the upstream answered with an error still asks `GetSwitch`. At 64 switches a bank of synchronous on/off ports drops from 128 to 64 calls per poll. A poll that takes longer than the poll period now logs one WARN per Connect with the measured time and the switch count. Clients read the same values. Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_remotealpaca_switch.cpp` on the scripted upstream and the fake task clock; ConformU not re-run.
