### Changed

- **QHY, Player One and SVBONY camera fakes time their exposures and guide pulses**, test fakes, issue #2693 (refs #2078). One helper, `FakeTimedCompletion` (`AlpacaCore/tests/fake_timed_completion.h`), times a completion on a `TaskClock`: with `set_timing_clock()` `FakeQHYSDK` reports the exposure time left until the EXPOSURE param has passed and `guide()` blocks for its pulse as `ControlQHYCCDGuide` does, `FakePlayerOneSDK` reports an image ready only after `POA_EXPOSURE`, and `FakeSVBSDK` delivers no frame before the exposure and blocks `pulse_guide()` for its pulse. Without a clock, the unit default, all of them still complete at once. `FakePlayerOneSDK` reports a pixel size of 2.9 um instead of 0, and `FakeSVBSDK` answers the CurrentTemperature control its caps list. The fake ConformU QHY and Player One camera hosts set the real clock, and both rows are now `run` in `scripts/conformu_fake_runs.tsv` with committed `Fake-camera-*` reports. The SVBONY row stays `pending`: its fake passes every ConformU check, but the driver's synchronous PulseGuide misses the 1 s STANDARD target for a 2 s pulse.
