### Fixed

- **SynScan motion initiators no longer wait for the old slew**, SynScan telescope driver, issue #1407. A new slew, Park, Unpark, MoveAxis or AbortSlew used to cancel and join the slew body in flight, so it waited for a serial exchange blocked until its timeout; it now supersedes the body and returns, and the body leaves every flag and the target alone once it is superseded, and the axes too, except that a landing move it had started is stopped before the new command goes out. The slew, Park and landing bodies release the driver lock around each handset exchange, so a state getter such as `AtPark` answers while a GOTO exchange is held. MoveAxis, AbortSlew and Unpark during a Park still wait for the one exchange in flight, because the protocol wrapper serialises exchanges. Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_synscan_initiator_supersede.cpp`. Every motion command now goes out in an ordered wire turn that checks ownership at the turn, so a superseded body's GOTO or move can no longer reach the mount after the stops of the AbortSlew, MoveAxis or Unpark that replaced it (`test_synscan_initiator_supersede.cpp`, "a queued goto never reaches the mount after AbortSlew"). A Tracking write takes that turn too, without stopping a slew: written during a landing move it goes out after the move's tracking restore, so Tracking false leaves the mount not tracking, and an AbortSlew or MoveAxis stop queued behind it restores the mode the client asked for (`test_synscan_goto_landing.cpp`, "Tracking off during a long RA landing move goes out after its restore", "AbortSlew queued behind a Tracking write keeps tracking off"). An initiator's checks no longer read the handset behind the old body's exchange: Park on an AZ-EQ mount with alignment Auto returns without waiting for it, and while AbortSlew, called during a Park right after connect, waits for its turn a state getter still answers (`test_synscan_initiator_supersede.cpp`, "Park on an AZ-EQ mount during a blocked goto exchange", "AbortSlew during a Park on a fresh connect").
