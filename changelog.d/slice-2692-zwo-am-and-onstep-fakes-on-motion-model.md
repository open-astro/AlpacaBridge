### Changed

- **The ZWO AM mount fake (`FakeZwoMount`) has a ConformU mode that moves like a mount** (issue #2692, slice 6 of #2078). Built from a `FakeMountMotionModel::Config`, it answers from the shared motion model: `:MS` reports slewing and the target only after its travel time and is refused under the horizon with `e5`, `:SMMC` and `:CM` re-label the reported position, `:Te`/`:Td` hold RA to the sky or let it drift east, `:Me`/`:Mw`/`:Mn`/`:Ms` and `:Mg` move their axis, and `:hP` and `:hC` reach their positions. The fake ConformU server hosts it, and an x64 full run of ConformU 4.6.0-rc.1 finds 0 errors, 0 issues and 0 configuration alerts. Test-only: no driver code changes, and the ZWO telescope row of the fake ConformU run list is `run`, with the x64 run as its report (an x64 placeholder the arm64 job replaces). Tests: the `FakeZwoMount ConformU mode` cases in `test_zwo_fake_mount.cpp`.
- **A new OnStepX mount fake (`FakeOnStepMount`, `fake_onstep_mount.h`) moves like a mount on the shared model** (issue #2692). `:MS#` takes its travel time and is refused with the firmware's digit (below the horizon, parked, a GOTO running, an axis moving), `:CM#` re-labels the position, `:Te#`/`:ST0#` follow the sky or let RA drift east, `:SXTR#`/`:SXTD#` move the position at the rate offset, a MoveAxis ends at the 10 s guide time limit unless it is re-sent, `:Mg#` moves its axis, and `:hP#`, `:hC#` and `:hR#` reach their positions or refuse with the firmware's `:GE#` code. The fake ConformU server hosts it. Its x64 ConformU run fails two checks, `Parked:MoveAxis Primary` and `Secondary` (ConformU sends rate 0 and expects an error while parked; the OnStep driver accepts a MoveAxis stop while parked since #2435), so the OnStep telescope row stays `pending` and names them. The focuser, rotator and switch rows are unchanged. Tests: `test_onstep_fake_motion_mode.cpp`.
- **`FakeMountMotionModel` takes a tracking rate offset** (issue #2692). `set_rate_offset()` moves the held position at a RA and Dec rate while tracking is on and no slew runs, which ConformU's `DeclinationRate` test measures. Test: `FakeMountMotionModel: a tracking rate offset moves the held position only while tracking and not slewing`.
