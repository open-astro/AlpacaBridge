### Changed

- **ZWO, ToupTek and Altair camera fakes for the fake ConformU server**, test fakes, issue #2610 (refs #2078). `FakeToupTekSDK::default_camera()` now lists bins 1 to 4 under MaxBin 4 and its TEC refuses a set point outside -50 to +30 C as `InvalidValue`, as the SDK's `E_INVALIDARG` does (`test_touptek_camera.cpp`, `test_altair_camera.cpp`); `FakeZWOSDK` gains `conformu_camera()` (bins 1 to 4, a 192 x 96 sensor whose binned size passes the width alignment at every bin) and refuses a TargetTemperature outside its -40 to +30 C caps instead of caps of -500 to 300 (`test_zwo_fake_sdk.cpp`). `FakeToupTekSDK::real_time_exposure` raises a trigger's frame event after the exposure time instead of at once, and the ConformU ToupTek and Altair factories turn it and frame delivery on (`test_touptek_camera.cpp`). The ZWO, ToupTek and Altair camera rows are now `run` in `scripts/conformu_fake_runs.tsv`, each with a committed `Fake-camera-*` report under `AlpacaCore/conformu/<Vendor>/Fake/`; the Altair report directory is named in the workflow's `REPORT_DIRS`.
