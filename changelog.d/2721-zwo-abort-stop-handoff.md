### Fixed

- **ZWO camera: AbortExposure and StopExposure no longer wait out the SDK stop** (AlpacaCore, issue #2721). On a Raspberry Pi 5 with SDK 1.41, `ASIStopExposure` blocked for the rest of a short exposure plus about 0.4 s, up to 3.18 s on an ASI120MM Mini, and the client's call waited for all of it. The driver now cancels the exposure and the exposure body sends the SDK stop on its own thread, so the call returns within one 50 ms status poll step and CameraState reads Idle at once. A StartExposure sent while that stop still runs waits up to 2 s for it, then is refused with `InvalidOperation`; a failed stop is logged instead of raised. Disconnect still waits for the stop before it closes the camera, with Connected reading false at once. Covered by four fake-clock cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_camera_clock.cpp`; decision 0020 rule 5 and its ZWO row updated. Not checked on a camera.
