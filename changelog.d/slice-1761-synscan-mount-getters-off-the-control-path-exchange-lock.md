### Changed

- **SynScan getters no longer wait on the handset**, SynScan telescope driver, issues #1761 and #1828. The connect handshake and the disconnect close no longer run under the driver's state lock: `Tracking`, `GuideRate`, `AtPark` and `IsPulseGuiding` answer within 20 ms while a connect is in flight at 200 ms per reply (`test_synscan_telescope_snapshot.cpp`, "Tracking and GuideRate do not wait for a connect that is in flight" and "each no-I/O getter alone is in budget while a connect is in flight"), and `set_connected(false)` returns within 5 s with a status tick, or two pulse bodies and a tick, mid-exchange at 1.2 s per reply (the bound holds up to about 1.18 s per reply when an AbortSlew is also in flight, and a silent handset at the default 5 s timeout is outside it) (same file, "set_connected(false) returns within 5 s ..."). The control-path helpers (site and time reads and writes, tracking mode, park position, the slewing re-read) send with the lock released; PulseGuide sends its mode read and rate command in one wire turn, and SyncToCoordinates is now serialised with Park, slews, MoveAxis and AbortSlew (`test_synscan_initiator_supersede.cpp`). SiteLatitude and SiteLongitude writes made at the same time keep both values, and a UTCDate write cut by a disconnect reports NotConnected (same file, "concurrent SiteLatitude and SiteLongitude writes keep both values" and "a UTCDate write cut by a disconnect reports NotConnected"). The polled frame is served through `util::PolledState`. The fault text a latched link fault reports changed from "SynScan mount communications compromised: <the failing exchange's own error text>" (for example "Invalid response to SynScan slew-status query") to "SynScan mount communications compromised: no valid reply to the state poll (3 consecutive failures)"; the error code stays DriverException, and a malformed Park status reply now surfaces the stored Park error instead of the fault text (`test_synscan_async_park.cpp`, "malformed Park status reply persists through Slewing"); a good control-path read no longer clears a fault, the next good poll does. `Connected` reads false until the first frame is held at the end of a connect. A connect whose site read fails sends no second site read before it returns (`test_synscan_telescope_snapshot.cpp`, "a connect whose site read failed sends no second w in the same connect"). A status tick waits for the landing hunt to finish and makes no exchange while it holds the line; a tick held past 4.9 s fails without renewing the served frame, and `set_connected(false)` ends a waiting tick at once (`test_synscan_goto_landing.cpp`, "a status tick waits out the hunt ...", "a status tick held past its bound ...", "set_connected(false) ends a tick waiting on the hunt's hold at once").
