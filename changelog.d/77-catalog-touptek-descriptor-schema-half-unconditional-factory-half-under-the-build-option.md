### Changed
- **ToupTek camera, AAF focuser, AFW filter wheel and switch moved into the device catalog** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, issue #942): one schema (`touptek_schema.cpp`, every build) and one factory (`touptek_catalog.cpp`, only under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_TOUPTEK`, the StellaVita PowerBox only also under `ALPACACORE_TOUPTEK_STELLAVITA`) replace the router's four register arms, its sanitize branch and five driver includes; the "ToupTek support not enabled" and "ToupTek StellaVita switch not built" texts are unchanged (tests `Builtin catalog - register_builtin_factories makes the ToupTek devices available only when built`, `Builtin catalog - register_builtin_schemas describes the ToupTek devices in every build`). The switch is one descriptor that branches on `switchType`: an unknown value is still refused with "Unknown ToupTek switchType" (test `Builtin catalog - ToupTek switch normalize accepts thermal and stellavita and refuses others`), a StellaVita `gpioChip` other than `/dev/gpiochip0` is still refused with the "Hardware config refused" text (test `Builtin catalog - ToupTek stellavita gpioChip must be the board chip`), and non-object `ports[]` entries are still skipped. `GET /management/v1/devicecatalog` now lists the four `touptek` descriptors, and a saved ToupTek entry keeps every field its device type declares whatever its `switchType` (a thermal switch keeps a stale `gpioChip`, a camera drops a stale `focuserIndex`; test `Builtin catalog - ToupTek sanitize keeps the declared fields per device type`). A `filterNames` that is not a list of strings, and a `ports` that is not a list, are now refused with the catalog's wrong-type text (`ports` used to be ignored). With ToupTek support off, an unknown `switchType` now answers "Unknown ToupTek switchType" instead of "support not enabled". `scripts/check_layering.py`'s AlpacaHTTP vendor-include baseline drops from 25 to 20.
