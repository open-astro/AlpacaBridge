### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher pole and recovery edge cases**, Sky-Watcher driver, issue #520. A `SiteLatitude` write that crosses the equator while an East/West pulse holds the RA axis now turns the axis round into the new tracking direction instead of leaving it running the old way: the drive direction is decided again when the pulse releases the axis, so a crossing inside the pulse-end restore, its restart stop-wait, its post-restore rate check or after a pulse dispatch failure is applied (`SkyWatcher async - SiteLatitude crossing inside the post-restore rate check reverses RA`, `SkyWatcher async - SiteLatitude crossing inside the pulse-end restart stop-wait reverses RA`, `SkyWatcher async - SiteLatitude crossing the equator inside the pulse-end restore window reverses RA`, `SkyWatcher async - a pulse dispatch failure after the live :I restores the drive in the new hemisphere`). A goto aimed at a target within the aim-ahead of hour angle 0 now checks its landing on the branch it was dispatched on, so it no longer draws futile refinement re-gotos (`SkyWatcher pointing - a target inside the goto aim-ahead of HA=0 is not re-goto'd for the other branch`). `Tracking` no longer stays true once a poll shows the RA axis stopped after a `Tracking=false` whose stop-wait timed out (`SkyWatcher async - Tracking reads false once a Tracking=false stop-wait that threw sees RA stopped`).
