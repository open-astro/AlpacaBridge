### Fixed

- **Sky-Watcher pole and recovery edge cases**, Sky-Watcher driver, issue #520. A `SiteLatitude` write that crosses the equator while an East/West pulse holds the RA axis now turns the axis round into the new tracking direction instead of leaving it running the old way: the drive direction is decided again when the pulse releases the axis, so a crossing inside the pulse-end restore, its restart stop-wait, its post-restore rate check or after a pulse dispatch failure is applied (`SkyWatcher async - SiteLatitude crossing inside the post-restore rate check reverses RA`, `SkyWatcher async - SiteLatitude crossing inside the pulse-end restart stop-wait reverses RA`, `SkyWatcher async - SiteLatitude crossing the equator inside the pulse-end restore window reverses RA`, `SkyWatcher async - a pulse dispatch failure after the live :I restores the drive in the new hemisphere`). A goto aimed at a target within the aim-ahead of hour angle 0 now measures its distance and slew-time estimate on the dec-axis branch the aim-ahead lands on, not the branch at the current sidereal time (an RA error of 180 degrees that inflated the estimate and aimed the goto too far ahead), and checks its landing on that same branch, so it no longer draws futile refinement re-gotos (`SkyWatcher pointing - a target inside the goto aim-ahead of HA=0 is not re-goto'd for the other branch`). `Tracking` no longer stays true once a poll shows the RA axis stopped after a `Tracking=false` whose stop-wait timed out (`SkyWatcher async - Tracking reads false once a Tracking=false stop-wait that threw sees RA stopped`), and only until a later motion command on either axis takes over: a `Tracking` read while a later goto holds RA stopped no longer turns tracking off, which had ended the motion-limit guard while the slew went on to restore tracking with no limit enforced (`SkyWatcher limits - a slew after a Tracking=false stop that threw keeps the guard running`).
