### Added

- **Contract sweep tier 3, failure after start**, test suite, issue #1861. Every tier-2 host of a camera, telescope, focuser, rotator or filter wheel now has a recipe that starts an operation on a healthy fake, faults the fake, and requires the completion property (`ImageReady`, `Slewing`, `IsMoving` or `Position`) to raise a non-`NotConnected` error rather than report the operation done; after the fault is undone it must still not report done. The recipes for the ZWO, QHY, ToupTek, Altair, SVBONY, Player One and gphoto drivers join the Celestron, SynScan, OnStep, Sky-Watcher, iOptron, Astroasis, Gemini and WandererAstro ones; a guard case in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_contract_sweep.cpp` fails when a host of those types has no recipe. A recipe over a driver adopted on the task clock (decision 0005 rule 2) whose fault or recovery waits on a driver timer builds the driver on a `FakeTaskClock` and steps that clock, so those cases wait in real time for no driver poll, retry or deadline. The ZWO EAF and CAA fakes make `moving` atomic, and the ZWO EFW, EAF and CAA fakes make `fail_reads` atomic and throw `AlpacaException` as the real wrappers do; the ZWO camera fake gains `fail_every_exposure`; the QHY and gphoto fakes gain `fail_from()`/`clear_fail_from()`; the Player One wheel fake gains a persistent `set_fail_reads()`; the SVBONY fake gains `set_exposure_max()`; and the iOptron iEAF fake gains `set_focuser_moving()` and `set_silent()`.
