### Added (CI)
- **Issues missing a type, priority or size label get a reminder comment** (`.github/workflows/issue-labels.yml`, `scripts/check_issue_labels.py`, issue #1361): an open issue without a type (`bug`, `enhancement`, `documentation`, `question`), priority (`P1`-`P5`) or size (`XS`-`XL`) label gets one comment naming the missing groups, edited in place as labels change and deleted once all three are set. Nothing is labelled or closed; closed issues and issues opened by a bot (or labelled `dependencies` or `github_actions`) are skipped. The bug-report and feature-request templates now pre-fill `bug` and `enhancement`. The `docs-drift` job runs the script's `--self-test`.
