### Fixed
- **Celestron NexStar Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore, issue #2409): a GOTO that the hand controller reports done but that came to rest more than 10 arcmin from its target now fails. Slewing reports the failure and the mount is stopped, for async and blocking slews. A GOTO answered with anything other than `#` is no longer taken as started: the slew fails and the mount is stopped. AbortSlew now checks that the mount stopped, and so do a `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` that stops a GOTO and an Unpark that stops a Park: Slewing stays true until it has, the stop is sent again each second, three times in all, and Slewing then reports the failure if the mount still moves. A Connect whose status poll cannot start now fails instead of leaving a connected mount with no MoveAxis cap or link-loss check, and a reconnect stop the mount did not answer is still sent by the next Connect. A GOTO started while a site or time write is on its way now waits for the write instead of reaching the mount first. Tested on the fake mount only, not on hardware.

### Breaking changes
- **Celestron NexStar SyncToCoordinates is bounded to 5 degrees** (issue #2409): a Sync that would move the pointing more than 5 degrees on the sky from where the mount reports it is refused with InvalidValue, and nothing is sent. An aligned hand controller keeps its alignment across power cycles, so every one of its Syncs is bounded. A mount with no reference (no hand controller, or a hand controller that reports itself unaligned) cannot slew before a Sync, so its first Sync after each Connect is not bounded and sets the reference; every later Sync in that session is. The 5 degree limit is fixed, not a setting. **After upgrading:** if a Sync is refused, slew close to the target first (or re-align on the hand controller), then Sync.
- **Celestron NexStar AbortSlew keeps Slewing true until the mount is still** (issue #2409): after AbortSlew of a moving mount, a `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` during a GOTO or an Unpark during a Park, Slewing reads true for about half a second while the driver checks the mount stopped, and site and time writes are refused until then. **After upgrading:** a client that writes the site or time right after one of these stops should wait for Slewing to read false.
