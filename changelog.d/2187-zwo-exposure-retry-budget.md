### Fixed
- **ZWO camera: an exposure retry that would run past the client's wait is not started** (AlpacaCore, issue #2187): after a transient `ASI_EXP_FAILED` the driver re-triggered the exposure up to twice whatever its length, and each attempt is a full exposure plus the SDK's failure latency (1.9-2.3 s on an ASI120MM Mini for a 1 s frame), so a guide client that waits for the frame for the exposure plus 15 s (PHD2) could drop the camera before the driver reported anything (24 camera drops in one night on a shared USB 2.0 hub). A retry now starts only when it is expected to end within 10 s of the exposure's own end, the expectation being the length of the attempt that just failed; otherwise the failure is kept at once and `ImageReady` raises it, so the client retakes its frame. A 1 s guide exposure keeps both retries, a 5 s one keeps one, and a 10 s or longer exposure is reported failed at its first `ASI_EXP_FAILED` (see Breaking changes). Covered by "ZWO fake SDK - a retry that would run past the client's exposure wait is not started" and "ZWO fake SDK - a short exposure whose retries fit the client's wait keeps both of them" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_fake_sdk.cpp`.

### Breaking changes
- **ZWO camera: an exposure of about 10 s or longer is no longer retried after a transient SDK failure** (AlpacaCore, issue #2187): it used to be re-triggered up to twice, so a client saw a 300 s frame arrive up to 600 s late with no error; it now gets a `DriverException` from `ImageReady` and `ImageArray` at the first failure. **After upgrading:** nothing to set; a client that sees the failure retakes the frame. An ASI120 (non-S) on a shared USB 2.0 hub, the camera that fails most, takes a lower `usbBandwidth` ("USB Limit" in the web UI).
