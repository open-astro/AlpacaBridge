### Changed

- **ToupTek and Altair camera exposures run in a `util::CaptureSession`** (ToupTek camera driver, issue #1418; [decision 0020](docs/decisions/0020-capture-session.md)). The driver holds no exposure thread, lifecycle mutex, active flag or deadline of its own, so no raw `std::thread` is left in it. What a client sees:
  - A StartExposure while an exposure is in progress raises `InvalidOperation` and the first frame still arrives; it used to stop the running exposure and start a new one (test `a StartExposure during an exposure is refused and the first frame still arrives`).
  - A failed exposure is kept: `ImageReady` and `ImageArray` raise `DriverException` and `CameraState` reads Idle until the next StartExposure or a reconnect (test `a failed exposure is kept until the next StartExposure`). An exposure the 15 s watchdog ends raises the watchdog's message, `did not complete before its deadline`, even when its frame arrives later (test `a frame arriving after the watchdog fails both image readers`).
  - AbortExposure, StopExposure and Disconnect stop the SDK frame wait before they join the exposure (tests `AbortExposure during an exposure wakes the SDK wait and leaves the camera idle`, `a disconnect during an exposure stops the SDK wait before the close`). An exposure that fails stops the stream it started (test `an exposure body that fails stops the stream it started`), and a StartExposure wakes an exposure the watchdog left waiting in the SDK instead of refusing (test `a StartExposure wakes a body the watchdog left in the SDK wait`).
  - Unchanged: AbortExposure after a completed exposure keeps the pull-mode stream and clears a kept failure (issue #979), and an abort or disconnect during a stream restart is not undone.

  Covered by fake-SDK tests only; ConformU was not re-run.
