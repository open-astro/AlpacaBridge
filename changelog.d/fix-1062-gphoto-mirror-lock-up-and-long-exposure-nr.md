### Fixed

- **GPhoto: an exposure with mirror lock-up on no longer stalls for 60 s** (GPhoto, issue #1062): a Canon body with mirror lock-up on in its menu answers `gp_camera_capture` only after a 60 s timeout, so every exposure failed. The driver now follows the camera's own setting (it never writes it) and fires the shutter with two `eosremoterelease` press/release pairs with a settle between them, then waits for the file. The setting is read at exposure start from `mirrorlock` (EOS 70D), else the read-only `mirrorlockstatus` (EOS 250D); a body with neither keeps the single-capture path. A body that reports mirror lock-up on but has no press/release control now fails `StartExposure` with an error telling you to turn mirror lock-up off in the camera menu, instead of stalling. A camera that left the USB bus (auto power-off) while the mirror lock-up setting is read at `StartExposure` is reported as disconnected (`NotConnected`), as it already was mid-exposure, pinned by `GPhoto camera fake - MLU read finding the camera gone from the USB bus reports a disconnect`. New field `mirrorLockupSettleMs` (500 to 10000 ms, default 2000), with an input on the DSLR / Mirrorless form. The driver was run on a rig with an EOS 70D; the EOS 250D is proven only by the gphoto2 command line bench probe, and both are covered by the fake SDK. If the mirror lock-up state cannot be read at connect, the connect skips the priming capture and still succeeds, pinned by `GPhoto camera fake - an unreadable MLU state at connect skips the priming capture quietly`. Pinned in `test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp` by `GPhoto camera fake - MLU on runs press release settle press release on the TaskClock`, `GPhoto camera fake - MLU on bulb holds the second press for the exposure`, `GPhoto camera fake - MLU on without a usable eosremoterelease refuses StartExposure`, `GPhoto camera fake - the MLU exposure deadline includes the settle and the file wait` and `GPhoto camera fake - an abort during the MLU settle still fires the second press and release`.

### Added

- **GPhoto: a long-exposure noise reduction setting** (GPhoto, issue #1062): the new `longExposureNR` field (`camera`, `off` or `on`, default `camera`) writes the Nikon `longexpnr` widget once at connect when it is `off` or `on`, and writes nothing for `camera`. A body without the widget (every Canon tested) logs one warning, "long-exposure NR is not settable on this camera; set it in the camera menu", and still connects. Nikon support comes from the libgphoto2 source and is tested on the fake only. Pinned in `test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp` by `GPhoto camera fake - longExposureNR writes longexpnr once at connect`, `GPhoto camera fake - longExposureNR camera writes nothing` `GPhoto camera fake - longExposureNR on a body without the widget warns once and connects`, `GPhoto camera fake - longExposureNR with no matching choice warns and writes nothing` (the body lists no On/Off choice) and `GPhoto camera fake - a camera gone from the USB bus during the longexpnr write fails the connect`; the form input is pinned by `AlpacaHTTP/tests/web/gphoto_form.test.js`.
