### Fixed

- **Gemini and Celestron focuser link loss**, Gemini focuser and Celestron focus motor drivers, issue #1643. A latched poll fault that stands for 30 s (`util::kLinkStalenessBound`) now drops `Connected`, makes operations throw `NotConnected`, empties `DeviceState` and closes the port; before, `Connected` stayed true on a dead link and every getter threw `DriverException` for as long as the fault stood. Nothing reconnects until the client asks, and the next Connect runs the first poll again. Both drivers now hold their state in `util::PolledState` ([decision 0009](docs/decisions/0009-link-loss-and-relink-policy.md)). Covered by "Gemini Focuser Snapshot - a fault that stands past the link-loss bound drops Connected" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_gemini_focuser_snapshot.cpp` and "Celestron Focuser Driver - a fault that stands past the link-loss bound drops Connected" in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_celestron_focuser.cpp`.
