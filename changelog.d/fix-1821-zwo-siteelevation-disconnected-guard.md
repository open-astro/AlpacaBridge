### Fixed

- **ZWO mount SiteElevation reads NotConnected while disconnected**, ZWO telescope driver, issue #1821. `SiteElevation` returned the stored elevation while the mount was not connected; ITelescopeV4 lists `NotConnectedException` for it, and `SiteLatitude` and `SiteLongitude` already threw it. A read before the first Connect, after a Disconnect or after a lost link now throws `NotConnected`, checked before the never-set `InvalidOperation` of decision 0019 (tests "ZWO Mount Telescope Driver - SiteElevation reads NotConnected while disconnected" and "... SiteElevation write and read are NotConnected after a lost link"). A write while the link is lost now throws `NotConnected` too (decision 0009 rule 3, as the latitude and longitude setters do) and is not applied by the next Connect. A write before the first Connect is still accepted and applied by that Connect. A client that read `SiteElevation` before connecting now gets an error instead of the value. FakeZwoMount evidence only; not checked on the rig.
