### Fixed

- **ZWO mount SiteElevation reads NotConnected while disconnected**, ZWO telescope driver, issue #1821. `SiteElevation` returned the stored elevation while the mount was not connected; ITelescopeV4 lists `NotConnectedException` for it, and `SiteLatitude` and `SiteLongitude` already threw it. A read before the first Connect or after a Disconnect now throws `NotConnected`, checked before the never-set `InvalidOperation` of decision 0019 (test "ZWO Mount Telescope Driver - SiteElevation reads NotConnected while disconnected"). A write while disconnected is still accepted and applied on the next Connect, which then reads it back. A client that read `SiteElevation` before connecting now gets an error instead of the value. FakeZwoMount evidence only; not checked on the rig.
