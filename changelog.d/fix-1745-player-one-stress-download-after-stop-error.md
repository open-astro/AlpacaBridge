### Fixed

- **Player One no longer reports a download error after a disconnect or AbortExposure ends an exposure** (Player One camera, issue #1745). The SDK stop makes the body's download fail with `POA_ERROR_OPERATION_FAILED`; the body could store that as a failure before the driver's abort took ownership, so `ImageReady` and `ImageArray` raised it. The driver now calls `CaptureSession::set_stopping(true)` before the SDK stop, so a body failure that lands while the stop is in flight is dropped instead of kept; a stop that fails calls `set_stopping(false)` and the exposure runs on, and a download failure nobody caused is still kept. Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_capture_session.cpp` ("a throw after set_stopping is dropped, and set_stopping(false) hands ownership back"), `AlpacaCore/tests/test_playerone_camera.cpp` ("A slow failing AbortExposure stop leaves the exposure running", "A download failure after a failed AbortExposure stop is kept", "Download SDK exceptions are reported and recover").
