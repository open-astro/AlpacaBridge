### Changed (tests)
- **Sky-Watcher: the stop-confirm, landing-settle, slew-complete, post-slew rate-check and homing waits and their deadlines run on the injected task clock** (open-astro#743 part 2): the telescope driver no longer calls `std::this_thread::sleep_for`, and the slew-timing state (`slew_force_until_`, `last_goto_dispatch_time_`, `last_landing_time_`, `last_slewing_poll_`) reads the same clock, so a fake-clock test drives them with `advance()`; `test_skywatcher_async.cpp` gains fake-clock cases for the axis stop-confirm (5 s), connect stop-confirm (2 s), slew-complete grace (2 s) and timeout (180 s) and AutoHome (300 s) deadlines, and nearly every other case moves off real sleeps (56 to 6; the slew-failure suite 3 to 0).
