### Fixed

- **ToupTek AAF focuser and thermal switch getters no longer wait on device I/O**, ToupTek driver, issue #1391. `Name`, `MaxStep`, `MaxIncrement`, `Connected`, `UniqueID`, `MaxSwitch` and `GetSwitchName` return within 20 ms while a status refill, a heater or fan read or a connect is in flight (the status refill and the connect handshake now run under a per-link I/O lock, with `mutex_` a short state lock), and `Connected=false` still waits for the exchange in flight before it closes the handle. The thermal switch now serves heater, fan and tail-light reads from a 100 ms status cache (reads inside the TTL cost no second `get_heat`, a write drops the frame, and three failed refills latch "communications compromised"; the three cases at the end of `AlpacaCore/tests/test_touptek_fake_sdk.cpp`). Covered by the `[io-lock]` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_touptek_focuser.cpp` and `AlpacaCore/tests/test_touptek_fake_sdk.cpp`; the rule is [decision 0016](docs/decisions/0016-getters-never-wait-on-device-io.md). A focuser `MoveAbsolute` racing a disconnect or reconnect answers `NotConnected`, not "position out of range" (`[stress]` case "ToupTek AAF focuser - concurrent connect/disconnect/operate stress"; sequential guard in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_touptek_focuser.cpp`).
