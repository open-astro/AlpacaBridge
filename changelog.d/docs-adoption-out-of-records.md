### Changed (docs)

- **Adoption rosters move out of the decision records** (docs, `docs/adoption/`): the `## Adoption` tables of records 0005, 0006, 0009, 0010, 0014, 0015, 0016, 0017, 0020, 0022 and 0027 move unchanged to `docs/adoption/<record name>`, and each record links its roster. A PR that adds or changes an Adoption row no longer touches `docs/decisions/`, so it needs no `ADR authorized` comment. `scripts/check_docs_drift.py` check 23 reads the rosters from the new folder, fails a record that still carries `## Adoption`, fails any file in that folder with no record of the same name or whose record is retired, and raises its roster floor from 6 to 11; `scripts/check_contract_sweep.py` reads the decision 0010 roster from its new path, and `scripts/check_instruction_structure.py` (relative links) and check 7 (path spans) scan `docs/adoption/`.
