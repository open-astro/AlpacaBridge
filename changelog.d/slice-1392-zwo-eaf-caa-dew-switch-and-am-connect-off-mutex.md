### Fixed

- **ZWO EAF, CAA, dew heater switch and AM mount: Name, UniqueID and MaxStep no longer wait on the device**, ZWO drivers, issue #1392 (part of #295). `set_connected(true)` used to hold the driver `mutex_` across the SDK open, the info reads and, on the AM mount, the whole serial handshake, so a client that polled `Name` or `UniqueID` during a connect stalled for the length of it. Connect now runs under the transition lock and the per-link I/O lock, `mutex_` is a short state lock, and identity and limits are published at the end of connect. `move`, `halt`, `SetReverse` and the heater write now hold the I/O lock across the SDK call, and a disconnect waits for the exchange in flight instead of closing the handle under it. The AM mount's site latitude and longitude writes no longer hold `mutex_` across the serial write, and its getters report `NotConnected` until the connect finishes, as `Connected` does. A site write that lands during the connect's site read-back is kept, because the connect's site apply and re-send hold the same write lock as the setters (`test_zwo_telescope.cpp`, "A site write during the connect site read-back is kept"). A guide rate write during the connect's guide-rate read is kept too (`test_zwo_telescope.cpp`, "A guide rate write during the connect read-back is kept"), and a disconnect that wins the I/O lock during an EAF, CAA or dew heater status refill no longer counts toward link health. Covered by the `[getter-stall]` cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_focuser.cpp`, `test_zwo_rotator.cpp`, `test_zwo_switch.cpp` and `test_zwo_telescope.cpp`; the rule is [decision 0016](docs/decisions/0016-getters-never-wait-on-device-io.md).
