### Changed

- **Player One camera exposure runs in a capture session**, Player One camera driver, issue #1605. The exposure thread, lifecycle mutex and deadline are replaced by `util::CaptureSession`, and the pulse-guide off-timer runs in an `AsyncOperation` slot. A failed exposure now keeps its message: `ImageReady` and `ImageArray` raise it as `DriverException` until the next `StartExposure` or a reconnect, and `CameraState` reads Idle. While the frame is read out `CameraState` is Download, and `PercentCompleted` raises `InvalidOperation` there. A body that is cancelled or fails stops the SDK exposure it started, `StartExposure` rejects NaN, infinity and huge durations before the session sees them, and a disconnect that leaves the camera connected keeps the session open, the Ready frame and `LastExposure*` ("a refused disconnect keeps the Ready frame and LastExposure"), and one that fails after the SDK exposure was stopped cancels the exposure body, so the camera is idle again, not Exposing ("a refused disconnect after a stopped exposure leaves the camera idle"). `ImageArray` before any exposure is again an asked-too-early `InvalidOperation` ("ImageArray before any exposure is asked too early"), and the exposure settings stay locked while a cancelled body is still configuring the camera ("settings stay locked while a cancelled body is still in setup"). Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_playerone_camera.cpp` ("AbortExposure wakes the readiness poll without a clock advance", "a cancelled exposure body stops the SDK exposure", "StartExposure rejects NaN, infinity and a huge Duration before the session", "a disconnect that leaves the camera connected leaves the session open"); the rules are [decision 0020](docs/decisions/0020-capture-session.md).
