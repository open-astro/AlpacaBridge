### Changed

- **Player One camera exposure runs in a capture session**, Player One camera driver, issue #1605. The exposure thread, lifecycle mutex and deadline are replaced by `util::CaptureSession`, and the pulse-guide off-timer runs in an `AsyncOperation` slot. A failed exposure now keeps its message: `ImageReady` and `ImageArray` raise it as `DriverException` until the next `StartExposure` or a reconnect, and `CameraState` reads Idle. While the frame is read out `CameraState` is Download, and `PercentCompleted` raises `InvalidOperation` there. A body that is cancelled or fails stops the SDK exposure it started, `StartExposure` rejects NaN, infinity and huge durations before the session sees them, and a disconnect that leaves the camera connected keeps the session open. `ImageArray` before any exposure is again an asked-too-early `InvalidOperation` ("ImageArray before any exposure is asked too early"), and the exposure settings stay locked while a cancelled body is still configuring the camera ("settings stay locked while a cancelled body is still in setup"). Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_playerone_camera.cpp` ("AbortExposure wakes the readiness poll without a clock advance", "a cancelled exposure body stops the SDK exposure", "StartExposure rejects NaN, infinity and a huge Duration before the session", "a disconnect that leaves the camera connected leaves the session open"); the rules are [decision 0020](docs/decisions/0020-capture-session.md).
