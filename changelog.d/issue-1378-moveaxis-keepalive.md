### Fixed

- **OnStep: a `MoveAxis` no longer stops by itself after 10 s** (issue #1378): OnStepX ends a manual move after its guide time limit (10 s by default), while `Slewing` kept reading true. The driver now re-sends a running move every 7 s until the client stops it. It re-sends only while the mount shows the move (`:GU#` `g` on OnStepX), so a move the firmware let lapse or stopped stays ended and `Slewing` then reads false, and never within 1 degree of the horizon or overhead limit, which OnStepX enforces only on the crossing. Firmware without `g` gets no re-send. The OnStep driver's timers (caches, slew grace, pulse guide, the re-send) now run on the task clock (decision 0005), so its MoveAxis and tracking tests advance a fake clock instead of sleeping. The UTCDate readback uses the shared `util::UtcOffset` (decision 0014), now also cleared on disconnect. Tests: `test_onstep_telescope.cpp` "OnStep Telescope Driver - a running MoveAxis is re-sent until it is stopped", "OnStep Telescope Driver - Slewing drops when OnStepX ends a MoveAxis by itself", "OnStep Telescope Driver - the MoveAxis re-send never restarts a move the mount ended", "OnStep Telescope Driver - a pulse guide does not end a running MoveAxis", "OnStep Telescope Driver - a Tracking write right after MoveAxis does not end the move", "OnStep Telescope Driver - the MoveAxis re-send stays clear of the altitude limits", "OnStep Telescope Driver - a MoveAxis on one axis does not delay the other axis's re-send", "OnStep Telescope Driver - a MoveAxis the mount does not show is never re-sent" and "OnStep Telescope Driver - a written UTCDate reads back advanced by the task clock and is dropped on reconnect".
