### Fixed

- **Bisque (TheSkyX) Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore, issue #2410): the rest of the motion-safety items. Fake-only evidence; not tested on hardware.
  - A Park that times out at 120 s now sends a verified `Abort()` before it fails, and Slewing raises the failure.
  - A stop that failed after a synchronous slew timeout, an unconfirmed `SlewToRaDec` or a Park timeout is sent again by the status poll 10 s later, not only at the 300 s slew bound.
  - A slew is over only once the mount is still after TheSkyX reports it complete (two position reads 500 ms apart that agree). A mount that comes to rest more than 600 arcsec from its target fails the slew: SlewToCoordinates throws, and Slewing raises it after an async slew.
  - A MoveAxis failure during a SlewToCoordinates wait (a 60 s cap stop) no longer fails that slew; Slewing still raises it.
  - TheSkyX refusals are told apart from a reply that never came by the exception's type, not by its message text.

### Breaking changes

- **Bisque (TheSkyX) Telescope Driver** (AlpacaCore, issue #2410): SyncToCoordinates is refused with InvalidValue when the sync would move the pointing more than 5 degrees on the sky from where the mount reports it is. TheSkyX keeps its pointing model at power-up, so every Sync is bounded, the first one after a connect included. A slew that comes to rest more than 600 arcsec from its target now fails, and Slewing stays true about one status poll longer after an async slew while the landing is checked. **After upgrading:** a client that syncs a mount that is lost by more than 5 degrees must slew near the object first, or re-home the mount in TheSkyX.
