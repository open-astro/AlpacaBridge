### Changed

- **Shared mount motion model and the iOptron mount in the fake ConformU server**, test fakes, issue #2614 (refs #2078). `AlpacaCore/tests/fake_mount_motion_model.h` adds `FakeMountMotionModel`, a protocol-neutral equatorial mount (GOTO over its travel time, tracking on and off, MoveAxis, timed pulses, park, home, side of pier from the hour angle, Alt/Az; `test_fake_mount_motion_model.cpp`), for the ZWO AM, Celestron, SynScan and OnStep fakes to reuse. `FakeIoptronMount` gains a ConformU mode, `FakeIoptronMount(model_code, FakeMountMotionModel::Config)`, that answers `:GEP#`, `:GAC#` and `:GLS#` and the motion commands from the model: a GOTO reads status 2 until its travel time has passed (and is refused below the horizon), `:GLS#` reads 6 after `:MP1#` lands, tracking follows `:ST1#` and `:ST0#`, and the arrows and `:Z` pulses move the axes, the Dec ones in the sense each pier side needs, with `:MH#` reading status 7, `:SLO`, `:SLA`, `:SUT` and `:GUT` round-tripping, and `:CM#` and `:Q#` answered (`test_ioptron_fake_motion_mode.cpp`); the scripted default and every existing iOptron case are unchanged. The `ioptron / telescope / fake_ioptron_mount.h` row is now `run` in `scripts/conformu_fake_runs.tsv`, hosted by `conformu_telescope_factories.h` with the made-up Sydney Opera House site, and `conformu-fake.yml` lists the new headers in its paths filter and ties them to the iOptron row.
