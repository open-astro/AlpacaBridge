### Added

- **iOptron iFR focuser-rotator (2", 3", 4")** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, issue #2002): the iFR is one unit with two Type-C ports, either of which controls both halves; AlpacaBridge drives it as two ASCOM devices, an `IFocuserV4` Focuser and an `IRotatorV4` Rotator, over ONE serial session. The second device to connect joins the session the first opened (`IfrLink::acquire()`, one `util::SerialLink` under the owner name "iOptron iFR"), each connects and disconnects on its own, and the port closes when both have let go; a pulled cable loses the link for both at once, and a unit that stops answering with the cable in latches a fault (reads and writes throw `DriverException`, `Connected` stays true) that becomes a lost link for both once it has stood 30 s (decision 0009). New web UI vendor "iOptron iFR" (catalog key `ifr`) with Focuser and Rotator, a Model selector (iFR 2"/3"/4", which names the unit and sets the focuser travel: 62,500 steps of 0.4 um on the 2"/3", 65,000 on the 4") and Auto-Detect (`ifrIndex`) or Serial (`portPath`). The focuser reports position, temperature and `StepSize` 0.4 um and has no temperature compensation; a move the firmware refuses (`0` ack) at an end stop is a `DriverException`, and a move to the current position is a successful no-op. The rotator's Sync is stored in the unit and survives a power cycle (no `rotator_sync_offsets.json` entry), `MechanicalPosition` is the physical angle whether or not Reverse is on, and a Sync while reversed is made in the normal frame because the firmware's own reversed Sync stores the wrong offset. Both hold `IsMoving` true from a move's ack until the motor is seen running or at its target (`util::HoldWindow`). Protocol summary from rig captures on an iFR 3", INDI `iafscaa.cpp` and iOptron Commander 10.0.2: `AlpacaCore/external/iOptron/iFR-protocol.md`. Only the iFR 3" is validated.
