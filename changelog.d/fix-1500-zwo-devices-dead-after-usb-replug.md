### Fixed

- **A ZWO EAF focuser unplugged and plugged back in works again after one Connect**, ZWO EAF driver, issue #1500. `EAF_ERROR_REMOVED`, `EAF_ERROR_CLOSED`, a BLE disconnect or `EAF_ERROR_INVALID_ID` on a device call used to count as one failed poll, so `Connected` stayed true and three of them latched "communications compromised"; one such error now makes `Connected` read false and every member throw `NotConnected` ("ZWO EAF <serial> was removed from USB; Connect again when it is back"), also when it comes from the temperature read while `Position` still answers (tests "A focuser removed from USB reads Connected false", "A read on an id the SDK no longer holds is a lost link", "A removed unit seen by an optional read is a lost link"). The loss is logged once at WARN with the serial (control characters replaced), the last position and the max step ("A lost link is logged once with its serial escaped"). A Connect after the replug finds the focuser under its new SDK id by the serial read at the first connect, kept in memory, not in the configuration; it never binds another unit, also when two focusers swap ids ("A replugged focuser is reopened by one Connect", "Two focusers swapped while disconnected are told apart by serial"), and refuses with `NotConnected` naming the recovery when the unit is not there ("A held focuser with no serial is not shared after a swap"). A Disconnect of a removed focuser no longer throws ("A Connected toggle across a replug reopens the focuser", "Disconnect after a removal does not throw"). Two drivers holding one SDK id share one open, and a driver whose id now names another unit gets `NotConnected` instead of driving it ("An idle focuser does not drive the unit that took its id"; the shared open bookkeeping is `vendor::zwo::SharedOpenTable`, covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_shared_open_table.cpp`). A generic read error still latches the fault after three polls as before, and the 30 s staleness bound of decision 0009 is not applied to the EAF yet. Refs #1500; remaining: EAF staleness bound, camera and dew heater, EFW and CAA. Fake SDK evidence only; not yet checked on the rig.
