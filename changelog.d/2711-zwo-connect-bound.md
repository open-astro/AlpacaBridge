### Fixed

- **ZWO mount network Connect ends within 7 s and says why**, ZWO AM mounts, issue #2711. A Connect to a mount at an explicit host that never answers (powered off, wrong address, off the network) used to wait the kernel's TCP timeout, about 127 s, holding the wrapper lock, and failed with no reason. The host name lookup and the TCP connect now end within 7000 ms, a Disconnect ends them early, and the failure reads "Failed to connect to ZWO mount: could not connect to <host>:<port>: no answer within 7000 ms" (or "host not found", or the system's reason). The Auto-detect Wi-Fi probe and the OnStep network connect use the same bounded connect (`util::connect_tcp`, `AlpacaCore/include/alpacacore/util/tcp_connect.h`). Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_tcp_connect.cpp` and `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_mount_network_isolation.cpp`.
