### Changed

- **The OnStep, Bisque and NexStar 60 s MoveAxis cap runs on the shared motion guard** (issue #2651, second part; ZWO AM follows): each axis has a slot body, `util::run_motion_deadline()`, and the three drivers use `util::kMaxMoveAxisDuration` as their 60 s. OnStep's cap no longer rides on the re-send body, so it still stops an axis on firmware that sends no `g`. A cap stop the mount ignores is sent three times, then Slewing raises `util::kStopNotVerifiedText` and nothing more goes out for that move; OnStep and NexStar verify each axis alone, Bisque's `DoCommand(10)` verifies both. Tests: the `[goto-safety]` MoveAxis cap cases in `test_onstep_telescope.cpp`, `test_bisque_telescope.cpp` and `test_celestron_async_slew_failure.cpp`.

### Fixed

- **A Celestron NexStar FindHome over a MoveAxis no longer leaves Slewing true** (issue #2651): FindHome clears the jog flags the home took from the jog, so Slewing reads false once the home lands and the cap sends no stop at the jog's 60 s. Test: "Celestron MoveAxis - a FindHome over a jog reads not slewing once home and leaves no cap".
- **An older MoveAxis whose cap starts last no longer shortens or removes the newer jog's cap on OnStep and Bisque** (issue #2651): two MoveAxis calls on one axis start their cap bodies after the locks are released, and the older call's start replaced the newer body. OnStep stopped the newer jog at the older call's 60 s; Bisque's replacement body owned nothing, so the newer jog ran with no cap. Each axis's start now takes a start lock and uses the axis's latest window. Tests: "OnStep Telescope Driver - an older MoveAxis whose cap starts last leaves the newer jog its own 60 s" and "Bisque MoveAxis - an older MoveAxis whose cap starts last leaves the newer jog its own 60 s". NexStar holds `initiator_mutex_` across the start, so it has no such window.
