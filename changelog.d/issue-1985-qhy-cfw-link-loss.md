### Fixed

- **A QHY integrated filter wheel whose camera reports it removed now reads `Connected` false** (QHY, issue #1985): `QHYCCD_ERROR_DEVICELOSE` from the wheel's status read or move drops `Connected` at once, `Position` and moves answer `NotConnected` instead of `-1` or a generic driver error, `get_link_fault()` keeps the reason until the next Connect or Disconnect, and `Connected=true` reconnects without a Disconnect first; the wheel releases only its own reference on the camera's shared USB handle, so a paired camera stays connected. A wheel at rest serves `Position` from its cache, so an unplug is noticed at the next move.
