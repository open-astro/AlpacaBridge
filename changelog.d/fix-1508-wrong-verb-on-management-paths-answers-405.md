### Fixed
- **A wrong verb on an Alpaca-defined management path now answers HTTP 400** (AlpacaHTTP router, issue #1508): `PUT`, `POST` or `DELETE` on `/management/apiversions`, `/management/v1/configureddevices` (and their unversioned aliases), and `DELETE` on `/management/v1/description`, used to answer 405. They now answer 400 with a `text/plain` message, as the Alpaca API Reference (section 2.5) says for a method a path does not define. AlpacaBridge-only management paths such as `faileddevices` keep 405. Covered by `test_routing` ("a verb other than GET on an Alpaca-defined management path is 400 + text/plain (#1508)").
