### Fixed

- **`CameraState` and `PercentCompleted` on a disconnected camera**, ZWO, QHY, SVBONY, ToupTek (and Altair), Player One and GPhoto camera drivers, issue #1334. Both properties returned Idle and 0 with `ErrorNumber` 0 while no camera was connected, so a client polling before connect saw an idle camera that was not there; they now throw `NotConnected` (0x407) like every other camera property. `DeviceState` on a disconnected camera still returns the empty list. Covered by the `get_camera_state` and `get_percent_completed` probes in the tier-1 contract sweep (`AlpacaCore/tests/test_contract_sweep.cpp`, `not_connected_probes`) and by the disconnected-state cases in `test_zwo_camera.cpp`, `test_svbony_camera.cpp`, `test_gphoto_camera.cpp`, `test_touptek_camera.cpp`, `test_qhy_camera.cpp`, `test_playerone_camera.cpp` and `test_altair_camera.cpp`, and, for a GPhoto camera that left the USB bus mid-exposure and disconnected itself, by the camera-gone case in `test_gphoto_fake_sdk.cpp`.
