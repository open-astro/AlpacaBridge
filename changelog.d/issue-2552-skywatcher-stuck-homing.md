### Fixed

- **A MoveAxis during a Sky-Watcher FindHome no longer hides the home's later failure**, Sky-Watcher driver, issue #2552. A MoveAxis takes no slew claim, so it does not supersede a FindHome, yet it started a new failure epoch, and a FindHome that then failed returned before it dropped its flag: `Slewing` read true with nothing moving until a GoTo or a reconnect (AbortSlew did not clear it), the failure was lost and the failure stop never ran. The Park and FindHome failure path now ignores the epoch, so it stops both axes, drops the flag and keeps its error (a Park was not affected: MoveAxis is refused while parking). The rate-0 MoveAxis on an axis with no jog commands nothing and no longer starts an epoch either, so a goto that times out after one also stops the axes and keeps its error. A MoveAxis after a failure still clears it (#575). Introduced in the 6.0 cycle. Covered by three new cases in `AlpacaCore/tests/test_skywatcher_async_slew_failure.cpp`.
