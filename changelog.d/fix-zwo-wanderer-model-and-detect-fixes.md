### Fixed

- **A device is no longer refused because its model is missing from a list** (issue #2204): the ZWO AM3N connects and is found by Auto, and an unlisted model of the ZWO AM mount, iOptron iEAF, iEFW and iFR, Gemini power hub, WandererAstro Cover, Rotator and Box, or Celestron CGX direct-USB family connects under a generic name with a warning, unvalidated. A reply that positively names another product is still refused, and the ZWO `:GMA#` serial check now does that job. A Celestron motor of an unknown model, and a WandererRotator other than the Mini, connect with motion refused, since the step scaling is that of the known model.
- **WandererRotator Auto finds the rotator on the first open after power-up** (issue #2203): the probe now allows the same handshake window as a manual connect.
- **ZWO AM Slewing no longer reads True for 2 s after an Unpark that does not move the mount** (issue #2178), which failed ConformU runs started from park.
- **Registering a ZWO camera no longer opens any camera** (issue #2074): the first connect learns the serial and model name and stores them in the entry. An entry with no serial or model name now reports what is attached in its connect refusal, not as a registration warning.
