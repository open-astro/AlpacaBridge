### Breaking changes

- **Celestron NexStar: a PulseGuide during a slew or FindHome is refused** (AlpacaCore, issue #2263): it answers `InvalidOperation` ("PulseGuide is not allowed while a slew, park or home is running") and changes nothing, where it used to be accepted and its guide pulse ran under the GOTO or the home. A PulseGuide during a Park was already refused (`InvalidWhileParked`) and still is. The refusal lasts while the driver reports the slew, Park or FindHome as slewing, a failed or deferred stop after one included. **After upgrading:** a client waits for Slewing to read false before it pulse-guides, as ConformU and the guiding programs already do.

### Changed

- **Celestron NexStar: a new motion command no longer waits for a running pulse guide** (AlpacaCore, issue #2263): a moving MoveAxis takes its axis from a PulseGuide on the same axis, a second PulseGuide takes the axis from the older one, and a slew, Park or FindHome takes both, at once and without joining the older pulse's background body (decision 0006 rule 3). The older pulse sends that axis no further guide chunk, and IsPulseGuiding follows the newer command. A slew or Park that is refused (an unaligned mount, a failed pier read) leaves a running pulse and IsPulseGuiding alone. A pulse on the other axis still runs on, and AbortSlew, MoveAxis(axis, 0) and disconnect stop a pulse as before. The driver-wide motion counter is replaced by the per-axis motion owner (`util::MotionOwner`).
