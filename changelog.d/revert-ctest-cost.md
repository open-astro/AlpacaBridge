### Changed (tooling)
- **The all-vendors CI job runs its tests in the order they were added again** (CI, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `run_all_tests.sh`): the unreleased slowest-first ordering (`CTEST_COST_DATA`, `scripts/ctest_cost.py`, `AlpacaCore/cmake/CTestCost.cmake`) is removed. Over 12 `build-vendors` runs with the ordering, ctest took 693 to 704 s against 678 to 686 s over the 19 runs before it, and 4 of the 12 went red on timing-sensitive cases (gphoto hook bound, Celestron CGX tier 3, SynScan Park status, a Sky-Watcher pulse race) against 6 of 79 before: starting every slow, thread-heavy case at once slowed the rest by more than it saved and starved the cases that measure time.
