### Fixed

- **CGX GOTO going the wrong way is stopped**, Celestron CGX driver, issue #2640. The mount controller does not always move an axis the shorter way to its GOTO target: on the rig it drove Dec the long way, over the pole, to a correct Normal-side target south of the equator. Every GOTO leg (the first fast leg, the GOTO after a meridian home route, and each slow correction) now reads the moving axes on each poll. An axis that moves more than 2000 encoder counts (about 0.04 degrees) farther from its target than it started is stopped at once, and the slew fails with `DriverException`. This stops the motion; it does not yet make those targets reachable. Fake-only; the rig checks are pending.
