### Changed

- **The ASCOM not-implemented answers live once, in `AlpacaDriver`**, AlpacaCore, issue #1757. `get_supported_actions`, `action`, `can_action`, `command_blind`, `command_bool` and `command_string` now have inline defaults: no actions, `ActionNotImplemented` naming the action, and `MethodNotImplemented` (0x400) for `Command*`. The 39 driver files (and the power-port switch template) that only restated those answers drop their copies, 710 lines from `AlpacaCore/src`. The drivers with real actions or a `Command*` pass-through keep theirs. No error code changes. Some message texts now match the default: the ZWO, Player One and ToupTek switch drivers, the power-port switches and WeeWX say "Command not supported" for `Command*`, and WeeWX names the action it refuses.
