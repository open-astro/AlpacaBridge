### Changed

- **ZWO mount: GOTO setup, pulse guiding and disconnect run in operation slots**, ZWO telescope driver, issue #1918. A second `SlewToTargetAsync` now returns at once while the first GOTO's setup waits for a mount reply, instead of waiting for that reply (`ZWO async - a second GOTO returns while the first body is blocked in a mount transaction`). Park, FindHome, MoveAxis, AbortSlew, Sync and Unpark no longer wait for the old GOTO setup to finish: they take its ownership and wait only for the one mount call already counted, then send their own command, and nothing the old GOTO would send follows it (`ZWO async - a fence waits for the GOTO call the send gate has counted`, `ZWO async - Park or a jog during a held GOTO target write waits one exchange and sends after it (PIN)`). A pulse-guide step is one wait on the task clock, so a slew or a disconnect ends it at once (`ZWO telescope clock - a pulse step is one clock wait, so one advance ends it`), and a pulse after a reconnect reaches the mount (`ZWO telescope clock - a pulse after a reconnect reaches the mount`). A disconnect still sends no stop, so an accepted GOTO keeps running after `Connected=false` (`ZWO async - Disconnect during a slew sends no stop and leaves the mount slewing (PIN)`). Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_async_slew_failure.cpp` and `AlpacaCore/tests/test_zwo_telescope_clock.cpp` on a fake mount; ConformU not re-run. The rules are [decision 0006](docs/decisions/0006-async-operation-ownership.md).
