### Fixed

- **A TCP hangup loses the mount link at once** (iOptron, Celestron NexStar and SynScan Wi-Fi, decision 0009 rule 2a, issue #2718): the controller closing or resetting the connection (EOF, `ECONNRESET`, `EPIPE`, `ENOTCONN`, `EBADF`, `ENOTSOCK`) on a reply read, a send, the iOptron blind-command ack drain or its TCP flush now releases the socket and answers `NotConnected`, `Connected` reads false and `DeviceState` is empty in the same poll tick, as over serial. Before, the fault stood for `util::kLinkStalenessBound` (30 s) first. A reply timeout, `EHOSTUNREACH` and `ENETUNREACH` still only fail the exchange for the fault latch. One errno list serves a read and a send: `util::socket_hangup_errno()` (`EBADF` is now on it). Tests: `AlpacaCore/tests/test_mount_tcp_hangup.cpp` (per mount: the poll after a reset, a hangup while a command waits, a send on a reset connection, a timeout that stays a failed exchange) and `test_serial_read.cpp` (the errno list).
- The decision 0009 Adoption rows for these three mounts stay `partial` now only because of #2717 (no `LinkLostStamp`), the same as the WandererAstro rows.
