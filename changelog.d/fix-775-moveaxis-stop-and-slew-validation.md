### Fixed
- **ZWO `MoveAxis(axis, 0)` no longer stops the other axis** (issue #775, ZWO telescope): the stop sends only that axis's directional stops (`:Qe`/`:Qw` or `:Qn`/`:Qs`) instead of `:Q` while the other axis is still jogging (the generic `:Q` is sent once the other axis is idle), so `Slewing` stays true until both axes are stopped (`test_zwo_telescope.cpp`, "MoveAxis stop on one axis leaves the other axis moving").
- **SynScan refuses `SlewToCoordinates`, `SlewToTarget` and their async forms with `Tracking` false and `PulseGuide` during a slew** (issue #775, SynScan): both raise `InvalidOperation` (0x40B); invalid coordinates still raise `InvalidValue` first.
- **Bisque guide rates are range-checked** (issue #775, Bisque): `GuideRateRightAscension`/`GuideRateDeclination` outside 0 to 1x sidereal raise `InvalidValue` and leave the stored rate unchanged; before, any finite value was stored.
- **Telescope `CanSlew` and `CanSlewAltAz` read false, and synchronous slews are refused** (issue #775, AlpacaHTTP): `SlewToCoordinates`, `SlewToTarget` and `SlewToAltAz` answer `MethodNotImplemented` (0x400) before any parameter parse, target write or driver call, for every telescope driver, as ASCOM's slew FAQ asks. The async forms and `CanSlewAsync`/`CanSlewAltAzAsync` are unchanged. Clients that call a synchronous slew must switch to the async form (`test_routing.cpp`, "telescope synchronous slews are MethodNotImplemented (#775)").
