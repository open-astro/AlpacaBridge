### Added
- **remotealpaca: Camera of an upstream Alpaca server** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP, web UI, issue #2703, slice 5 of #1753): the "Remote Alpaca" vendor now also serves the Camera device of another Alpaca server as a device of this server, with the same four settings (upstream host, port, device number, poll period) and the same Connect, snapshot and link rules as the other proxied devices. `CameraState`, `ImageReady`, `PercentCompleted`, the temperatures, `CoolerPower`, `IsPulseGuiding` and `LastExposure*` answer from the poll's snapshot (five calls a poll on a typical cooled camera); the sensor and the client's settings are read once at Connect, written through by this server's writes, and keep answering while the link is faulted. `StartExposure` reads `CameraState` Exposing and `ImageReady` false at once, and only the poll reads `ImageReady` true. `PulseGuide` reads `IsPulseGuiding` true at once and refuses a pulse longer than 60 s. The image is downloaded from the other server's `ImageArray` as ImageBytes when a client asks for it, with a body cap sized for the sensor's full frame (at most 512 MiB) and a timeout scaled to it (at most 300 s), beside the poll, so a long download never reads as a lost link; an other server that serves the image only as JSON is not supported. `AbortExposure` and `StopExposure` are forwarded even while the link is faulted; members an ICameraV1 or V2 upstream lacks answer `NotImplemented` without being called. Built under `ALPACACORE_ENABLE_REMOTEALPACA`; fake evidence only, ConformU against the simulators is a rig step.

### Changed
- **`util::HttpClient` gains `get_with()`** (AlpacaCore, #2703): a GET with its own `Accept` header and body cap, and every reply carries its `Content-Type`; `get()` and `put_form()` keep the 1 MiB cap. The camera base class's `DeviceState` takes a `device_state_age()` hook, as the rotator and switch bases do, so a snapshot-served camera stamps when its state was measured; every other camera is unchanged.

### Added (tests)
- **remotealpaca Camera over a scripted upstream** (issue #2703): `test_remotealpaca_camera.cpp` (24 cases, two of them the client's ImageBytes and UTC time reads), four `[stress]` cases, the contract-sweep entry with its tier-2 host, tier-3 recipe and snapshot rig, a `test_http_client.cpp` case for `get_with()`, two catalog cases and two `test_routing` cases (the config round trip and the router's self-loop refusal). `AlpacaUpstreamScript` gains a Camera kind whose exposures run until `finish_exposure()` and whose image it serves as ImageBytes.
