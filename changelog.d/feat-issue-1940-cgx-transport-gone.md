### Breaking changes
- **Celestron CGX/CGX-L direct USB and the Celestron focus motor: a pulled USB cable reads Connected false at once** (AlpacaCore, issue #1940, decision 0009): the call that meets the pulled cable, and every member after it, now answers `NotConnected` instead of a `DriverException`, `Connected` reads false at once instead of after the 30 s loss bound (or never, for the mount before #1940), `DeviceState` is empty and the port is released; nothing reconnects on its own. **After upgrading:** plug the cable back in and reconnect (no Disconnect is needed first); on the CGX, run FindHome before the next slew, Sync or PulseGuide.

### Changed
- **Celestron CGX/CGX-L direct USB and the Celestron focus motor: transport-gone evidence is a lost link** (AlpacaCore, issue #1940, decision 0009 rule 2a): `CelestronCgxProtocol` treats a hangup or end-of-file on a read, and `EIO`/`ENXIO`/`ENODEV` on a read or write, as the port gone: it releases the port, throws `NotConnected` and latches a lock-free `link_alive()` until the next connect. Both drivers read that latch with their poll's: the state poll ends in the tick that sees it, a CGX slew, Park or pulse body ends on its next exchange, a connect that meets it fails with `NotConnected`, and `get_link_fault()` names the pulled cable until the next Connect or Disconnect. A timeout is still a desync for the fault latch, not a loss; for the client-visible change, see Breaking changes.

### Changed (tests)
- **Celestron CGX and focus motor pulled-cable cases** (AlpacaCore, issue #1940): pty-fake cases that pull the cable during a command, the state poll, the connect and (CGX) a slew, Park or pulse body; `FakeCelestronFocuser::sever_link()`; the CGX protocol, cached-state and #1457 stop-retry cases now expect `NotConnected` for a removed device.
