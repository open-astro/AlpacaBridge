### Fixed

- **Celestron CGX GOTOs across the equator on pier side 0**, Celestron CGX driver, issue #2640. The mount's controller never drives Dec past one fixed encoder count in a GOTO. When the shorter way crosses that count, it goes the long way round instead. FindHome used to label Dec home so that count sat on the equator for pier side 0 (SideOfPier 0, a target west of the meridian). A GOTO there from north of the equator to south of it, or back, then drove Dec over the pole toward the far side of the pier. The #2657 watchdog stopped such a GOTO with a "moved away from its GOTO target" error. FindHome now labels Dec home a quarter turn round, as it already did for RA, which puts that count where neither side of the pier can reach. On the bare CGX, GOTOs to Dec +5, +1, -1 and -5 on both sides of the pier, and two meridian flips, all took the shorter way, and ConformU 4.6.0-rc.1 passed over USB with 0 errors, warnings or issues (`AlpacaCore/conformu/Celestron/CGX/Linux-arm64-usb.txt`). Covered by `AlpacaCore/tests/test_celestron_cgx_goto_direction.cpp` and `test_celestron_cgx_geometry.cpp`.
