### Added
- **Celestron CGX: SkyPortal WiFi (AUX over TCP) transport in the AUX link** (AlpacaCore): `CelestronCgxProtocol::connect_tcp()` talks to the SkyPortal adapter from source 0x20. It re-sends a read-only motor query once when its reply is lost (300 ms, within the caller's deadline) and never re-sends a motion or write command. It resyncs past collision debris and bad checksums, discards the adapter's startup backlog and treats the adapter closing the connection as a lost link. The USB path is unchanged.
- **Celestron CGX: the driver runs over SkyPortal WiFi** (AlpacaCore): a CGX configured with a numeric adapter host (port 2000 by default) connects over TCP instead of USB. Over TCP each exchange gets 1 s (room for the one re-send of a lost read-only query, version and model probes included), every Connect takes the mount over with a verified stop, every Disconnect closes the socket and drops the home reference (FindHome again after a reconnect), one adapter serves one device, a motor controller's report is acknowledged and anything but a low-battery warning costs the reference, a stop nobody acknowledges ends with send-only stops, and the description counts the lost replies a re-send recovered. The USB driver is unchanged.
- **Celestron CGX over SkyPortal WiFi: a lost motion reply is settled by the axis status, never re-sent** (AlpacaCore): when a home start, GOTO, label write, guide pulse or stop gets no reply within 300 ms, the link reads the axis status instead (home searching, the GOTO closer to its target, the label read back, the guide active, the axis still). A command that took effect counts as done, with a warning and the recovered-reply count; one that did not fails as before. A stop the status cannot confirm gets the send-only stop at once instead of after three rounds, and stop checks over WiFi get 600 ms more to finish. USB is unchanged.
- **Celestron CGX over SkyPortal WiFi: a home survives a burst of lost requests** (AlpacaCore): on the rig the WiFi adapter lost requests in bursts right after a home started, a position read and its one re-send both, which failed the home. A lost read-only query is now re-sent up to twice (300 and 600 ms after it first went out, within its 1 s), and during a home the travel guard takes the state poll's encoder readings instead of reading the encoders again, so a home sends about a third fewer requests. Motion commands are still never re-sent; USB is unchanged.

- **Celestron CGX: USB or SkyPortal WiFi, chosen in the device form** (AlpacaCore, AlpacaHTTP): the CGX entry's Connection Type picks the mount's direct USB port or the SkyPortal WiFi adapter (its numeric IPv4 or IPv6 address, 1.2.3.4 on the adapter's own hotspot, port 2000); a name is refused, and a saved CGX entry with no connection type stays USB. A "Find adapter" button on the form lists the adapters heard within 5 s (`GET /management/v1/networkadapters?vendor=celestron`, one search at a time, holding no lock) and fills the address in, labelled as the adapter (`1.2.3.4 (adapter Celestron-AFFC, Telit WE310G4)`: the beacon names the adapter's own WiFi even when it has joined another network); Save still applies it. Both adapter modes work, set up by hand: this board on the adapter's own WiFi, or the adapter on the same network as this board (this board's hotspot included).
- **Celestron CGX: SkyPortal WiFi adapter beacon listener** (AlpacaCore): `listen_for_aux_beacons()` listens on UDP port 55555 for a bounded window and lists each SkyPortal adapter it hears (source address, SSID, module) for the form's "Find adapter" button. It sends nothing.
- **Celestron CGX: CGX-L motors are accepted** (AlpacaCore): the direct AUX driver (USB or WiFi) used to refuse motion unless both motors reported the CGX's model 0x1788. Motors reporting 0x18xx, the CGX-L's family per INDI's celestronaux driver, are now driven the same way (same mechanics and axis scaling) and named "Celestron CGX/CGX-L"; a log line at connect says the CGX-L is not yet qualified on hardware. A pair of two families, or any other model, still connects with motion refused.

### Fixed
- **Celestron CGX over USB: a reconnect no longer reads stale bus traffic** (AlpacaCore): the USB port stays open across a Disconnect, and on the rig an hour of AUX frames collected there while another client drove the mount over WiFi; the next Connect read them as its first reply and failed with "AUX frame checksum is invalid". A Connect on the kept port now discards waiting input first, as a fresh open always did.
- **Celestron CGX: a GoTo over WiFi no longer fails its 3-arcsecond arrival check** (AlpacaCore): on the rig every WiFi GoTo used all three fine corrections and one failed at 3.06". The motors landed on target, but the mount waited untracked between aiming a correction and reading where it landed, and over WiFi (about 21 ms an exchange) the sky moved 2-4" in that time. The mount now starts tracking as soon as each leg lands, before the check reads it, and turns tracking off again before another correction; each correction after the first is aimed ahead by the time the previous one took from aiming to tracking. A position read takes Dec before RA, so the tracking RA axis is read closest to the time its position is worked out. A failed refinement still ends with tracking off. This is shared with USB, where the October pilot's arrivals used 2.2-2.4" of the 3".

- **Celestron CGX over WiFi: a lost tracking-rate reply no longer fails a GoTo** (AlpacaCore): on the rig a GoTo failed because the reply to one tracking-off command was lost, and that failure also dropped the home reference. A tracking-rate command sets the whole rate, so sending it again is harmless: over WiFi its lost reply is now re-sent up to twice (300 and 600 ms), like a position read, and counted in "lost replies recovered". GoTo, home, position-label and guide commands are still never re-sent; USB is unchanged.
