### Fixed

- **ZWO AM: an older Sync no longer stops a newer guide pulse** (AlpacaCore, issue #2093): a Sync waiting out an "equipment moving" (`e4`) reply sent its stop (`:Q`) and retried even after a PulseGuide had started, so the pulse was cut short and the client was not told. A PulseGuide now counts as a newer motion command for the Sync, as a GOTO does: the Sync sends nothing after the pulse's `:Mg` and fails with `InvalidOperation` ("Sync superseded by a newer motion command"). A stored GOTO failure is kept.
- **ZWO AM: a redundant Connected=true no longer lets the next pulse overlap the running one** (AlpacaCore, issue #2095): a `Connected=true` while already connected dropped the queued pulses and also marked the pulse drain as idle while it still ran a step, so the next PulseGuide started a new drain and sent its `:Mg` over the running pulse. The running drain now stays in charge; the next pulse queues behind its step, and IsPulseGuiding stays true until both have run. Queued pulses are still dropped, and a real connect still resets the drain.
- **ZWO AM: a PulseGuide that races a reset and another PulseGuide no longer cuts the newer pulse** (AlpacaCore, issue #2096): a PulseGuide that had decided to start the pulse drain but not yet started it could, after an AbortSlew (or other reset) and a second PulseGuide, start its drain over the newer one, so the second pulse's remaining steps were never sent although it had been accepted. The drain decision and its start are now one step against a reset and another PulseGuide.
